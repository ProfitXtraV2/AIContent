How this works

This page is the automated monthly audit: a scheduled job pulls Search Console (impressions, clicks, position, indexed pages, striking-distance), PageSpeed/CWV, and Ahrefs (DR, referring domains, keywords, traffic), appends a dated snapshot to the history, and recomputes the tracking score. Lab CWV is noisy on a low-traffic site (no CrUX field data yet) so it's de-weighted. The deep qualitative audit (schema/E-E-A-T/technical findings) is run on-demand, not monthly. Dated reports live in docs/seo-audit/<month>.md.
