# Conversion Links — DentalVia

Single source of truth for **internal links and consultation CTAs** used in all DentalVia
articles. Extracted from the live site (`dentalvia.de`) on **2026-09-10**. This brand has
no affiliate programme — there are **no affiliate links**. Every link in this file points to
a page on `dentalvia.de` itself; the conversion goal is a free consultation lead via
`/kontakt/`.

**Never invent a URL.** If an article needs a page that does not appear in this table,
insert `[LINK NEEDED: <topic>]` as a placeholder for the human editor — do NOT guess a
path or link to any external domain.

These paths are referenced by the SEO stage (Task 4), the Brand Gate (Task 3), and the
daily run (Task 6). Keep this file updated whenever new pages go live.

## CTA Rules

**Primary CTA — free consultation**

Every article ends with exactly one CTA block targeting `/kontakt/`. Use the label
**„Kostenlose Beratung"**. The block may also surface the phone number (+49 89 1234567)
and the WhatsApp contact — but **only inside the CTA block, never inline in body copy**.
The email address `beratung@dentalvia.de` follows the same rule.

**Mid-article link (optional, pillars only)**

Long pillar articles (>1 500 words) may include a single mid-article text link to
`/kontakt/` — e.g. a sentence such as _„Jetzt unverbindlich anfragen"_ — in addition to
the end-of-article CTA block. Short articles (≤ 1 500 words) get the end block only.

## Internal-Link Rules

These rules are enforced by the Brand Gate (Task 3) and the SEO-stage prompt (Task 4).

1. **Link count:** every article links to **≥ 2 and ≤ 5** internal pages (excluding the
   mandatory `/kontakt/` CTA block, which does not count toward the body-copy quota).
2. **Treatment pages on first mention:** whenever an article body mentions a specific
   treatment by name (implants, veneers, crowns, etc.), link that first prominent mention
   to the matching treatment page from the table above.
3. **Cost & comparison articles must include:** `/kosten/` + `/garantie/` — both are
   required in any article whose primary angle is price, comparison, or quality assurance.
4. **Relevance requirement:** link choices must be editorially justified by the article's
   content. Do not add links only to hit the minimum count.
5. **No external links to competitor sites, price-comparison portals, or bare clinic
   domains.** External links are limited to official sources cited for factual claims
   (e.g. statutory insurance regulations), and only when no internal page covers the same
   point.
6. **`[LINK NEEDED]` flag:** if an article topic naturally calls for a page that does not
   yet exist (e.g. `/patienten-bewertungen/`), insert `[LINK NEEDED: <topic>]` in the
   article draft and leave the surrounding anchor text as plain text until the page is
   published and added to this registry.
