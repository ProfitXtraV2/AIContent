**Verdict: Shows strong AI patterns, 85% confidence.**

While the article is highly structured, fact-dense, and free of fluff, it exhibits several classic LLM writing patterns. It reads like a well-prompted, data-constrained GPT-4 output. The text relies heavily on formulaic paragraph setups, tautological transitions, and a slightly robotic, didactic tone that lacks the natural cadence of a human expert (especially one writing from a first-person or branded perspective like a "Patientenkoordinator"). 

Here is the breakdown of the specific patterns that triggered this assessment, along with actionable recommendations to humanize the text.

***

### 1. The "X determines Y" Formula (Repetitive Structural Setups)
**The Pattern:** AI models frequently use the exact same syntactic structure to introduce new concepts, usually by stating that "Factor A determines Outcome B" before providing the data.
**Flagged Passages:**
*   *"Den [LINK...] bestimmen zuerst das Material und die Herstellung."*
*   *"Der Unterschied zwischen dem günstigsten und dem teuersten Frontzahn-Veneer steckt im Material und im Herstellungsweg..."*
*   *"Die Haltbarkeit entscheidet wieder das Material..."*

**Recommendation:** 
Vary the paragraph openings to break the repetitive rhythm. Instead of stating the mechanics of the cause-and-effect every time, jump straight into the reality of the patient's situation. For example, in the durability section, advise the writer to start directly with how long the different materials last, rather than announcing *that* the material decides the durability. 

### 2. Tautological Transitions (Stating the Obvious)
**The Pattern:** LLMs often create a "bridge" sentence that explains a concept using the concept itself before giving the actual reasons. 
**Flagged Passage:** 
*   *"Hinter dem niedrigeren Auslandspreis steckt vor allem eine Kostenfrage: Löhne, Praxismieten und Laborkosten liegen in vielen Ländern..."*

**Recommendation:** 
Remove the tautology ("Behind the lower price is a cost question"). Instruct the writer to delete the first half of that sentence and start directly with the concrete economic factors (e.g., "Löhne, Praxismieten und Laborkosten..."). This makes the text punchier and less patronizing.

### 3. Declarative, Philosophical Subheadings
**The Pattern:** AI tends to frame straightforward medical or financial facts as profound, definitive statements in H2s.
**Flagged Passage:** 
*   *"## Ein Veneer ist eine ästhetische Entscheidung"*

**Recommendation:** 
Change this H2 to something more direct and grounded that reflects the actual content of the paragraph (which is about health insurance not paying for it). Suggest using a standard, patient-focused heading like "Warum die Krankenkasse nicht zahlt" or "Veneers als rein kosmetische Leistung".

### 4. The Didactic Q&A Format
**The Pattern:** When answering questions, AI often uses a stark, abrupt "No." followed by a textbook explanation. It mimics a sterile FAQ bot rather than an informative article.
**Flagged Passage:** 
*   *"Zahlt die Kasse mit? Nein. Veneers gelten als rein ästhetische Behandlung, deshalb..."*

**Recommendation:** 
Soften the transition. Advise the writer to integrate the negative answer into a fluid, conversational sentence (e.g., explaining that because it is an aesthetic treatment, patients must bear the costs themselves, without the robotic "Nein." full stop). 

### 5. Bland, Sanitized Summaries
**The Pattern:** AI struggles to write compelling conclusions, often resorting to generic adjectives ("praktisch", "wichtig") and neatly wrapping up the premise in a highly predictable way.
**Flagged Passages:** 
*   *"## Sofia als praktische Option"* (H2)
*   *"Passt die Versorgung, dann ist Sofia bei gleichem Material und gleicher Zahnzahl die klar günstigere Adresse."*

**Recommendation:** 
Replace the generic adjective "praktische" in the H2 with a specific, tangible benefit (e.g., focusing on EU standards or flight proximity). For the final sentence, advise the writer to inject a bit of the author's persona (Georgi Todorov). It should sound like a closing thought from a real patient coordinator, not a sanitized summary generated by a machine.

### 6. Process Signposting
**The Pattern:** AI loves to narrate chronological steps using rigid sequence markers.
**Flagged Passage:** 
*   *"Am Anfang stehen Beratung, Farbabgleich und eine digitale Simulation. Bei klassischen Veneers wird danach präpariert... und für die Zwischenzeit bekommen Sie ein Provisorium. Eingeklebt werden die fertigen Schalen erst zum Schluss."*

**Recommendation:** 
Combine these staccato, step-by-step sentences into a more fluid description of the patient journey. Advise the writer to focus on the patient experience rather than a sterile, manual-like sequence of events.
