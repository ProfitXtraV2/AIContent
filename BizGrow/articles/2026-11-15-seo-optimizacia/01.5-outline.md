## STRUCTURE DECISIONS (vs. the synthesis draft)

1. **Opening split out as the lead.** The synthesis draft opens inside the first H2. In the `ContentPage` port the opening is `lead` (under the H1, not an H2), and the template renders the „Запазете час“ booking button and the „Безплатна диагностика“ → /diagnostika/ button right under it. The opening is therefore its own block (`kind: hero`, no heading), and `#kakvo-e` starts with the definition, not the offer statement.
2. **`#cena` moved to the end and carries the closing CTA.** Default order ends on policies → booking CTA. The price is given in the report after the diagnosis, so the price section leads naturally into the booking CTA. `#ochakvania` moves before it. The template's end-of-page CTA band follows `#cena`, so no separate „next step“ H2 is added (it would duplicate the band and push the count to 9).
3. **Main term in exactly one H2.** The synthesis `#cena` heading „…цената на SEO оптимизацията“ would be a second H2 with the main term; it becomes „Как се формира цената на SEO услугите“ (carries secondary „seo услуги цена“ instead).
4. **`#ochakvania` heading drops „реклама в Google“** (sibling /reklama/ owns „реклама в гугъл“); the ads comparison stays as one paragraph with the /reklama/ link.
5. **What the client gets / keeps under control** (default-order item 5) is folded into `#ochakvania`: the report, the client's choice of fixes, approval of every text, the monthly report. Access/ownership stays an OWNER CONFIRM (O6).
6. **No example scenario** (optional item 6): none exists, a new one needs the owner; not designed.
7. **Port shape:** `ContentPage.sections[]` has `paragraphs[]` then optional `bullets[]`. In every section with a list, the Author writes the paragraphs before the list and puts nothing after it except flags (flags are removed at Step 6). `steps` become bullets with a **bold** step label in the port.
