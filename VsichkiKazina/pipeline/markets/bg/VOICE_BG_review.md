### Paragraph shape
Paragraphs run 2-4 sentences and usually state → elaborate → move on, without a takeaway punchline line. Many sections end mid-register on a practical detail rather than a conclusion. The fact→meaning→example→punchline template does not appear.

### How opinion and verdict are expressed

Mostly implicit jumps between sections (the heading does the work).
