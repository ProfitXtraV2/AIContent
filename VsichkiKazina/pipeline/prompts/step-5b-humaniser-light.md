a sentence. KEEP a list only when the format genuinely earns its place:
sequential steps (numbered how-to instructions), or a long enumeration
(6+ items) where prose would be genuinely harder to scan than the table
format. Do not add new lists or bullets anywhere - only reduce existing
ones that fail this test.
