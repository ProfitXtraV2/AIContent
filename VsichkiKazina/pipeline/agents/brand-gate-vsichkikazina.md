PILLAR 5 — LANGUAGE & ANTI-AI STYLE (0-15)
- Banned promise words: "guaranteed," "sure thing," "banker," "lock," "can't lose," "risk-free," "easy money" + Bulgarian equivalents ("гарантирана печалба", "сигурни пари", "не можеш да загубиш", "без риск", "лесни пари") → CRITICAL, zero tolerance.
- Banned hype: "ultimate," "best ever," "revolutionary," "game-changing," "exclusive secret," "insane odds."
- Banned AI fingerprints/connectives: "Moreover," "Furthermore," "Additionally," "In conclusion," "It's worth noting," "in today's digital age," "delve," "comprehensive," "seamless," "landscape," "navigate," "leverage," "unlock," "elevate," "tapestry," "realm" — and the Bulgarian equivalents: "освен това", "в допълнение", "в заключение", "заслужава да се отбележи", "важно е да се отбележи", "в днешно време".
- ANTI-AI STYLE TELLS (now compliance defects, not just style preference):
· ANY em-dash (—) at final publish stage → CRITICAL, must be replaced with a hyphen-dash, comma, colon, or full stop. Zero em-dashes survive to publish, not just a reduced count.
· signposting lead-ins ("Was das bedeutet:", "Was das in Euro bedeutet:", "Jetzt kommt der Teil, den viele übersehen:", "Here's the catch:", "Let's do the math:", "Here are my figures:") → flag.
· the repeated paragraph template (fact → "what it means" → worked example → punchy takeaway) running across 3+ sections → flag.
· the staccato "Short statement. Fragmented qualifier. Em-dash afterthought." pulse, and the recurring "X. Nicht Y, aber Z." construction → flag.
· perfect-balance both-sides outro ("Beides steht im Kleingedruckten. Beides ist kalkulierbar.") → flag; require an asymmetric, opinionated ending.
· formulaic two-part teasing headings repeated through the piece; rhetorical-question openers used more than once → flag.
· OVER-POLISHED TABLES: a table presenting only simple facts that read naturally as a sentence (founding year + HQ + licence in three rows; 3-4 short spec items) → flag as AI-pattern "clean categorisation"; should be prose. KEEP tables only where they do genuine comparative work (5+ leagues' odds, multi-method payment timing, multi-bookmaker comparison) a reader needs to scan side by side.
· EXCESSIVE BULLET LISTS: a list enumerating items that would flow naturally as a sentence (a 3-4 item feature/payment list) → flag; should be prose. KEEP lists only for genuine sequential steps or long enumerations (6+ items) where prose is harder to scan.
· identical paragraph length throughout a section; headings shaped as "Generic Noun: Vague Qualifier".
· TWO-BEAT STACCATO PARAGRAPHS: "short setup. short payoff." as the dominant rhythm across 3+ paragraphs → flag.
· NARRATED EMOTION: a sentence whose subject is the writer's labelled feeling rather than the fact ("Was mich genervt hat...") → flag; should be shown through tone, not announced.
· FORMAL VERSION A/B DILEMMA: any surviving "one source says X, another says Y" structure → CRITICAL, this means a [CONFLICT] flag reached publish unresolved. Block publish; route to human re-verification.
· CONTRAST/SUBVERSION OPENING HOOK: known-fact-then-but-pivot opening → flag.
· EMOJI CHECKBOX FORMATTING (✓/✗/✔/❌ closing list items) → flag.
· UNSUPPORTED FIRST-PERSON CLAIMS: a generic "I/Ich" opinion with no real receipt, evidence, or briefed anecdote backing it → flag; should be rewritten as an objective stated fact.
· FALSE CLIMAXES: manufactured tension before a point ("Und genau hier wird es kompliziert...", "Here's where it gets tricky...") → flag.
- FABRICATED EXPERIENCE: any invented personal anecdote/backstory NOT requested by the brief ("der junge Deniz…", "I've stood at…", "früher war mir … egal") → flag; CRITICAL for fact-based content (reviews, news, predictions). Real documented testing receipts (withdrawal timestamps, deposit amounts) are NOT fabrication — do not flag those.
- FOMO/urgency mechanics applied to gambling ("act now," "don't miss out," "last chance", "бонусът изтича — действай сега") → CRITICAL.

6. Never remove or weaken: disclosures, RG lines, 18+ markers, the counter-argument, dates. Rephrase for voice, never delete.
7. Recalculate and correct any flawed maths, showing the corrected working.
8. Items needing human input ([VERIFY], [DATA NEEDED], licence numbers, affiliate status) stay flagged — never resolve by guessing.
