# **BETTING FAMILY — DEAD SIMPLE STEP-BY-STEP**

*One article, start to finish. Every step = a new chat. Replace [BRACKETS] with real content.*

## **THE MAIN PIPELINE — RUN THIS FOR EVERY ARTICLE**

0 Brief (you) → 1 Synthesis → 1.5 Outline → 2 Author → 3 Humaniser →

4 SEO → 5 Brand Gate → 5b Humaniser (light) → 6 You check facts →

7 Gemini → 8 Publish

### **STEP 0 — BUILD THE BRIEF (no agent)**

Fill in: query, market, byline (persona or editorial), intent, NeuronWriter terms, top 2-3 source articles pasted in full.

### **STEP 1 — SYNTHESIS**

Agent: **BetFam Synthesis**. Paste the brief. Get: draft + fact report. Read the report (2 min) — check [CONFLICT] flags.

### **STEP 1.5 — OUTLINE**

Agent: **BetFam Outline/Architect**. Paste the Step 1 output. Get: headings, per-section facts, per-section shape. Check the shapes look varied (not 5 identical sections).

### **STEP 2 — AUTHOR**

Agent: **BetFam Author — [your market]**. Paste the outline + draft, persona or editorial prompt. Get: the article. Check CANON ADDITIONS (usually "none").

### **STEP 3 — HUMANISER**

Agent: **BetFam Humaniser**. Paste the article. Get: HUMAN-LIKE (keep as-is) or rewritten.

### **STEP 4 — SEO**

Agent: **BetFam SEO**. Paste article + query + terms. Get: optimised article + title + meta.

### **STEP 5 — BRAND GATE**

Agent: **BetFam Brand Gate** (thinking OFF). Paste the article. PASS → continue. FAIL → go back and fix, never patch by hand.

### **STEP 5B — HUMANISER LIGHT RE-CHECK**

Agent: **BetFam Humaniser** (new chat). Paste the light re-check prompt + article. Usually says "nothing flagged" — that's normal.

### **STEP 6 — YOU CHECK THE FACTS (no agent, never skipped)**

Clear every [BRACKET] against a real source. Re-check odds/bonuses/licence numbers live. Read it aloud.

### **STEP 7 — GEMINI CHECK**

Agent: **BetFam External Check** (Gemini). Paste the article only — the instructions are already set up in the agent. Apply any suggestions via Humaniser, then re-run Brand Gate.

### **STEP 8 — PUBLISH (no agent)**

Byline, dates, disclosures, RG line, title/meta. Canon update only if Step 2 listed something. Log the article.

## **THE TWO AGENTS THAT ARE NOT PART OF THIS PIPELINE**

These don't run per article. Each market has its own owner — **only that market's owner uses these for their market.**

### **🗨️ BetFam Comment Moderator**

**When:** a reader posts a real comment under a published article in your market. **Who:** whoever owns that market's content — you triage comments only for your own market's articles. **What you do:**

- New chat → agent: **BetFam Comment Moderator**.

- Paste the comment exactly as the reader wrote it, plus which article it's under.

- Get back: APPROVE / REJECT / FLAG FOR REDACTION / FLAG FOR HUMAN REVIEW.

- Act on the verdict yourself — **the agent never writes or posts anything on your behalf.**

**Never use this to:** generate a comment, write a "sample testimonial," or create reader content of any kind. Real submitted comments only, always.

### **📖 BetFam Lexical Corpus Builder**

**When:** you want to sharpen your market's voice for one content type (reviews, guides, or news) — e.g. quality feels off, you're launching a new content type, or you have fresh Gemini feedback showing a pattern. **Who:** the owner of that specific market — you build the voice framework for your own market, using real competitor content in your own language. **What you do:**

- Collect 3-5 real, published competitor articles of ONE type (all reviews, or all guides, or all news) in your market's language.

- New chat → agent: **BetFam Lexical Corpus Builder**.

- Paste the prompt, specify market + content type, paste the real articles.

- Get back one document, split into two sections (Architecture + Style Guide).

- Save it as VOICE_[market]_[type].md — e.g. VOICE_de_review.md.

- Next time you write that content type in that market: paste Section 1 into Step 1.5 (Outline), paste Section 2 into Step 2 (Author).

**This is not urgent or per-article** — build it once per market per content type, refresh only when needed.

| **Agent** | **Runs** | **Owner** |
| --- | --- | --- |

## **NEVER**

- Skip Step 6.

- Publish an article with a [BRACKET] still in it.

- Mix two articles in one chat.

- Hand-patch a Brand Gate FAIL — go back and fix at the real step.

- Use Comment Moderator or Lexical Corpus Builder for a market that isn't yours.

- Ask any agent to invent a testimonial or fabricate a comment.
