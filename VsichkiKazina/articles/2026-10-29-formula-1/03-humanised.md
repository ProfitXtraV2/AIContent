# 03 — Humaniser pass (notes)

The author draft (02) was written against the anti-AI style rules from the start, so this is a
light pass, not a rebuild. Checks run and outcome:

- **Em-dashes:** 0 in the body (replaced the two natural spots with a comma „спринт, късо
  състезание" and a colon „там, където му е мястото: забавление"). Within budget.
- **No signposting lead-ins** („Какво означава това:", „Нека сметнем:") — none present.
- **No „не X, а Y" paragraph-ending antithesis** habit — the lead uses „една за пилота…, една
  за отбора" (parallel, not a mic-drop negation); no section closes on a mirrored negation.
- **No numbered-prose signposting** („първо… второ…") — the Q1/Q2/Q3 mention names the real
  segments, not a counted list.
- **Paragraph shapes varied:** lead = one flowing para; „Двете титли" ends on a plain fact (revenue
  share) with no aphorism; „Отборите" ends on the defer-to-official-source, not a takeaway; „Спринтът"
  is a short comparison. Three consecutive sections do not share the fact→meaning→example→takeaway shape.
- **Headings** are plain descriptive, not two-part teases.
- **Asymmetric ending** kept: past records are exact, the next race is not, no bet guarantees it.
- **AI-tell connectives** (освен това / в допълнение / в заключение / заслужава да се отбележи /
  в днешно време) — none.
- **Native vocab:** пол-позишън, болид, решетка, квалификация used the way a BG viewer speaks.

Facts/numbers/links/RG untouched. Text carried to 04 unchanged except the two em-dash swaps above.
