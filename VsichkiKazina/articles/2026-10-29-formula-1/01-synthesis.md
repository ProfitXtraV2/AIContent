# 01 — Synthesis (Формула 1 — как работи)

**Intent:** informational, head query „формула 1" (18000/kd1); the searcher wants to
understand how the sport works, not live standings. Evergreen how-it-works guide, sibling of
the Ла Лига / Тенис / Ефбет Лига guides.

**Angle lock:** explain the mechanism — what F1 is, the two championships, race points, the
sprint, teams/cars, the race weekend + knockout qualifying, race distance, and the all-time
records. One sober "betting, from a distance" bridge + one responsible-gambling moment. ZERO
predictions, ZERO current standings/results/fixtures/odds.

**Facts to carry (verified, see 00-brief SOURCES):**
- Top class of open-wheel single-seaters; sanctioned by FIA; World Championship from 1950
  (first race Silverstone, 13.05.1950).
- Two titles: Drivers' (from 1950) + Constructors'/teams' (from 1958 = sum of a team's two cars).
- Race points since 2010: 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1 (top 10); must finish ≥90% of distance.
- Fastest-lap bonus point existed 2019–2024, scrapped from 2025 → none now.
- Sprint (~100 km, Saturdays on some weekends): top 8 score 8-7-6-5-4-3-2-1; since 2023 does
  NOT set Sunday's grid.
- 2 cars (2 drivers) per team; to сезон 2026 г. = 11 teams / 22 cars (Cadillac debut, Sauber→Audi);
  historic norm 10/20; count changes → point to official source for the current grid.
- Weekend: FP1/FP2 Fri, FP3 Sat, qualifying, race Sun. Knockout qualifying (since 2006) = Q1/Q2/Q3;
  slowest drop in Q1 & Q2, fastest 10 fight for pole in Q3 (exact drop count varies with grid size).
- Race = least laps exceeding 305 km (Monaco ~260 km); 2-hour racing cap (up to 3h window with
  stoppages); mandatory ≥1 pit stop + ≥2 dry tyre compounds; ends when leader completes the laps.
- Records (autumn 2026): most drivers' titles Schumacher & Hamilton 7 each; constructors Ferrari 16.

**Changeable → date it / defer:** team count (11 „към сезон 2026 г."), current standings
(never printed; refer to official source). Everything else here is a stable rule/record.
