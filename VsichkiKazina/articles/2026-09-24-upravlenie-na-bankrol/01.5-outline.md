H1: Управление на банкрол в онлайн казино: бюджет, лимити и размер на залога

OPENING DIRECTION: open with a direct definition-plus-boundary of what a банкрол is and is not
(the money set aside for play, not savings/rent/hoped-for winnings). No known-fact-then-pivot,
no rhetorical question, no announced intro. First sentence states the thing.

SECTION 1 — Банкролът е парите за игра, нищо друго
Facts: банкрол = money set aside for casino play, affordable to lose entirely; if losing it hits
rent/utilities/food, it is too big; casino games favour the operator long-run; gambling = paid
entertainment with a knowable cost, not income/investment/strategy („хазартът не е финансова стратегия").
Terms/entities: банкрол, управление на банкрол, платено забавление.
Direction: define банкрол by what it is and what it excludes; land the entertainment-not-strategy
doctrine as plain fact (the house edge), no sermon. This carries the mandatory RG doctrine touch.
Shape: single flowing analytical paragraph (4–5 sentences), no takeaway line.

SECTION 2 — Откъде идва сумата
Facts: budget from disposable income after rent/bills/food/savings; 1–5% of monthly disposable
income, lower end for beginners; worked example €800 disposable × 3% = €24 (примерни).
Terms/entities: бюджет за казино, разполагаем доход.
Direction: show the budget comes from leftover entertainment money; one worked € example, labelled примерни.
Shape: short narrative + one worked numeric example (3–4 sentences). Do NOT open the calc with a signpost.

SECTION 3 — Отдели парите физически, не само наум
Facts: a bankroll in the same account as salary is not a bankroll; keep play money separate
(separate account / e-wallet / prepaid card); a separate pot makes overspend a conscious act.
Terms/entities: отделяне на парите за игра от битовите пари.
Direction: argue for physical separation of play money from household money; concrete methods in prose.
Shape: short blunt build (2–3 sentences), no worked figure. Deliberately the shortest section.

SECTION 4 — Размерът на залога като процент от банкрола
Facts: unit betting = fixed % of bankroll per stake; банкрол €200 → 1% = €2, 2% = €4; upper bound
~5% = €10 (примерни); smaller share lasts longer and survives a bad run; volatility context
(висока волатилност = long dry runs, small stake is the defence; game does not owe you a turnaround).
Terms/entities: размер на залог, единичен залог, unit betting, волатилност.
Direction: teach % staking with the €200 worked set; connect stake size to how long the bankroll
lasts and to volatility. This is the article's core numeric section.
Shape: narrative build (4–6 sentences) with the worked €200/1–2%/5% figures woven in.

SECTION 5 — Сесиен банкрол срещу общ банкрол
Facts: общ банкрол = whole-period sum; сесиен = one sitting's slice; splitting protects the whole
budget; example €200 / 4 sessions = €50 per session (примерни); enter a session with €50 not €200.
Terms/entities: сесиен банкрол, общ банкрол.
Direction: distinguish общ vs сесиен банкрол with the €200 → 4×€50 split; end on the protected remainder.
Shape: worked numeric example with minimal prose around it (3–4 sentences).

SECTION 6 — Кога да спреш: стоп-загуба и печеливш лимит
Facts: стоп-загуба (лимит за загуба) = pre-decided stop amount, no exceptions; at €50 session the
session pot is also the loss cap; win-limit = winnings on screen are not winnings until withdrawn;
example enter €50, at balance €75 withdraw at least the €50 stake (примерни).
Terms/entities: стоп-загуба, лимит за загуба, печеливш лимит, кога да спреш.
Direction: cover both the loss stop and the win stop; the win-limit is the under-discussed half.
Both figures tie to the €50 session. Do not manufacture tension.
Shape: two short paragraphs, one per limit; uneven lengths.

SECTION 7 — Защо гоненето на загуби е капан
Facts: chasing = raising stake/frequency after a loss to win it back; most common, most costly error;
past loss is fixed, next bet is independent with the same house edge; bigger stake only speeds
depletion; a hard daily/weekly loss cap breaks the chain.
Terms/entities: гонене на загуби.
Direction: explain the mechanism (independence + edge), state plainly it fixes nothing; tie the
protection back to the loss limit. No „not X but Y" mic-drop ending.
Shape: single flowing analytical paragraph (4–5 sentences).

SECTION 8 — Лимитите като инструмент, зададен предварително
Facts: licensed operators offer three limits, worth most when set BEFORE the first deposit;
deposit limit (cap on money in), loss limit (cap on money lost), session/time limit (how long);
lowering usually applies at once, raising often waits 24–72h — the delay is protection.
Terms/entities: депозитен лимит, загубен лимит, сесиен лимит, лимит за загуба.
Direction: present the three tools and the pre-commitment logic; the 24–72h raise-delay is the key
practical detail. This is the one genuinely enumerable set → a short plain list of 3 is allowed here.
Shape: brief lead sentence + a 3-item list (the three limits) + one sentence on the 24–72h delay.

SECTION 9 — Самоизключване, когато лимитите не стигат
Facts: self-exclusion blocks account access for a chosen period; per-operator, or for all BG-licensed
casinos via the национален регистър на уязвимите лица (НАП); filed in writing by the affected person
only; not reversible by calling support before the term ends; a control tool, not a verdict.
Terms/entities: самоизключване, регистър на уязвимите лица, НАП.
Direction: explain самоизключване without judgement; name the НАП register and the write-only filing.
Shape: single flowing paragraph (4 sentences).

SECTION 10 — Ако правиш само едно нещо (closing)
Facts: decide amount + limits before first deposit; keep play money separate; stake a small share
per bet; the rest is discipline to keep your own rules. Inline RG touch + verbatim 18+ line + /otgovorna-igra/.
Direction: asymmetric, opinionated close — the discipline is the whole game, hardest on the night you
want to break it. Carry the inline 18+ line and the /otgovorna-igra/ link here.
Shape: short closing paragraph (3–4 sentences), no both-sides bow, no summary restatement.
