## STRUCTURE
1. **Open with a 5-step checklist** that answers the query immediately (this is the core SEO
   win — a scannable „how to choose" the SERP guide articles compete on):

   5) Мобилно и поддръжка (мобилен браузър/приложение, български език, инструменти за лимити).
2. **By player type (keep the live page's strong structure, but GENERIC — what to check for each,
