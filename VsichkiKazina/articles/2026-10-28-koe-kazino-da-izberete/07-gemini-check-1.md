### 1. Pattern: Formulaic Signposting / The "Pivot" Transition
**The Flagged Passage:** 
> "Казино, което минава и петте, е добро за повечето хора. Но „добро за повечето" и „най-доброто за вас" рядко са едно и също, затова следващата крачка е да наместите избора спрямо това как играете."

**Why it reads as AI:** 
LLMs are programmed to hold the reader's hand. When moving from one section to another, AI almost always uses a "pivot" sentence that summarizes the previous point, introduces a contrast ("But..."), and explicitly announces what the next section will do ("the next step is..."). It feels like a textbook transition rather than natural web copywriting.

**Recommendation:**
*   Delete the second sentence entirely. You don't need to announce to the reader what the next step is; just take them there. Let the preceding paragraph (about testing customer support) be the natural end of the checklist, and let the upcoming H2 do the heavy lifting for the transition.

### 2. Pattern: Robotic / SEO-Stuffed Subheading
**The Flagged Passage:** 
> "## Как да изберете онлайн казино според вашия тип игра"
> "Как да изберем онлайн казино зависи и от това как играем..."

**Why it reads as AI:** 
The body copy of this article is punchy and conversational, but this H2 and the immediate sentence following it read like an AI trying to force an exact-match SEO keyword into the text. Repeating the exact phrasing of the title ("Как да изберете онлайн казино") makes it feel mechanical and breaks the established expert tone.

**Recommendation:**

AI loves to group things into neat, bolded lists. **Recommendation:**
