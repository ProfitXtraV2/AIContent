--- SOURCE 1: general slot-mechanic terminology (industry-standard, cross-provider) ---
Wild symbols substitute for other paying symbols to complete a combination, most commonly excluding the scatter/bonus symbol. Standard sub-types recognised across providers: standard/classic wild (plain substitute), expanding wild (stretches to fill a whole reel), sticky wild (stays in place across a number of spins, typically inside a bonus/free-spins round), walking/shifting wild (moves one reel per spin/re-spin until it exits the grid), stacked wild (appears multiple times stacked on the same reel, raising the chance of a wild landing in an active row), multiplier wild (substitutes and also multiplies the win it completes, e.g. x2/x3).
--- SOURCE 2 [https://games.netent.com/video-slots/jack-and-the-beanstalk/ + independent review sites, WebSearch 12.09.2026] ---
Jack and the Beanstalk (NetEnt): when a wild lands, it triggers a re-spin and then shifts one reel to the left before the next re-spin; this repeats until the wild walks off reel 1. Classic public example of a walking/shifting wild. No RTP/bonus figures pulled into this article (out of scope).
--- SOURCE 3 [https://starburstgame.eu/ + independent review sites, WebSearch 12.09.2026] ---
Starburst (NetEnt): a wild landing on reels 2, 3 or 4 expands to cover the whole reel, locks in place and triggers a re-spin on the remaining reels; can repeat up to 3 times. Combined with a "win both ways" payline mechanic. Classic public example of an expanding wild + re-spin.

SYNTHESIS — CORE FACTS TO CARRY FORWARD:
1. Definition: a wild substitutes for other symbols in a payline/way to complete or improve a combination. It almost always excludes the scatter/bonus-trigger symbol, which is a separate symbol with its own job (triggers free spins, or pays scatter-style regardless of position) — mention in plain text, no link (companion topic).
2. Types, each with behaviour + where typically met:
   - Стандартен (обикновен) wild: sits on a reel like any other symbol, replaces regular paying symbols on an active line. Most common, found in almost every video slot.
   - Разширяващ се (expanding) wild: when it lands, it stretches to fill the entire reel it's on, turning one symbol into a full reel of wilds. Often paired with a re-spin. Example: Starburst.
   - Залепващ (sticky) wild: stays fixed in its position for a set number of subsequent spins (often inside a free-spins/bonus round), while the other reels keep spinning around it.
   - Вървящ / местещ се (walking/shifting) wild: moves one position (usually one reel) per spin or re-spin, until it walks off the grid entirely. Example: Jack and the Beanstalk.
   - Натрупан (stacked) wild: several wild symbols appear stacked on top of each other on the same reel, so the reel can turn (partly or fully) wild in one spin, raising the chance of a wild sitting in a paying row.
   - Множител-wild (multiplier wild): behaves like a standard/other wild type but also multiplies the win it takes part in, e.g. x2 or x3 (brief mention, not a main type to expand).
3. Honesty core (must not be buried): all wild variants make winning combinations look and feel more frequent/dramatic on screen, but none of them change the game's long-run RTP or house edge — that number is fixed in the game's math model before the reels are even drawn. A slot generous with wilds on screen typically compensates with lower base-symbol payouts or a tighter paytable elsewhere; the visible "help" and the long-run return are two different layers of the same design.
4. RTP framing: RTP is a long-run statistic over very many spins, never a promise for one session; wild frequency is a feature of game design/volatility, not a signal of better real odds.
5. Practical reader takeaway: try new symbol behaviour in demo mode first (free, no deposit) to see how a game's specific wild type behaves before betting real money; check a game's own info/paytable screen for its exact wild rules, since behaviour varies by title even within the same type name.
