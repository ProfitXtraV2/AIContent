H1: Wild символи в ротативките: видове и как работят

OPENING (no heading, 2-4 sentences): lead directly with the definition/finding, no rhetorical question, no "everybody knows X" hook. State plainly what a wild does and that it (almost always) excludes the scatter. Plant the scope: several distinct behaviours travel under the same "WILD" label.

## Какво прави wild символът
- Facts: substitutes for other paying symbols on an active line/way to complete or upgrade a combination; almost never replaces the scatter/bonus symbol, which has its own separate job (triggers free spins / pays regardless of position) — name this fact in plain text, no link.
- Terms: wild, диви символи, wild символ какво е, scatter (plain text)
- Direction: single flowing paragraph, definitional, dry and direct. Land the wild/scatter distinction as one clear sentence, not a hedge. This is a good natural spot for the glossary link (/blog/rechnik-kazino-termini/) since the reader may want the fuller term list.
- Shape: one paragraph, no list.

## Стандартен wild — основата
- Facts: plain substitute wild, sits on the reel like a normal symbol, replaces regular symbols on an active line; the most common form, present in nearly every video slot; no extra mechanic attached.
- Direction: short, almost blunt paragraph — this is the "nothing special" baseline the other types get compared against. Don't overwrite it; let it be brief on purpose (varies the rhythm against the longer sections around it).
- Shape: short paragraph (3-4 sentences), deliberately plain.

## Разширяващ се, залепващ и вървящ wild
- Facts: expanding wild (stretches to fill the whole reel, often triggers a re-spin); sticky wild (stays fixed in place for a set number of following spins, typically inside a bonus/free-spins round, while the rest of the reels keep turning); walking/shifting wild (moves one reel per spin/re-spin until it exits the grid entirely).
- Terms: разширяващ се wild, залепващ wild, вървящ wild
- Direction: prose enumeration of three distinct behaviours in one section — NOT a bulleted list (per anti-AI-tell rule against excessive lists for things that flow as sentences... but these are three fairly rich mechanics, so give each its own sentence-cluster within one flowing section, transitions carrying the structure, no "first/second/third" counting language).
- Shape: medium-length section, several short paragraphs, no takeaway one-liner at the end (ends mid-detail, moves straight to the next section).

## Натрупан wild и множител-wild
- Facts: stacked wild — several wild symbols stacked on the same reel, so a whole reel or most of it can turn wild in one spin, raising the chance a wild sits in a paying row; multiplier wild — briefly, behaves like whichever wild type it is but also multiplies the win it takes part in (e.g. x2/x3), mentioned as a variant layered on top of the others rather than a separate family.
- Direction: shorter section, matter-of-fact, no worked euro example here (save the illustrative figure for the honesty section) — vary shape against the section above.
- Shape: short, 2 paragraphs.

## Две познати игри, две различни движения
- Facts: Jack and the Beanstalk (NetEnt) as the named walking-wild example — wild lands, triggers a re-spin, shifts one reel to the left, repeats until it walks off reel 1. Starburst (NetEnt) as the named expanding-wild example — wild landing on reels 2-4 expands to cover the whole reel, locks, triggers a re-spin on the rest, can repeat up to three times, paired with a win-both-ways payline mechanic. No RTP/bonus figures for either game — mechanic description only.
- Direction: concrete, grounded section built entirely around two named real games — gives the reader something checkable in demo mode. Natural spot for /kazino-igri/rotativki/ link (readers curious where to find such mechanics browse the rotativki hub).
- Shape: two short paragraphs, one per game, plain descriptive, no forced symmetry between them (let one be a sentence longer than the other).

## Помагат на екрана, не и на дългосрочния резултат
- Facts (THE HONESTY CORE — must land clearly, not be buried): every wild variant makes winning combinations look/feel more frequent and dramatic, but none of them change the game's long-run RTP or house edge; that number is fixed in the math model before a single reel turns. A slot generous with wilds on screen typically compensates with a tighter base paytable elsewhere. One brief illustrative (marked примерни) numeric framing is fine here if it stays generic and RTP-only (e.g. "RTP от 96% означава, че в дългосрочен план играта връща средно €96 от всеки €100 заложен оборот, независимо колко wild символи виждаш по пътя" — a restated general RTP mechanic, not tied to any named game).
- Terms: RTP, wild ротативки
- Direction: this is the myth-correction section — state the correction as a direct claim, not a "Version A/B" hedge, not a rhetorical question. Natural spot for /slot-igri/visok-rtp/ link (reader who wants the RTP angle can go compare high-RTP titles there).
- Shape: single dense analytical paragraph, ends on the fact itself, no aphoristic mic-drop sentence.
