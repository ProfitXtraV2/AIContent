## FACTS (all researched — see 06-verification.md; Wizard of Odds referee)
- 53-card deck = 52 + one joker (bug). Joker in the 5-card hand: ace, or completes straight/flush/straight flush/royal;
  in the 2-card hand always an ace.
- 7 cards to player and dealer; split into 5-card back (high) + 2-card front (low); back MUST outrank front (foul = auto loss).
- Both player hands must beat dealer's; win one/lose one = push; lose both = lose; copies (ties) go to the dealer.
- Commission: winning bets pay even money less 5%.
- House edge (player not banking): headline ≈ 2.84%; house-way (non-banking) ≈ 2.72%; optimal ≈ 2.51% (context).
- Push rate ≈ 40.5% (house-way).
- Strategy: play the house way (near-optimal); strongest 5 in back, next-best two in front without fouling.
- Side/Fortune bets carry much higher edges (Emperor's Challenge 4.171%; Progressive 11.54% etc.) → steer away.
- RTP derived ≈ 97.16% (= 100 − 2.84).
