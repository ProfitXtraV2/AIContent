### **Flagged Passages & AI Patterns**

**1. Pattern: The "Prompt-Driven" Formulaic Outline (Interrogative Signposting)**
*   **The text:** 
    *   *Какво е таванът на печалбата и какво включва*
    *   *Колко рядко се стига до него*
    *   *Таванът, RTP и волатилността*
    *   *Къде да намериш максималната печалба на ротативката*
    *   *Как да четеш числото на корицата*
*   **Why it flags:** This H2 structure reads exactly like a standard ChatGPT output when given a prompt like: *"Write an article about max wins. Cover what it is, how rare it is, how it relates to RTP, where to find it, and how to read it."* AI defaults to highly predictable, question-based or list-like subheadings to organize its thoughts.

**2. Pattern: Clinical/Textbook Transitions (Robotic phrasing)**
*   **The text:** *"Записан като множител, таванът се мащабира със залога."*
*   **Why it flags:** While mathematically accurate, this sentence is very dry and clinical. AI often uses this exact "Verb-phrase comma subject-verb" structure (*Written as a multiplier, the cap scales with the bet*) to transition between paragraphs when explaining technical concepts. It temporarily breaks the engaging, conversational tone established in the intro.

**3. Pattern: The Didactic "Staccato" Wrap-Up (Information Dumping)**
*   **The text:** *"Полезни са други числа: RTP на версията, която върти твоето казино, волатилността и колко струва завъртането при твоя залог. Два слота се сравняват по таван в евро само при един и същ залог, иначе числата не са съпоставими. Хазартът не е финансова стратегия, а ротативката е направена така, че дългосрочно да печели операторът."*
*   **Why it flags:** AI struggles with natural conclusions. Instead of a smooth narrative wrap-up, it tends to spit out a rapid-fire, staccato list of "final rules" or moral lessons. The rhythm here becomes very choppy—three dense, instructional sentences in a row that feel like a checklist rather than a closing thought.

### **Concrete Recommendations**

*   **To fix Pattern 1 (The Outline):** Change a couple of the H2s from direct questions to more narrative or punchy statements. For example, instead of *"Къде да намериш максималната печалба на ротативката"*, you could use something like *"Дребният шрифт: къде се крият лимитите"*. Instead of *"Колко рядко се стига до него"*, you could use *"Реалността зад числата: колко често пада max win"*. This breaks the robotic "What/Where/How" pattern.
*   **To fix Pattern 2 (Clinical Transition):** Soften the transition to make it sound like a human explaining it to a friend, rather than a textbook. You could recommend combining it with the previous thought or adding a conversational bridge (e.g., instead of starting with "Записан като множител...", start directly with how the math affects the player's pocket). 
*   **To fix Pattern 3 (Staccato Wrap-up):** Suggest varying the sentence length in the final paragraph. Connect the thoughts with conjunctions rather than hard stops. For example, linking the comparison of slots directly to the RTP/volatility point would make it flow like a cohesive paragraph rather than a bullet-less list of instructions.
