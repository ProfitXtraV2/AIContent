## WEB-VERIFIED FACTS (source, accessed 02.10.2026)
- Max win = highest payout a game can award from one round, usually expressed as a multiple of the bet; round ends and pays when cap reached. Cap includes base game + bonus + cascades + multipliers of that round. (win.gg/how-max-win-works-online-slots; slotdecoded.com/max-win-slots-explained; WebSearch summary)
- Developers set the cap during testing, typically 2,000x-50,000x of the bet; a small group of extreme titles goes to 500,000x. (win.gg)
- Cap scales with bet: multiplier fixed, cash value changes. (win.gg, slotdecoded)
- RTP and max win are separate; high max-win games typically have high volatility, lower hit frequency. (slotdecoded, win.gg)
- Gates of Olympus (Pragmatic Play): max win 5,000x; RTP 96.50% default, other configurations 95.51% and 94.50%; volatility high/very high; hit frequency 28.82% (about every 3.47 spins); max-win probability 1 in 718,391 spins; bets EUR 0.20-100. (SlotCatalog, OLBG; pragmaticplay.com page confirms RTP 96.50% and multipliers up to 500x but states no max win)
- Sweet Bonanza (Pragmatic Play): max win 21,100x; RTP 96.51%; volatility medium-high; hard ceiling in the math model, round ends and pays; achievable only in free-spins round; odds "tens of thousands of bonus rounds" (SlotCatalog, sweet-bonanza-australia.readme.io: secondary).
- Book of Dead (Play'n GO): max win 5,000x; RTP 96.21% default (lower builds 94.25/91.25/87.25/84.18); high volatility. (SlotCatalog)
- Extreme example: Nolimit City Tombstone Slaughter 500,000x cap, odds roughly 1 in 189 million spins (hearthstats.net, secondary source, approximate).
- Starburst max win: sources conflict (500x SlotCatalog vs 800x netent.com in an earlier article) -> NOT used.
