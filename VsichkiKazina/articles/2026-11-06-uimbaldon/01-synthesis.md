# 01-SYNTHESIS — vk-0326 Уимбълдън

**Intent:** informational. Reader searching „уимбълдън / уимбълдън история" wants to understand
what the tournament is, why it is special, how it works and a few memorable facts. NOT live
results/draws (those go stale; brief forbids them).

**Angle (distinct from vk-0310 Тенис правила):** that guide explains the rules of the game
(points, sets, tiebreak). This one is a PROFILE of the tournament: its age/history, the grass,
the traditions, its place among the Grand Slams, and the format specifics that are Wimbledon's
own (best-of-5 men, final-set tiebreak history, roofs). Cross-link to the rules guide rather than
re-teaching scoring from scratch.

**Load-bearing facts (verified, with sources in 00-brief):**
- 1877 first Championship; AELTC organiser; Church Road from 1922.
- Oldest tennis tournament; third Grand Slam of the year; only one on grass (AO left grass 1988).
- Events + years added (ladies' singles 1884; doubles/mixed 1913).
- Format best-of-5 men singles / best-of-3 others; final-set tiebreak history → 2022 unified 10-pt at 6-6.
- Centre Court ~14,979 + roof 2009; No.1 Court roof 2019.
- Traditions: near-all-white dress rule (firm from 1963), strawberries & cream, Royal Box, ryegrass from 2001.
- Seeding introduced 1924; 32 seeds in singles since 2001.

**Uncertainties → flags:**
- „Ягоди със сметана ↔ Хенри VIII" = мълва → present as legend, not fact.
- Exact all-time title counts dispute (all-time vs Open Era) → records framed „към 2026 г." + [VERIFY], no disputed numbers in the infographic.
- Dress-code origin: 1963 is the firm date for the enforced rule; „tradition" older but undocumented precisely.

**Tone:** editorial „ние"/neutral, dry, concrete, no hype. Signed Георги Тодоров. One RG touch +
the honest betting-reality paragraph (no predictions). 0 operators, 0 affiliate.
