### 1. The "Wrap-Up Bow" (Forced Paragraph Summary)
**Flagged text:** *"Трите водят до усещане за едри печалби по различен път."* (End of the "Къде се обърква с други механики" section).
**The Pattern:** AI models are trained to be helpful and conclusive, which means they almost always add a neat, summarizing sentence at the end of a paragraph to tie everything together. In human writing, especially technical explanations, you can just state the facts and move on without summarizing what you just said.
**Recommendation:** Delete this sentence entirely. The paragraph already perfectly explains the difference between expanding symbols, expanding wilds, and colossal symbols. You don't need a summary sentence to hold the reader's hand at the end.

### 2. The Didactic SEO Transition
**Flagged text:** *"Тези правила ги намираш в речника с казино термини и в правилата на самата игра, които винаги си струва да отвориш преди първия залог."*
**The Pattern:** AI (when prompted to include internal links) often creates a very mechanical, instructional sentence to force the link in. It breaks the narrative flow to give the reader a sudden piece of "best practice" advice just to justify the hyperlink.
