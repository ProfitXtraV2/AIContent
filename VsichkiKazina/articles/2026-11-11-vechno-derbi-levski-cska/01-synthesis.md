# 01 — Synthesis (fact bank)

Primary intent: informational — what the Левски–ЦСКА derby is, its history and its
landmark numbers. Reader is a fan/curious player, not a bettor seeking a tip.

## Verified-stable facts (printed as fact)
- Вечното дерби = the biggest match in Bulgarian football: Левски („сините") vs ЦСКА
  („червените"/„армейците"), the two most titled and popular clubs. (bg.wikipedia)
- First derby: 05.05.1948, софийско първенство, Левски 1:0, ~12 000 at стадион „Юнак";
  first derby goal Борислав Цветков‑Жук, 36'. ЦСКА founded 1948. (bg.wikipedia)
- Cup of Bulgaria balance: 22 Левски – 9 ЦСКА (both Wikipedia + dsport agree). Stable.
- Biggest margin: Левски 7:1 (23.09.1994); also 7:2 (17.11.1968). (bg.wikipedia)
- Record attendance: ~70 000, Националния стадион „Васил Левски", 11.03.1967, 1:1. (bg.wikipedia)
- 1948/49 Купа на Съветската армия final played 3 times (1:1, 2:2, Левски 2:1 on 17.05.1949) —
  longest final by playing time in BG football. (bg.wikipedia)
