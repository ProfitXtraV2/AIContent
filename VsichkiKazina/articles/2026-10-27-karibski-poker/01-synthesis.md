## Fact spine (each point traces to 00-brief sources)
1. It is a HOUSE game: you play against the dealer, not other players. No bluffing, no community
   cards, each hand independent. This is the single most important framing — strategy ≠ poker skill.
2. Structure: ante (compulsory) + optional progressive side bet. Then 5 cards each, one dealer card
   face up.
3. The one decision: after seeing your five, FOLD (lose the ante) or RAISE exactly 2× the ante. No
   draw, no swap.
4. Dealer qualification: A-K high or better. If the dealer does NOT qualify → ante 1:1, raise pushes.
5. If dealer qualifies: beat it → ante 1:1 + raise on the pay table; lose → both gone; tie → push.
6. Raise pay table (US): пара 1:1 · 2 чифта 2:1 · тройка 3:1 · кента 4:1 · флош 5:1 · фул 7:1 ·
   каре 20:1 · стрейт флош 50:1 · роял 100:1. Tables vary / some cap.
7. Maths: house edge 5,224% of the ante; element of risk 2,555% of total action; average total bet
   ≈ 2,045 единици.
8. Strategy (simple): raise on a pair or better, fold below ace-king. Refined ace-king cases add a
   sliver; simple version is near-optimal.
9. Progressive side bet: separate, pays flush+, royal = full meter / стрейт флош ~10%; fixed lower
   prizes vary by table (примерни: каре $500, фул $100, флош $50). Edge often >25% — expensive.

## De-AI / honest angle (the one real reader takeaway)
Caribbean Stud LOOKS like poker, but your skill ceiling is low: there is exactly one fold-or-raise
decision and a house edge of 5,224% per ante that no strategy removes. The 100:1 royal headline is
real but rare. The progressive is a second, pricier bet, not a feature of the base game. Honesty beat:
играта е негативна по очакване; единственото, което контролирате, е кога да пасувате.
