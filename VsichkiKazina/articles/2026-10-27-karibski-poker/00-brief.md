## FACTS (sourced — every figure traces to a source below)
- Played against the house/dealer, NOT against other players; no bluffing; no community cards;
  each hand is independent. [Wikipedia]
- Standard 52-card deck. Player places an ante; optionally an extra progressive side bet
  (usually $1 at live tables; online often smaller). [Wizard of Odds; Wikipedia]
- Deal: each player and the dealer get 5 cards; all face down except ONE of the dealer's cards,
  which is turned face up. [Wizard of Odds; Wikipedia]
- After seeing the five cards, the player either FOLDS (forfeits the ante) or PLAYS by placing a
  raise/bet of EXACTLY twice the ante. One decision only; no drawing/replacing cards. [Wizard of Odds; Wikipedia]
- Dealer qualifies with Ace + King or higher (i.e. at least ace-king high, or a pair or better). [Wizard of Odds; Wikipedia]
- Dealer does NOT qualify → ante pays even money (1:1) and the raise pushes (returned). [Wizard of Odds; Wikipedia]
- Dealer qualifies and player wins → ante pays 1:1, raise pays per the pay table below. [Wizard of Odds]
- Dealer qualifies and player loses → both ante and raise lose. Tie → both push. [Wizard of Odds; Wikipedia]
- U.S. raise pay table (on the raise, when dealer qualifies and player wins):
  Чифт или по-слабо 1:1 · Два чифта 2:1 · Тройка 3:1 · Кента 4:1 · Флош 5:1 · Фул хаус 7:1 ·
  Каре 20:1 · Стрейт флош 50:1 · Роял флош 100:1. [Wizard of Odds] (tables vary; some cap payouts — вариант)
- Base-game house edge: 5.224% of the ante (optimal play return -0.052243). [Wizard of Odds]
- Element of risk: 2.555% (expected loss relative to the average total wager). [Wizard of Odds]
- Average total amount bet ≈ 2.045 units (the player raises ≈ 52.23% of the time). [Wizard of Odds]
- Simple near-optimal strategy: always raise with a pair or better; always fold with less than
  ace-king. (A refined rule adds a few ace-king cases; the simple version costs only a sliver of
  edge — Wizard's refined strategy gives 5.225% vs 5.224% perfect.) [Wizard of Odds]
- Progressive side bet: optional, pays on flush or better; royal flush typically takes 100% of the
  progressive meter, straight flush ~10%; lower hands pay fixed amounts that VARY by table (a
  commonly cited US set: каре $500, фул хаус $100, флош $50 — примерни, varies by location).
  House edge on the side bet is high — frequently cited above 25% unless the jackpot is very large.
  [Wikipedia; general game references — mark fixed amounts as примерни/variable]
