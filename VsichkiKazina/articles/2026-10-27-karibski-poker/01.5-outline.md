1. ## Срещу казиното, не срещу масата
   - Independent hands, dealer is the only opponent, no community cards, no bluff. Why „покер" is

   - Shape: short, 2-3 sentences.

3. ## Раздаването
   - 5 cards each, one dealer card face up. You see your hand, dealer's rest hidden.
   - Shape: short prose.

4. ## Единственото решение: пас или залог
   - Fold → lose ante. Play → raise EXACTLY 2× ante. No draw. Worked € example (анте €10 → залог €20,
     общо €30 в игра). Carry the poker-hand-ranking link here (/blog/poker-kombinacii/) since you
     judge your five against the standard ranking.
   - Shape: worked figure, no takeaway one-liner.

5. ## Кога дилърът „играе" (квалификация A-K)
   - Dealer qualifies on A-K high or better. If NOT qualify → ante 1:1, raise pushes (returned).
     The counter-intuitive bit: a strong hand of yours earns only the ante when the dealer misses.
   - Shape: analytical paragraph + the non-qualify consequence.

8. ## Проста стратегия, която не греши много
   - Raise on a pair or better; fold below ace-king. Refined ace-king cases exist but add a sliver.
     Emphasise: strategy limits the damage, does not flip the edge.
   - Shape: plain rule, prose (not a numbered list), honest caveat.
