SECTION 1 — Какво е Тото Джокер и защо не е „допълнително число"
Facts: game of Български спортен тотализатор (БСТ); you play positions, not a number; factory or electronically generated slip number; draw sets 3 positions + 1 digit each; win when your positions carry the same digits. Myth: "допълнително число от 1 до 10" is wrong. Results board shows „Печеливши позиции" (e.g. 2, 3, 5) and „Печеливши числа" (e.g. 3, 9, 8). History: introduced with тираж 63 in 1999 [VERIFY].
Terms/entities: тото джокер, toto joker (Latin variant, once, naturally, e.g. as the spelling people search), БСТ, фабричен / електронно генериран номер, позиция–цифра.
Direction: Explain the position/digit mechanic in plain terms first, then correct the "extra number" myth using the results-board example as proof; history goes last as one sentence, not as an opener.
Shape: narrative build, 2 paragraphs, ~170 words.

SECTION 4 — Какъв е реалният шанс за печалба от Тото Джокер?
Facts: assumption: 3 different positions from 1–9, digit 0–9 for each, all outcomes equally likely [VERIFY draw method / equal digit likelihood]. C(9,3) = 84 → 1 на 84 for positions. I група: 1/84 × 1/1000 = 1 на 84 000. II група two paths: (a) all 3 positions drawn, exactly 2 digits right: 1/84 × 3 × (1/10)² × 9/10; (b) exactly 2 of your positions drawn (18 of 84 triples) and both digits right: 18/84 × 1/100. Sum 69/28 000 ≈ 1 на 406. Any prize: 13/5250 ≈ 1 на 404. Comparison: 6/49 main prize 1 на 13 983 816, ≈ 166 × harder than Joker I група.
Terms/entities: шанс за печалба от тото джокер (heading + once in body), I група, II група, ТОТО 2 – 6/49.
Direction: State the assumption up front with its flag, then walk positions → I група → II група (both paths, the non-obvious one is the value-add) → any prize; finish with the 6/49 comparison and a plain note that "more reachable" is not "likely".
Shape: worked numeric example with bolded step labels and a 2-item bullet for the II група paths, ~280–320 words. Longest section of the article.

SECTION 5 — Помагат ли системите?
Facts: >3 positions = system, all triples played. 4 positions = 4 combinations = €0,80; 5 = 10 = €2,00; all 9 = 84 = €16,80. With 4 positions I група chance = 4 на 84 000 = 1 на 21 000 at 4× the cost. Chance and cost rise in the same proportion; expected return per € unchanged.
Terms/entities: система (пълни комбинации).
Direction: Give the three cost points compactly, show the 4-position example, and land on the point that a system buys more tickets at once, it does not improve the game. Keep Joker-specific (anti-cannibalisation: vk-0270 owns systems in general).
Shape: short; 3-line compact list (positions → combinations → price) + 2–3 sentences. ~100 words. Use a list, not a table (only 3 items).

Shape: narrative build around a worked wallet example, 2–3 paragraphs, ~160 words. (Differs from Section 4: one running scenario, no step labels, no formulas.)
