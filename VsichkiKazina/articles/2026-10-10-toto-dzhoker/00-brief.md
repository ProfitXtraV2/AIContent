## ANGLE (locked)
Neutral educational lottery guide. How Тото Джокер of Български спортен тотализатор (БСТ) works —
it is a POSITION/DIGIT game tied to the factory (or electronically generated) number of a ТОТО 1 /
ТОТО 2 slip, NOT an "extra number 1–10" like foreign lotteries (some low-quality BG sites
describe it wrongly that way — do not repeat that error; a short myth-correction is welcome).
Explain: how you join (cannot be played alone), what is drawn, the two winning groups, the price,
the 50% prize fund and jackpot rollover, then the real odds computed from these mechanics with
working shown, expected value framing (half of the money comes back as prizes on average), the
„тото джокер 6 от 49" search intent (it rides on 6/49 AND the other ТОТО 1/ТОТО 2 slips, except
Рожден ден), and the „измами" angle. RG section. NO number/position picks, NO predictions, NO
"hot positions", NO operator promotion, NO affiliate links.

### ODDS — computed (pure combinatorics; verified with python3 Fractions + full enumeration)
MODEL / ASSUMPTIONS: a draw picks 3 DIFFERENT positions out of 1–9 and, for each, a digit 0–9,
every outcome equally likely. The fetched rules page confirms positions 1–9 and 3 pairs, but does
NOT publish the draw method or confirm that every digit 0–9 is equally likely → the figures below
must carry ONE [VERIFY] on the assumption (method shown in full).
- Ways to choose 3 positions from 9: C(9,3) = 84.
- I група (all 3 pairs): your 3 positions must be exactly the drawn 3 → 1/84; each of 3 digits
  must match → (1/10)^3 = 1/1000. P = 1/84 × 1/1000 = **1 / 84 000**.
- II група (exactly 2 pairs): (a) all 3 positions drawn, exactly 2 digits match:
  1/84 × 3 × (1/10)² × (9/10) = 0.027/84; (b) exactly 2 of your positions drawn (3 × 6 = 18 of 84
  draws) and both digits match: 18/84 × 1/100 = 0.18/84. Sum = 0.207/84 = **69/28 000 ≈ 1 на 406**.
- Any prize (I or II): 13/5250 ≈ **1 на 404**.
- Systems: 4 positions = C(4,3) = 4 combinations = €0.80; 5 positions = 10 combinations = €2.00;
  all 9 positions = 84 combinations = €16.80. More combinations raise the chance and the cost in
  the same proportion — expected return per € unchanged.
- Expected value: with 50% of receipts returned as prizes, on average **€0.10 of every €0.20**
  combination comes back over the long run (jackpot rollovers shift money between draws, they do
  not change the 50% share). Comparison point (for context only): C(49,6) = 13 983 816 for the
  6/49 main prize.

## SCAM ANGLE (general principles; no named fake sites)
- No system/„формула"/paid „печеливши позиции" can beat a random draw; anyone selling
  "guaranteed" Joker systems sells nothing (NАП: стратегиите за добър късмет не увеличават шансовете).
- Advance-fee pattern: unsolicited message „спечелихте от тото/лотария, платете такса, за да
  получите печалбата" — a real organiser does not ask for a fee to pay a prize. Secondary source:
  Revolut BG fraud education page
  https://help.revolut.com/bg-BG/help/security-logging-in/how-do-i-protect-myself-from-fraudsters/fraud-education-advance-fee-scam/
  (lottery-commission impersonation, upfront fee). Online winnings at БСТ are credited automatically
  to the player account (source pack) — no "claim fee" step exists.
- Look-alike/phishing sites: play only via БСТ's own channels/licensed points; check НАП's lists of
  licensed/unlicensed sites; report to illegal_gambling@nra.bg.
- Keep the slip/квитанция — it is the proof of a valid bet (rules page).
