## FACT INVENTORY (universal game-math — CORROBORATED, cite-ready)
1. Payline = a fixed/predefined line running from the first reel to the last; a win registers only when matching symbols land on an active line, read from the leftmost reel to the right on adjacent reels. Some slots also pay „both ways" (right-to-left). [1spin4win]
2. Fixed paylines: same number every spin regardless of stake (e.g. a slot „always has 27 paylines"). Adjustable/selectable paylines: player activates how many lines to play; stake is calculated per line, not per spin. [1spin4win]
3. Ways-to-win drops the fixed path: pays for matching symbols on adjacent reels starting from the leftmost, regardless of their vertical row position. 243 ways = 3 symbols high × 5 reels (3^5 = 243). 1024 ways = 4 symbols high × 5 reels (4^5 = 1024). [1spin4win; IGT via WebSearch]
4. Megaways: 6 reels, each showing 2–7 symbols, recalculated every spin; maximum 7^6 = 117 649 ways. Cluster-pays is a further variant (win by touching groups, not lines). [covers.com; 1spin4win]
5. Paylines do NOT determine RTP. More paylines change hit rate and payout size, not the return percentage. [1spin4win]
6. Losses disguised as wins (LDW): a payout smaller than the total stake on a spin, still accompanied by winning sounds/animations ("bet two dollars, win back fifty cents"). Exclusive to multiline betting; players wager many lines, so LDWs outnumber genuine wins. Findings: players show the same physiological response to LDWs as to real wins (Dixon et al., 2010); 58% preferred games with LDWs (Graydon et al., 2018); players verbally mislabel LDWs as wins and overestimate their win frequency (Jensen et al., 2013). [PMC6209046]
7. Cost per spin = залог на линия × брой линии (bet-per-line × number of lines). [derived arithmetic, universal]
