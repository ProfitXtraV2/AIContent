H1: Линии на печалба в ротативките: как всъщност печелите

SECTION 1 — Какво е линия на печалба
Facts: payline = predefined path across reels; matching symbols pay from leftmost reel rightwards on adjacent reels; symbol off an active line pays nothing; five-reel classic; some pay both ways.
Terms/entities: линия на печалба, печеливши линии, payline, барабани, съседни, най-ляв барабан.
Direction: State what a payline physically is and how a win registers, using the „symbol off the line pays nothing" detail so the reader immediately sees that on-screen clusters aren't wins. Do not open with a definition-textbook cadence.
Shape: single flowing analytical paragraph (4–5 sentences), no takeaway line.

SECTION 2 — Фиксирани и избираеми линии
Facts: fixed lines = constant every spin regardless of stake; adjustable/selectable = player picks count, stake per line; reducing lines removes paying paths.
Terms/entities: фиксирани линии, избираеми/регулируеми линии, залог на линия.
Direction: Draw the fixed-vs-selectable distinction and puncture the „fewer lines to save money" misread. End on the concrete consequence, not an aphorism.
Shape: short blunt build (2–3 sentences).

SECTION 3 — Начини за печалба: когато линията изчезва
Facts: ways-to-win = matching symbols on adjacent reels regardless of row; 243 = 3^5; 1024 = 4^5; Megaways 6 reels 2–7 symbols, max 7^6 = 117 649; cluster-pays a further variant; route Megaways/cluster deep-dive to /kazino-igri/ (slot listings) in prose.
Terms/entities: начини за печалба, ways-to-win, 243 начина, 1024 начина, Megaways, cluster-pays.
Direction: Explain how ways-to-win drops the fixed path, then give the configuration math for 243/1024/117 649 as plain arithmetic (not a step-paused walkthrough). Cross-reference Megaways/cluster as further variants covered elsewhere; do NOT re-explain them in depth (anti-cannibalization vs „Нови казино игри 2026" + fruit-slots). This section carries the heaviest fact load, so let it run longer.
Shape: narrative build with embedded numbers (5–6 sentences), longest section.

SECTION 4 — Повече линии не значи по-добри шансове
Facts: number of lines/ways does NOT improve odds or change RTP; RTP is the long-run return baked into the game math, independent of lines played; more lines = more frequent, smaller payouts; cost per spin = bet-per-line × number of lines; €0.02 × 20 = €0.40; €0.02 × 40 = €0.80 (illustrative).
Terms/entities: RTP, шансове, начини за печалба, залог на линия, общ залог, цена на завъртане.
Direction: Deliver the central honest point directly. Show the cost-per-spin arithmetic with the illustrative figures marked as such, then the „more simultaneous lottery tickets, not better odds" reframe. Start the calculation with the numbers, no signposting label.
Shape: worked numeric example inside prose (4–5 sentences).

SECTION 5 — Печалба, която е по-малка от залога
Facts: LDW = payout smaller than total stake, still celebrated with sound/animation; €0.40 staked on 20 lines, one line pays €0.20 → net minus €0.20; exclusive to multiline; Dixon 2010 (equal physiological response); Graydon 2018 (58% preferred LDW games); Jensen 2013 (mislabel/overestimate wins).
Terms/entities: загуба маскирана като печалба, LDW, залог, честота на печалбите.
Direction: This is the natural RG hook. Walk the concrete €0.20-on-€0.40 example, name it a loss dressed as a win, then bring the research to show it's a documented effect, not an opinion. Do not moralise; let the mechanism carry it.
Shape: narrative build (5–6 sentences) with cited research; second-longest.

SECTION 6 — Какво да гледате вместо броя линии
Facts: line count on the cover tells you nothing about odds; the two things that matter are total cost per spin and the game's RTP; RTP read as a real number in the six-criteria method (/kak-ocenyavame/); хазартът не е финансова стратегия; slot = paid entertainment with a knowable long-run cost.
Terms/entities: RTP, цена на завъртане, брой линии.
Direction: Close asymmetrically with the actionable verdict — ignore the line-count headline, look at spin cost and RTP. Link /kak-ocenyavame/ for RTP-as-a-number and /otgovorna-igra/ for the RG resource. End on the „many lines make the cost smoother to swallow, not smaller" line, not a balanced summary.
Shape: short opinionated close (3–4 sentences), then RG footer block.
