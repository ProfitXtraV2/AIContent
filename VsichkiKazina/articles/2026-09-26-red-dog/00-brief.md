## ANGLE
Single-decision card game: bet whether a third card lands strictly between the first two (the "spread").
DISTINCT vs Casino War (vk-0180) / Бакара (vk-0012) / Дракон Тигър (vk-0175): the whole game is about the WIDTH of the
gap between two cards and one raise decision, not a straight higher-card duel. Must cover: 1 deck, ace high, suits
irrelevant; spread = ranks strictly between; consecutive (spread 0) = push; pair → third card, match pays 11:1 else push;
spread ≥1 → call or raise, third card between = win by the payout table; payouts 5:1/4:1/2:1/1:1; the ONLY strategy
(raise only on spread 7+); house edge + the counter-intuitive deck-count effect (more decks = LOWER edge); element of risk.

## FACTS (all researched — see 06-verification.md; Wizard of Odds referee)
- 1 standard 52-card deck; two cards face up; ace high; suits irrelevant.
- spread = number of ranks strictly between the two cards.
- Consecutive (spread 0) → PUSH (wager returned). Pair → third card; matches the pair → 11:1; otherwise PUSH.
- Spread ≥1 → player may CALL or RAISE (add up to the original wager); third card strictly between → win per table; else lose.
- Payout by spread: 1 → 5:1 · 2 → 4:1 · 3 → 2:1 · 4–11 → 1:1. Matched pair (third card) → 11:1.
- House edge (single deck, optimal play): 3.155% (per initial wager); element of risk 2.672% (per money actually risked).
- Deck-count effect (Wizard): MORE decks LOWER the edge — 1: 3.155% · 2: 3.077% · 4: 2.884% · 6: 2.798% · 8: 2.751%.
- Optimal strategy: RAISE only when spread ≥ 7; otherwise call. Threshold = 7 (Wizard).
- RTP derived: single deck ≈ 96.85% (=100−3.155); 8 decks ≈ 97.25% (=100−2.751).
