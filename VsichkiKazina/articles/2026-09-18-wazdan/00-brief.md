## HONEST ANGLE (brand doctrine)
Volatility Levels is presented as a real convenience but NOT a way to change RTP or the house edge — it only shifts the shape of the wins, not the long-run maths. Max-win caps are theoretical ceilings, not expected outcomes. RTP is a long-run statistic over millions of spins; providers ship multiple RTP builds and the operator picks one → check the info panel. Studio licences ≠ casino licence; what matters for the BG player is the operator's НАП licence.
