## Core message
BF Games is a UK-registered B2B slot maker (since 2013, Bee-Fee/London, Malta subsidiary) whose HTML5 titles run in 25+ regulated markets including Bulgaria. Its games are tested (eCOGRA) and licensed as a supplier (UKGC, MGA), which confirms fairness to the stated rules, not a player advantage. Headline RTPs cluster around the modern ~96% average, but titles ship in several adjustable RTP builds so the operator chooses the number, and the BF Daily Jackpots progressive is funded from bets and does not lower the house edge. Check the real RTP in the info panel.
