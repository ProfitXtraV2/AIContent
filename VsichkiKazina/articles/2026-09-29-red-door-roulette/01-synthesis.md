# 01 — SYNTHESIS · Red Door Roulette (Evolution)

INTENT: informational / branded live-roulette explainer. Reader wants to know what Red Door
Roulette is, how the bonus works, and what the RTP really means before playing.

HONEST CORE (locked, drives the whole piece):
- The showpiece (keys, 2x–20x, a 64-segment Crazy Time-style bonus wheel to 4000x) is funded by
  ONE cut the paytable hides: straight-up pays 19:1, not 35:1.
- Entry to the bonus is a COMPOUND condition (bet on the exact winning number AND that number
  carries one of the 3–15 keys the machine marks) → rare by construction.
- RTP 97,09% (straight-up) / 97,30% (other bets) are long-run averages that already include the
  cut. House edge ≈2,9%/≈2,7% — same order as ordinary single-zero roulette. The feature reshapes
  the payout distribution; it does not remove the edge.
- Other bets (split 17:1, red/black 1:1, dozens/columns 2:1) are untouched — the cost is collected
  only from straight-up bets.

FACTS CONFIRMED (≥2 sources): single-zero 37-pocket European wheel; straight-up 19:1; keys 2x–20x;
3–15 bonus numbers/round; 64-segment bonus wheel with Double (doubles all + respins); max 4000x;
cash cap €500 000/round; RTP 97,09%/97,30%.

FLAGGED [VERIFY]: release date (Nov 2023 vs June 2023 conflict); exact segment composition / the
4000x ceiling (version-dependent, provider snippet + DBs). NOT claimed: a stake-multiple max-win
(sources conflict); betting-window length (not found).

PILLAR CONFIRMED DISTINCT: primary kw + mechanic differ from Lightning Roulette (vk-0186) and
XXXtreme Lightning Roulette (vk-0209). Key-condition + separate Crazy Time-style wheel is the
distinguishing mechanic; deepest straight-up cut (19:1).
