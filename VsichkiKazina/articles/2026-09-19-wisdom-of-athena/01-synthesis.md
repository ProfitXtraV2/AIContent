# 01 — Synthesis · Wisdom of Athena

Core claim: Wisdom of Athena (Pragmatic Play, 2023) is a scatter-pays / tumble slot in the same
engine family as Sweet Bonanza and Gates of Olympus, but with its own multiplier logic.

Facts locked (sourced):
- Grid 6 reels × 5 rows; pay-by-count, min 8 identical symbols anywhere. [pragmaticplay.com/BigWinBoard]
- Tumble/cascade; a progressive grid unlock adds play area during a sequence. [BigWinBoard]
- NO wild. Multiplier symbols x2–x500 land and are SUMMED at the end of each tumble sequence. [BigWinBoard]
- Free spins: 4+ scatter → 10 FS; 3+ scatter in a sequence → +5 FS; collected multiplier held across round. [BigWinBoard]
- RTP default 96.07% (lower builds 95.09%, 94.09%). House edge 3.93%. [pragmaticplay.com/BigWinBoard/ClashOfSlots]
- Max win 5000x; volatility high; bet ~€0.20–€100; ante +25%; bonus buy 100x. [BigWinBoard/ClashOfSlots]
- WoA 1000 (2024) is a distinct title: cap x1000, max 10000x, RTP ~96%. [BigWinBoard]

Angle: "по-висок таван ≠ по-добри шансове"; multipliers are the whole story and they are rare.
