### 2. The "Not X, but Y" Antithesis Overload
**The Pattern:** Formulaic Symmetrical Phrasing. AI loves to sound profound by constantly contrasting what something *is* with what it *isn't*. Used once, it’s good writing; used five times in a short article, it creates a robotic, predictable rhythm.
**The Quotes:**
*   *"залагаш спечеленото, не основния си банкрол"*
*   *"гризе печалбата ти вместо депозита"*

*   *"начин да харчиш банкрола по-бързо, не по-умно"*
**The Recommendation:** Break the symmetry in at least half of these instances. For example, instead of the poetic "не са сметка, а усещане" in the final section, state the psychological reality directly (e.g., "Системите създават илюзия за контрол..."). ### 3. Dramatic Personification of Math
**The Pattern:** Narrated Emotion / Personification. To make dry mathematical concepts engaging, AI often assigns human intent or physical actions to numbers and probabilities.
