## Key facts carried into the draft (public law; 0 operator facts, 0 fabricated figures)
- Data collected in 3 groups: identity (name, ID/document, address, DOB), financial (card/account), behavioural (bet history, device, IP, session time).
- Reason: largely a legal obligation under KYC + AML; the regulator wants to know who plays, that they are 18+, and the money's origin.
- Framework: GDPR = Регламент (ЕС) 2016/679 + ЗЗЛД; supervisory authority КЗЛД; licensed operator must publish a privacy policy, state processing purposes, appoint a DPO.
- GDPR rights: access, rectification, portability, object to marketing/profiling + withdraw consent, restriction; complaint to КЗЛД.
- Right to erasure LIMITED: AML requires retaining KYC + transaction records for a set period after account closure (commonly ~5 years) — cannot be deleted during that period. Erasure clears marketing profile, not the AML-held identity/transaction records.
- Marketing consent is separate and withdrawable without closing the account.
- Licence-first: licensed = GDPR-bound + DPO + КЗЛД oversight; offshore may not honour GDPR → data exposed with no local recourse.
- Retention "~5 години" stated qualitatively ("обикновено около пет години") — general AML standard (ЗМИП); human may confirm exact BG period at publish.
