## GAME FACTS (web-verified — see SOURCES; uncertainty → [VERIFY])
- Provider: NetEnt. Release: 23.01.2009 (NetEnt official page).
- Grid: 5 reels × 3 rows; 25 fixed paylines (bet lines). Luxury theme (yachts, limousines, champagne, watches).
- Overall RTP: 96.60% (NetEnt official).
- RTP breakdown (fruityslots): base game RTP ~89% (base spins 62.8% + free spins 16.8% + bonus wheel 9.3%) + 7.6% contribution to the progressive jackpots = 96.6% headline. HOUSE margin = 100 − 96.6 = 3.4%.
- Volatility: low (bigwinboard, mvideoslots). NetEnt lists a 53% figure = hit frequency, not volatility rank.
- Max win excluding jackpots: 1,700× bet (NetEnt official). (mvideoslots quotes 2,162× — provider figure preferred.)
- Three progressive jackpots: Rapid (small, frequent), Major (middle), Mega (top, network/global). NetEnt estimated Mega/global value ~€4,000,000.
- Bonus wheel: land 3 bonus-wheel symbols on an active payline (leftmost reel onwards) → the bonus game with 3 concentric wheels. You STOP the wheel; landing on an arrow moves you inward toward bigger jackpots, a coin gives a cash prize and ends the round. Rapid on the outer wheel, Major/Mega on the inner (level 3 holds all three).
- Free spins: 3+ champagne scatters → pick a scatter to reveal number of free spins + a multiplier.
- Record win: €17,861,813 on 20.01.2013, at Paf (Åland), on a €0.25 bet; Guinness World Record for the largest online slot jackpot at the time (held until Oct 2015). VERIFIED historical context only.

## HONEST ANGLE (brand doctrine — core to this piece)
The 96.6% headline is padded by the 7.6% that is skimmed into the jackpot pool from players' own wagers. The return you actually feel in normal play is ~89%, because that 7.6% only comes back through jackpots almost nobody wins. The pool is funded by the players, not the operator. Real odds of the Mega tier are lottery-scale (network progressive) — reference the concept hub, do not duplicate its odds math. No promise words.
