## Core message
Mega Fortune is a 2009 NetEnt luxury slot (5x3, 25 lines) whose fame rests on a network progressive with three tiers (Rapid/Major/Mega) won on a bonus wheel. The headline RTP of 96.6% includes 7.6% that funds the jackpots from players' own bets; the return you experience in normal play is around 89%. The pool is player-funded and the Mega tier is lottery-scale.
