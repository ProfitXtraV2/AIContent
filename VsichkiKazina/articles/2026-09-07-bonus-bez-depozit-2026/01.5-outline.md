OPENING (no heading) — SHAPE: honest reframe, flowing. Direction: the ONE offer where you can
win real money without risking your own — which is why its terms are the tightest. It's a trial
access with a ceiling, not a windfall. Avoid „не A, а B". Terms: бонус без депозит, казино без депозит.

§1 — "Колко е „малката сума"" — SHAPE: short analytical paragraph, no worked number.
Direction: no-deposit bonuses are small by design (€5–€20 or 20–50 FS); the operator gives
enough to feel the site, no more. A big no-deposit bonus on an unknown site is a caution signal.

§2 — "Сметката, която банерът пропуска" — SHAPE: worked € calculation delivered directly (NO
command-verb bridge, NO false climax). Direction: €10 bonus × 40 wagering (base = само бонус,

— a €120 notional win pays €50; plus срок (7 дни → ~€57/day) and макс. залог (€5).

§3 — "Верификацията (KYC) и тегленето" — SHAPE: single build correcting a misconception.
Direction: "без депозит" ≠ "без формалности"; withdrawal still needs KYC (ID, sometimes address),
occasionally a small verification deposit. Catches no-deposit players most. Link: /depoziti-i-teglenia/.

§4 — "Лицензът от НАП" — SHAPE: the BG-specific turn (fold "казино бонус без депозит българия").
Direction: a no-deposit bonus is only real if the operator holds a valid НАП licence; from an
unlicensed site it's a lure with no protection on winnings. First check = the public НАП register,
before the bonus size. Link: /zakonno-li-e/.
