## Search intent (one line)
The searcher wants a no-deposit bonus (real money / free spins without depositing) and, beneath
that, "is it worth it / what's the catch". A useful pillar delivers the honest breakdown, not a
sales pitch, and names the catches: cap, wagering, KYC, licence.

## Gaps competitors leave (our differentiator)
1. Listing pages sell the offer; nobody computes the REAL ceiling — the max-cashout cap means
   even a cleared bonus pays at most €50–€100.
2. The KYC surprise: "без депозит" makes players think "без формалности"; withdrawal still needs
   ID (sometimes a small verification deposit).
3. The BG licence-first point: an unlicensed no-deposit bonus is a lure; winnings there have no
   protection. First check is the НАП register, not the bonus size.

## Facts / figures (ALL illustrative — none sourced)
- Size: typically €5–€20 bonus credit or 20–50 free spins.
- Worked: €10 no-deposit bonus × 40 wagering (само бонус) = €400 turnover; a €120 notional win
  is capped to a €50 max-cashout; срок 7 дни → €400/7 ≈ €57/day; макс. залог напр. €5.
- Free-spins variant: 30 FS × €0,10 = €3 of risk-free play; winnings carry the same wagering + cap.
- Slots contribute ~100%; base is само бонус (no deposit exists to add).
