*   Final H2: *"Какво реално получавате"*

**The Issue:** LLMs love to tie an article up in a neat bow by mirroring the exact phrasing or premise of the title in the final heading. It creates a "full circle" structure that feels slightly artificial and repetitive to a human reader who has already absorbed the premise.

**The Recommendation:** Change the final H2 so it doesn't echo the title. Make it a conclusive statement rather than a repeated question/premise. 
*   *Actionable fix:* Change the H2 to something like "Финалната сметка" (The final calculation) or "Струва ли си времето?" (Is it worth the time?).

### 2. Pattern: The "Neat Bow" Balanced Summary Formula
**The Flagged Text:** *"Бонусът без депозит е най-нискорисковият начин да пробваш казино: не залагаш свои пари, губиш най-много малко време."*

**The Issue:** This perfectly balanced, colon-separated summary sentence (*"X is the most Y way to Z: [benefit 1], [benefit 2]"*) is a classic LLM concluding rhythm. AI uses this structure to rapidly synthesize the preceding paragraphs before delivering the final warning.

**The Recommendation:** Break the mechanical rhythm of this sentence. Make it punchier and less formulaic.

### 3. Pattern: Additive / Mechanical Transitioning
**The Flagged Text:** *"Към сметката се добавя и приносът на игрите: 400 € оборот се трупат бързо на ротативки..."* (Under the H2: *Сметката, която банерът пропуска*)
