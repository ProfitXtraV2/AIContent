- Studio: Yggdrasil Gaming (игдрасил). Founded 2013 by Fredrik Elmqvist (ex-NetEnt). Swedish roots, HQ Malta (San Ġiljan / Sliema); dev office Krakow (Poland).
- Ownership: grew within the Swedish gaming group Cherry AB; Cherry taken private in 2019 by a consortium led by the private-equity fund Bridgepoint — studio is PE-backed today. Elmqvist stepped down as CEO in April 2022 (after 9 years), stayed on the board.
- Licences (B2B, on the studio): Malta MGA B2B Critical Supply (Type 1 & 3), UK Gambling Commission B2B, Gibraltar, Sweden, Romania, Isle of Man, Greece, Belgium.
- Technology: GATI framework — regulation-ready standardised toolkit for fast multi-market distribution; YGG Masters program licenses GATI to partner studios (ReelPlay, Peter & Sons, Bang Bang Games, Hot Rise Games).
- Mechanics (GEMs — Game Engagement Mechanics, rolled out from 2020): Splitz (symbols split into more symbols, up to 248,832 ways — Temple Stacks); GigaBlox (oversized symbols up to 6x6, first in Lucky Neko, 2020); MultiMax; GigaRise + DoubleMax (2021); Wild Fight + DuoMax (2022). Multiplier Wilds = sticky wilds carrying multipliers (Vikings series, Berzerk state up to 8x).
- 2021 IGT distribution deal opened North America (US/Canada regulated jurisdictions).
