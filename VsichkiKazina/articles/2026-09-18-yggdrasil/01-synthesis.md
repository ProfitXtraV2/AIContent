## SYNTHESIS REPORT

### Entity union (what a complete answer must cover)
1. Who the studio is: founded 2013, founder Fredrik Elmqvist (ex-NetEnt), Swedish roots, HQ Malta.
2. Ownership: part of the Swedish group Cherry AB; Cherry taken private in 2019 by a Bridgepoint-led consortium (private equity). Founder stepped down as CEO in 2022, stayed on the board.
3. Signature technology: GATI framework (regulation-ready standardised toolkit, fast multi-market distribution) + YGG Masters (licensing GATI to partner studios — ReelPlay, Peter & Sons, Bang Bang Games, Hot Rise Games).
4. Signature mechanics (GEMs, from 2020): Splitz (splitting symbols, up to 248,832 ways), GigaBlox (oversized symbols up to 6x6, first in Lucky Neko), Multiplier Wilds (sticky wilds with multipliers, Vikings series), plus MultiMax / GigaRise / DoubleMax / Wild Fight / DuoMax.
5. Well-known slots with their numbers: Valley of the Gods, Temple Stacks: Splitz, Lucky Neko: Gigablox, Vikings Go Berzerk / Reloaded, Jokerizer.
6. The RTP caveat: certified RTP versions differ; the number by the name is the game's, not the casino's.
7. What it means for the BG player: the studio's B2B licences are not the operator's НАП licence; the latter is what regulates the player.
8. Caveats: high-max modern titles are volatile; Multiplier Wilds/Gigablox change the shape, not the house edge; demo mode before real money.

## ORIGINAL DRAFT (facts in, expression out — for the outline/author to restructure)

Yggdrasil is a Swedish-rooted studio, founded 2013 by former NetEnt man Fredrik Elmqvist, now headquartered in Malta. It grew inside the Cherry AB group; Cherry was taken private in 2019 by a Bridgepoint-led private-equity consortium, so the studio is PE-backed. Its reputation rests less on any one theme and more on two things: a technology platform (GATI, licensed to partner studios via YGG Masters) and a family of signature mechanics (GEMs) rolled out from 2020 — Splitz, GigaBlox and Multiplier Wilds chief among them. Splitz splits symbols to multiply ways to win (Temple Stacks reaches 248,832 ways); GigaBlox drops oversized symbols up to 6x6 (first in Lucky Neko); Multiplier Wilds stick and carry multipliers (the Vikings series). Well-known titles: Valley of the Gods (96.20% RTP, up to 5 800x, 2017), Temple Stacks: Splitz (96.10%, up to 25 000x, 2020), Lucky Neko: Gigablox (96.40%, up to 6 950x, 2020), Vikings Go Berzerk Reloaded (96.00%, up to 25 000x, 2021), plus the 2016 original Vikings Go Berzerk and the minimalist early classic Jokerizer (up to 98% in Jokerizer mode). RTP is not one number: each title ships in several certified versions and the casino picks which to load. For the BG player, the studio's B2B licences (MGA, UKGC, Gibraltar and others) confirm the RNG and RTP are independently tested, but they are the studio's, not the operator's — the operator's НАП licence is what regulates the player.
