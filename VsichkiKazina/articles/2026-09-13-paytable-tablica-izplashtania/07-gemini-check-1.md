### Flagged Passages, Patterns, and Recommendations

**1. Pattern: Formulaic Topic Sentences (Signposting)**
*   **Flagged Text:** *"Печалбата се образува по три различни модела и таблицата казва кой е в сила при тази игра."*
*   **Why it reads like AI:** AI models love to announce exactly what they are about to do before doing it. Stating "there are three different models and the table tells you which one applies" is a classic LLM transition mechanism used to set up a list or a paragraph of definitions.
*   **Recommendation:** Make the transition sharper and less didactic. Suggestion: Drop the setup about "three different models" entirely and jump straight into how fixed lines, ways-to-win, and clusters dictate the payouts. 

**2. Pattern: Over-explaining / Didactic Clarification**
*   **Flagged Text:** *"Символите се делят на високи и ниски по стойност, а таблицата казва колко от тях трябват за печалба, тоест 3, 4 или 5, и колко плаща всеки брой."*
*   **Why it reads like AI:** The insertion of *"тоест 3, 4 или 5"* feels like an LLM trying to be overly helpful by defining basic parameters that the reader can already infer. It interrupts the natural flow of the sentence with unnecessary micro-details.
*   **Recommendation:** Remove the explicit *"тоест 3, 4 или 5"* clarification. Let the sentence state that the table shows how many symbols are needed for a win, without spelling out the exact numbers.

**3. Pattern: Repetitive Hedging (Robotic Caution)**
*   **Flagged Text:** 
    *   *"Стойностите са примерни, но разликата..."* (Paragraph 2)
    *   *"Числата са примерни."* (Image 1 caption)
    *   *"Праговете са примерни и се различават..."* (Paragraph 4)
    *   *"Стойностите са примерни."* (Image 2 caption)
*   **Why it reads like AI:** When AI is prompted to be compliant or cautious (especially in gambling content), it tends to latch onto a single hedging word and repeat it mechanically across the text. The repetition of *"примерни"* (exemplary/illustrative) four times creates a slight robotic rhythm.
*   **Recommendation:** Vary the vocabulary or trust the reader's context. You can change some instances to synonyms like *"ориентировъчни"* (orientational) or *"илюстративни"* (illustrative), or simply delete the disclaimer in the text where the surrounding context already makes it obvious that you are giving an example.

**4. Pattern: The "Checklist" Summary Setup**
*   **Flagged Text:** *"Преди първото завъртане с истински пари отвори таблицата и мини през няколко неща: RTP-а, дали печалбите се смятат ×залог или ×залог на линия, къде е прагът за бонуса и какъв е таванът на максималната печалба."*
*   **Why it reads like AI:** While this is good, actionable advice, summarizing the exact H2s of the article in a neat comma-separated list right before the conclusion is a very common LLM structural habit (the "recap before the wrap-up"). 
*   **Recommendation:** Instead of listing the specific elements again, condense this to advise the player to simply "check the math and the rules" before spinning. You've already educated them on what those are in the body of the text; you don't need to list them again.
