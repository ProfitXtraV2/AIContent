SECTION 1 — [intro, no heading] Договорът на играта е в екрана „i"
Facts: paytable/info screen discloses in one place — symbol values, how many symbols pay & on what, special symbols, RTP, волатилност, max win, features. Reading it before the first real spin = informed vs banner-truster.
Terms/entities: таблица с изплащания, paytable, пейтабъл, информационен екран.
Direction: open on the concrete contrast between what the banner shows and what the info screen documents; land the "read it first" claim. Do NOT open with a rhetorical question or a known-fact-then-but pivot. No literal doctrine quote.
Shape: narrative build, 3–4 sentences.

SECTION 2 — Къде е бутонът и какво отваря
Facts: „i" / „?" / menu / pause; game pauses while reading; screen scrolls; some games split rules across tabs (символи / линии / функции / информация).
Terms/entities: информационен екран, бутон „i".
Direction: purely practical — how to physically open and navigate it. Keep it short and unfussy.
Shape: short blunt statement, 2 sentences, no worked example.

SECTION 3 — Колко струва всеки символ
Facts: high vs low symbols; how many needed (3/4/5) and value per count; the ×залог vs ×залог на линия confusion. Illustrative: залог €1 / 20 линии → €0,05 на линия; символ 10× на линия = €0,50, не €10 (примерни).
Terms/entities: стойност на символите, високи/ниски символи, ×залог, ×залог на линия.
Direction: explain symbol values, then make the ×bet vs ×line-bet confusion concrete with ONE illustrative euro figure marked примерни. Do not end on a mirrored „не A, а B".
Shape: single flowing analytical paragraph with one embedded worked figure (~110 words). [INFOGRAPHIC 1: paytable-elementi.svg — what the paytable contains — placed at the end of this section, the natural "anatomy" spot.]

SECTION 4 — Линии, начини за печалба или „навсякъде"
Facts: fixed paylines (e.g. 20); ways-to-win (243/1024) pay adjacent by reels; pay-anywhere/cluster/scatter pays; direction left-to-right vs both ways.
Terms/entities: брой линии, начини за печалба, pay-anywhere, cluster, посока на изплащане.
Direction: distinguish the three payout models plainly and note the direction check; link the glossary for the paylines/ways concept. Avoid counting-signpost prose („Първата… Втората…") — describe them as distinct models without numbering them in the sentence rhythm.
Shape: narrative build, ~90 words. Internal link: /blog/rechnik-kazino-termini/.

SECTION 5 — Wild, scatter, bonus и прагът за задействане
Facts: wild replaces; scatter pays regardless of position and/or triggers a feature; bonus triggers; trigger threshold (e.g. 3+ scatter → free spins), примерно.
Terms/entities: специални символи, wild, scatter, bonus, безплатни завъртания.
Direction: say what each special symbol does and where the bonus trigger threshold lives in the table; one illustrative threshold, marked примерно. Glossary link for the wild/scatter definitions if not already spent.
Shape: single flowing paragraph, ~75 words.

SECTION 6 — RTP, волатилност и таванът на печалбата
Facts: RTP = long-run statistic over millions of spins, never a session promise; info screen often to 2 decimals (примерно 96,42%); multiple RTP builds exist → the info-screen number is the one THIS game runs; волатилност ниска=чести малки / висока=редки големи, budget/temperament fit not a quality ranking; max win = ×залог ceiling (примерно 5000×), not a promise.
Terms/entities: RTP в играта, волатилност, макс. печалба, ×залог.
Direction: three linked facts, each stated with its honesty caveat. This is the heaviest section. Do NOT stack symmetric „колкото по-X, толкова по-Y" or „ниска значи…; висока — обратното" glossary balance — vary the explanation lengths. Link the concept glossary here if not spent; link kak-raboti-razigravaneto only if bonus money is mentioned (it is, via free-spins winnings context) — keep natural.
Shape: narrative build, longest section ~150 words.

Terms/entities: демо режим.
Direction: a short practical pre-play routine, then demo. This is the one section allowed to read as a compact sequence, but keep it as fluid prose, not a textbook condition→action list and not numbered prose. Link demo guide.
