## ANGLE (the pillar's spine — Тодоров's doctrine applied to games)
"Банерът е реклама, таблицата с изплащания е договорът." The paytable (the i / info screen)
is where the game discloses, in one place, exactly how it pays: symbol values, how many
symbols and on what (line vs anywhere), the special symbols, the game's RTP, волатилност,
max win cap, and how the features work. Reading it for 30 seconds before the first real spin
is the whole difference between an informed player and someone trusting the banner. Honest
close: the paytable will not tell you when you will win — nothing does — but it tells you
what you are actually playing.

## WHAT THE PAYTABLE CONTAINS (section spine — vary shapes)
1. HOW TO OPEN IT — the „i", menu or paytable button; usually paused, scrollable; some
   games split „правила" across tabs.
2. СТОЙНОСТ НА СИМВОЛИТЕ — high vs low symbols; how many needed to pay (3/4/5); whether the
   payout is ×залог (×bet) or ×залог на линия (×line bet) — a real source of confusion.
3. ЛИНИИ vs НАЧИНИ ЗА ПЕЧАЛБА — fixed paylines, „ways" (напр. 243/1024), or pay-anywhere
   (cluster/scatter pays); left-to-right vs both ways. (Link paylines concept if useful.)
4. СПЕЦИАЛНИ СИМВОЛИ — wild, scatter, bonus; the paytable states what each does and the
   bonus trigger threshold. (Link wild/scatter glossary.)
5. RTP и ВОЛАТИЛНОСТ — most games disclose RTP in the info screen (often to 2 decimals) and
   a volatility indicator; note some games ship in multiple RTP builds, so the number in the
   info screen is the one that game actually runs. МАКС. ПЕЧАЛБА as a ×bet cap.
6. КАК СЕ ЧЕТЕ ПРЕДИ ИГРА — a short practical routine: check RTP, check max win, check if
   wins are ×bet or ×line, check the bonus trigger; then demo.

## FACTS / HONESTY
- RTP = long-run statistic over millions of spins, never a session promise (link concept).
- Volatility = temperament/budget fit, ниска = чести малки, висока = редки големи; not a
  quality ranking.
- Max win is a ×bet ceiling, not a promise.
- Do NOT cite a specific game's RTP as fact; if an example number is used, mark примерни.
