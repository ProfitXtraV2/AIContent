## HONEST ANGLE (brand doctrine)
Endorphina is the software maker, not the operator: the casino takes the bets and holds the money. Certification (GLI/RNG) confirms the game runs by its stated rules and the advertised RTP is real, but those rules still build in the house edge; a certificate is not a player advantage. Typical ~96% RTP means a long-run house edge of ~4%; the real figure depends on the title and the build the operator runs, so check the info panel. Risk/gamble game does NOT change the long-run edge; it just adds swings.
