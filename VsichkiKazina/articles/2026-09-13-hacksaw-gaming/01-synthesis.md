# 01 — Synthesis (Hacksaw Gaming provider profile)

## Angle
A studio a Bulgarian player meets often in the newer, high-variance corner of a casino lobby,
without knowing the name. The value of the piece: explain WHO makes these games, WHAT the house
style is (modern high volatility + Feature Buy), and HOW to read their headline numbers honestly.

## Key facts locked (from reachable public pages)
- Founded 2017, Msida, Malta; started with scratchcards / instant-win, pivoted to video slots ~2019.
- Additional presence in Sweden; studio-level licences MGA + UKGC among others.
- House temperament: high volatility is the default, not a fluke of one title.
- Signature mechanic: Feature Buy (купуване на бонус), often tiered price points.
- RTP shipped in multiple versions per title; operator chooses → check in-game info panel.

## Numbers to carry consistently through all stages
- Wanted Dead or a Wild: 12,500x · RTP 96.38% (down to 88.42%) · висока волатилност · Feature Buy 80x/200x/400x
- Chaos Crew: 10,000x · висока волатилност · RTP около 96% (VERIFY exact)
- Le Bandit: 10,000x · RTP 96.30%
- RIP City: 12,500x · RTP 96.22% · средна волатилност · Maxx bonus 200x
- Worked RTP example: обявен RTP 96.38% → при €1000 оборот средно ~€964 обратно, ~€36 за казиното (house edge ~3.62%).

## Honest through-line
Max-win multipliers are ceilings, not expectations; feature buy is paying up-front for variance,
not better odds; the lower-RTP version is the trap → info panel.

## Anti-cannibalization
Reference /blog/games-providers/ for the general concept; do not re-teach "what is a provider".
Distinct from Pragmatic/Amusnet/Novomatic/NetEnt/Play'n GO profiles: this one leans on the
high-volatility + Feature Buy identity, not a broad "games for everyone" catalogue.
