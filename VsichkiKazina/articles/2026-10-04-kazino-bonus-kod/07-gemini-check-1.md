Here is the breakdown of the patterns and how to fix them.

### 1. Pattern: Formulaic Enumeration / Signposting
**The Quote:** *"Полето за код се появява на три места по пътя на играча. Първото е формата за регистрация... Второто е касата при депозит... Третото е разделът „Бонуси“..."*
**The Issue:** LLMs struggle with fluid transitions and often default to robotic, ordinal counting (First, Second, Third) to organize information. This creates a staccato, textbook-like rhythm that rarely occurs in natural human web writing.
**The Recommendation:** Strip out the explicit counting ("Първото е", "Второто е", "Третото е"). Blend these locations into a natural sequence of events — a player might see it during registration, later at the cashier, or inside their profile — without numbering the steps.

### 2. Pattern: Didactic Contrasts / Antithesis
**The Quotes:**
*   *"Низ от няколко букви и цифри сам по себе си не добавя пари към сметката ви."*
*   *"Кодът е бърз, условията под него не са."*
**The Issue:** AI writing models love to open paragraphs with dramatic, slightly philosophical contrasting statements. When overused, it makes the text sound like a lecture rather than a helpful guide.
**The Recommendation:** Soften these dramatic hooks. Get straight to the functional definition without the philosophical preamble; for the second, simply state that entering the code takes seconds but understanding the terms requires attention.

### 3. Pattern: The "Magic Number" Grouping

**The Issue:** AI frequently synthesizes information by grouping it into a definitive numbered set ("the 5 numbers that decide everything"). It feels overly polished and formulaic.
**The Recommendation:** Remove the "five numbers" framing. Simply list the crucial factors without boxing them into a numerical count.

### 4. Pattern: Robotic Phrasing / Over-formal Directives
