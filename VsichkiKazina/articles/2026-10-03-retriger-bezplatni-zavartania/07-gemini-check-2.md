### **Flagged Passages, Patterns, and Recommendations**

#### 1. Pattern: Formulaic Categorization (The "Rule of Three")
> **Quote:** *"Едни заглавия повтарят горе-долу първоначалния брой завъртания, други добавят само фиксиран и обикновено по-малък, а трета група направо нулира брояча и пуска рунда отначало."*

*   **Why it flagged:** AI models love to break variables into exactly three neat, perfectly balanced buckets ("Some do X, others do Y, and a third group does Z"). It reads like a textbook categorization rather than a natural explanation.
*   **Recommendation:** Break the rigid, symmetrical rhythm of this sentence. You can combine the first two scenarios into a general rule, and present the "resetting the counter" as a wild exception. This makes the pacing feel more organic and less like a generated list.

#### 2. Pattern: The Aphoristic Summary (Over-polished concluding sentence)
> **Quote:** *"Хазартът не е финансова стратегия, а ротативката е платено забавление с предварително известна дългосрочна цена; ретригерът удължава забавлението, без да сваля тази цена."*

*   **Why it flagged:** AI frequently ends sections with a perfectly balanced, slightly preachy, neatly tied-up philosophical summary. The mirrored structure here (*"платено забавление... цена"* / *"удължава забавлението... цена"*) separated by a semicolon is a classic LLM rhetorical device used to wrap up a thought with a "bow."
*   **Recommendation:** Keep the core message, but dismantle the mirrored symmetry. Make it punchier. You could split this into two shorter, blunter sentences. Let the thought end abruptly rather than wrapping it up in a poetic, slogan-like bow. 

#### 3. Pattern: Bolted-on SEO Signposting
> **Quote:** *"Как претегляме тези неща, когато оценяваме една игра, сме описали в метода си на оценяване."*

*   **Why it flagged:** While internal linking is necessary, appending a standalone "we wrote about this here" sentence at the very end of a paragraph is a common AI/SEO writer crutch. It disrupts the natural flow of the advice and feels mechanically inserted.
*   **Recommendation:** Integrate the reference organically into the preceding sentence. Instead of a standalone signpost, weave the mention of your evaluation methods directly into the sentence where you discuss checking the game's rules or maximum win caps.

#### 4. Pattern: Didactic / "Math Problem" Framing
> **Quote:** *"Самото влизане в бонуса от нулата е друга история, разказана в разбора за scatter символите; оттук нататък приемаме, че вече сте вътре в рунда."*

*   **Why it flagged:** The phrase *"оттук нататък приемаме"* (from here on, we assume) mimics the logic-setting parameters often found in AI prompts or math textbooks. It’s a bit too instructional.
*   **Recommendation:** Soften the transition. Instead of explicitly telling the reader what "we are assuming," simply state that this article focuses strictly on what happens *after* the bonus has already started, leaving the initial trigger to the other guide.
