### B. How the jackpot accumulates (rollover)
- When nobody matches all 6 numbers, the first-category (jackpot) fund is not paid out and is carried
  over into the next draw, adding to it a share of the new draw's stakes — this is why 6/49 and 6/42
  reach millions. (General mechanism — described in news coverage, e.g. novinite 10.06.2010: „Nobody
  won the jackpot … so the prize rolled over to about BGN 5.5 M"; БСТ campaign page action.toto.bg
  on the 2026 6/49 run.)
- Prize fund: old rules mirror (toto2-info.w-bg.net, undated, partly garbled) indicates the prize fund
  for 6/49 is **not less than 50% of the stakes** of each draw → [VERIFY: current БСТ rules for 6/49 —
  % награден фонд и дял на I категория]. Do not state the jackpot's percentage share.
- If several players hit the jackpot, it is split equally between them (2019: 9 501 430.40 лв.
  „разделен от четирима щастливци" — action.toto.bg). Bigger jackpots → more tickets sold → higher
  chance the jackpot is SHARED; the odds per combination do not change.
- Any cap / forced roll-down rule for 6/49: NOT found in any reachable source → [DATA NEEDED] if
  mentioned; better not to claim one exists or not.

### F. Odds of the jackpot (pure combinatorics — show the working; python3-checked)
- 6/49: C(49,6) = 49·48·47·46·45·44 / 6! = **13 983 816** → 1 към 13 983 816 per combination.
- 6/42: C(42,6) = **5 245 786** → 1 към 5 245 786.
- 5/35: C(35,5) = 324 632 per draw (but no jackpot amount shown in the pack — mention only as contrast
  or skip).
- Time illustration: with one combination in every draw (2 draws/week ≈ 104 per year), expected wait
  for one 6/49 jackpot ≈ 13 983 816 / 104 ≈ **134 460 години**; for 6/42 ≈ 5 245 786 / 104 ≈
  **50 440 години**. (Label as an average/illustration, assumes 104 draws a year.)
- 10 combinations in one draw → 10 / 13 983 816 ≈ 1 към 1 398 382 — still astronomically small.
- Jackpot size does not change these odds. It only changes how much you'd win and how likely you'd be
  to SHARE it.
- Zodiac / Джокер / Рожден ден jackpot odds → owned by sibling articles; skip.

### G. Responsible gambling angle (mandatory section)
- „Гоненето" на голям джакпот не подобрява шансовете; buying more combinations because the jackpot is
  big raises cost linearly while odds stay tiny. Gambler's fallacy: „отдавна не е падал, значи скоро
  ще падне" is false — draws are independent.
- Treat a ticket as entertainment with a fixed budget; НАП RG framework from
  `source-packs/nap-gambling-law-and-rg-2026-10-10.md` (budget ≤5% of net income guidance, register
  under чл. 10г / self-exclusion), link /otgovorna-igra/. 18+.
- Be wary of „гарантирани системи" / paid tips promising the jackpot (one sentence; no deep dive).
