Facts: landing enough scatters again during an active free-spins round extends it with extra spins (retrigger); real example: Sweet Bonanza awards +5 spins for 3+ scatters during its bonus round; not every scatter-based game offers a retrigger — some cap the round hard.

## H2 5 — Wild без scatter: примерът Starburst (~80-100 words)
Facts: Starburst (NetEnt) has no scatter symbol at all and no free-spins round; its wild expands over reels 2-4 and locks for a re-spin instead.
Direction: short contrast section proving wild and scatter are independent mechanics, not two names for the same idea. Plain descriptive heading (not a two-part tease). Keep "wild" in plain text only, no link (wild-hub sibling article not live).

## H2 6 — Купуване на бонус: пряк път, не по-добра математика (~110-130 words)
Facts: feature-buy/"купуване на бонус" is an opt-in paid shortcut straight to the bonus round; studios price it against the odds/average value of the bonus a player would otherwise wait for, so it is built to be roughly math-neutral, not a discount on the house edge or a way to beat RTP; it changes only WHEN the bonus happens, not the underlying odds.
