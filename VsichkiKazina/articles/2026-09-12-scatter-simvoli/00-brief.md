## VERIFIED PUBLIC-KNOWLEDGE FACTS (via WebSearch, for illustrative/example use only)
- Sweet Bonanza (Pragmatic Play): landing 4-6 scatter symbols (the round lollipop/candy scatter) anywhere on the grid triggers the bonus round of 10 free spins. Landing 3+ scatters again during the free-spins round retriggers +5 additional spins. Sweet Bonanza also pays via a "scatter pays"/cluster mechanic (8+ matching symbols anywhere pay, no paylines) combined with a tumble (cascade) feature. Source: pragmaticplay.com game pages + aggregated slot-review summary via WebSearch 12.09.2026.
- Starburst (NetEnt): has NO scatter symbol and NO free-spins round at all. Its wild symbol (reels 2-4) expands to cover the reel and locks for a re-spin; a fresh wild during the re-spin extends it further. Used here purely to show that wild ≠ scatter and not every slot uses the scatter/free-spins system. Source: WebSearch 12.09.2026 aggregated slot-review summary.
- Feature-buy / "bonus buy": industry-standard practice is that studios price the buy to be roughly RTP-neutral (the buy cost is calibrated against the odds and average value of the bonus you'd otherwise wait for) rather than a way to get better odds; some certified buy-RTPs sit fractionally above or below the base RTP by design of the specific game, not because the player is beating the math. Source: WebSearch 12.09.2026 (iGamingWheel/Webopedia/BigWinBoard aggregate on feature-buy pricing methodology).
- General scatter/wild mechanic facts (scatter pays anywhere off any payline; wild substitutes for other symbols to complete line wins; 3+ scatters is the most common — not universal — bonus-trigger threshold) are standard, decades-old public slot-mechanic knowledge, not tied to one operator; treated as evergreen fact, not flagged [VERIFY].
