## SOURCES (card-game rules — reachable, non-operator)
- pagat.com/rummy/canasta.html (John McLeod, Classic Canasta) — fetched 24.10.2026. Verbatim figures:
  · Deck: two 52-card packs + four jokers = **108 cards**.
  · Players: four in fixed partnerships (partners opposite). Also 2-player and 3-player variants.
  · Deal: 4 players **11 cards** each; 2 players **15** each; 3 players **13** each (some play 11).
  · Two-player: draw **two** from stock, discard one; need **two canastas** to go out.
  · Wild cards: jokers and twos. Joker = **50** pts, 2 = **20** pts (ace also 20).
  · Card point values: Joker 50; A & 2 = 20; K,Q,J,10,9,8 = **10**; 7,6,5,4 = **5**; black three = 5
    (only when melded while going out).
  · Red threes: bonus cards, laid face up; **100 each** if the team has melded (**minus 100 each**
    if not); all four red threes = extra **400** (so **800** total), or **minus 800** if not melded.
  · Meld: at least **three** cards, at least **two** natural, at most **three** wild; no all-wild melds;
    a team cannot hold two separate melds of the same rank.
  · Canasta: a meld of **seven or more** of one rank. Natural/pure (no wild) = **500**; mixed/impure
    (with wild) = **300**.
  · Initial meld minimum by team cumulative score: negative = 15; 0–1495 = **50**; 1500–2995 = **90**;
    3000+ = **120**. (Red-three and canasta bonuses do NOT count toward this minimum.)
  · Turn: draw top of stock OR take the whole discard pile → optionally meld → discard one (ends turn).
  · Taking the pile: only if you can meld its top card at once using two natural cards from hand
    (or a matching team meld); never when the top card is a wild card or a black three.
  · Freezing: the pile is frozen for everyone if it holds a wild card or a red three starts it, and
    frozen for your team until your team has melded; a frozen pile is taken only with two natural
    matching cards.
  · Black threes: stop cards — discarding one stops the next player taking the pile (does not freeze it);
    melded only when going out, as a group of 3–4 with no wilds.
  · Going out: the team must already have at least one canasta; meld all but one card and discard the
    last, or meld everything at once. Concealed (going out "hidden" in one turn, whole hand incl. a
    canasta, no earlier meld) = extra **100**, for **200** total.
  · Scoring each hand = bonuses + melded card values − cards left in hand.
  · Game ends when a partnership reaches **5000**; the higher total wins.
- en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canasta — corroborates 108 cards (some decks add a 3rd joker → 110), 500/300
  canasta bonuses, 2p=15 / 3p=13 deal, target 5000 (classic) with modern American variants using a
  higher target. Used only to confirm variant spread (NOT as a primary figure source).
