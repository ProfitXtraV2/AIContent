## VERIFIED GAME-MATH FACTS (from game-education sources — NOT operator T&C)
- Rules: player picks numbers from a field of 1–80; the game draws 20 at random; a player number that matches a drawn number is a „улучване"/catch; the count of numbers you pick is your „spot"/брой (pick-1 … pick-10/15/20). [Wizard of Odds; Wikipedia]
- House edge / RTP RANGES (present as ranges only):
  - Онлайн кено: house edge from under 4% to over 35% (RTP roughly 65%–96%+ depending on paytable). [Wikipedia]
  - Наземно/live кено (US survey data): returns ~50%–80%, i.e. house edge ~20%–40%. [Wizard of Odds; Wikipedia]
  - Видео кено: returns ~84%–95% (house edge ~5%–16%), "about as bad as slot machines" per Wizard of Odds. [Wizard of Odds]
  - Comparison benchmark: most non-slot casino games run under ~5% house edge; slot RTP benchmark ~96%. Keno is typically far worse. [Wikipedia; general game-math]
- Odds (standard combinatorial facts, hypergeometric distribution):
  - Catch ALL 10 in a pick-10 game: ≈ 1 in 8,911,711 (~1 в 8,9 милиона). C(20,10)/C(80,10). [Techopedia; Wizard of Odds calculator]
  - Solid 20 (all 20 of a pick-20): ≈ 1 in 3,535,316,142,212,173,800 (~1 in 3,5 quintillion). [Wikipedia probability table]
  - Any single ball drawn: 1 in 80 per game / 20 of 80 = 25% of the board comes out each draw. [Wizard of Odds]
  - Illustrative small-catch scale (approx, education sources): catch 1 on a small pick ≈ 1 in 4; catch 2 ≈ 1 in 17; catch 3 ≈ 1 in 73 — [VERIFY: exact value depends on the pick size and paytable] — treat as approximate, do not state as a fixed operator figure.
- Key honest point: probabilities are FIXED by the maths (they do not change between casinos), but PAYOUTS differ by paytable, so the SAME game (e.g. pick-6) can return anywhere from ~60% to ~95% depending on the paytable. Choice of which numbers you pick makes no difference. [Wizard of Odds; Techopedia]
