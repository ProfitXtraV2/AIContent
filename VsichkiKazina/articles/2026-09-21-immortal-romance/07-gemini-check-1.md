### 1. Contrast/Negation Signposting ("Not just X, but Y")
LLMs frequently use artificial contrast to create smooth transitions between sentences or concepts. Instead of just stating a fact, the AI tells you what the fact *isn't* before telling you what it *is*.

*   **Flagged Passage 1:** *"Славата му идва не от темата с вампирската любовна история, а от бонуса Chamber of Spins..."* (Its fame comes not from the vampire love story theme, but from the bonus...)

For the first passage, advise the writer to simply state that the game is famous for its Chamber of Spins bonus, without needing to dismiss the vampire theme first. For the second passage, drop the "not just decor" setup and directly state: "Всяко от имената отключва отделно ниво..." (Each of the names unlocks a separate level...).

### 2. Cliché Metaphorical Framing
When introducing a primary feature, AI models default to a very narrow set of metaphors—most notably calling something the "heart," "core," or "backbone" of the subject.

*   **Flagged Passage:** *"Сърцето на играта е бонусът с безплатни игри..."* (The heart of the game is the free spins bonus...)
*   **Recommendation:** Strip the cliché metaphor. Advise the writer to use a more direct, player-centric opening for this section. For example, start directly with how the bonus is triggered or simply state that the main draw of the game is the bonus, avoiding the phrase "Сърцето на играта" (The heart of the game).
