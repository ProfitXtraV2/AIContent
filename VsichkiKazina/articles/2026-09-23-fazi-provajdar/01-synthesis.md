# 01 — Synthesis (Fazi / Fazi Interactive provider profile)

## Reader & intent
Bulgarian player who keeps seeing classic fruit-and-sevens slots that look like EGT's but are made by someone else, and searches "fazi" / "фази слотове" / "fazi interactive" to learn who makes them and whether they are worth playing. Commercial-informational: wants a plain profile, not a sales page. Brand value-add over an aggregator: honest RTP framing (wide multi-build spread, operator picks the version), the certification-≠-advantage point, the Gold/Diamond/Platinum jackpot reality check, and an explicit "this is not EGT/Amusnet" clarification.

## Core message
Fazi (online arm Fazi Interactive) is a Serbian B2B game maker from Niš with a deep land-based roulette/cabinet heritage (since 1991), online since 2013. Its fruit-slot look feels familiar and trustworthy because it echoes retail machines and other Balkan makers, but the familiarity changes none of the math: the games are certified only to their stated (house-favouring) rules, the Gold/Diamond/Platinum progressive is funded from players' stakes and does not lower the house edge, and the same title ships in multiple RTP builds the operator chooses. Check the real RTP in the info panel.

## What to include (traceable facts)
- Serbian origin / Niš (office also Belgrade); founded 1991 as land-based hardware maker (electronic roulette, casino systems, cabinets); 2,500+ roulette configs, ~85% Serbia share.
- Online arm Fazi Interactive launched 2013; HTML5 games.
- Product breadth: video slots, roulette, video poker, dice + land-based equipment; catalogue "над 150" (listings vary 150–260); 50+ markets.
- Certification vs operator licence distinction (ISO 9001:2015 + ISO 27001:2022; B2B supplier licences MGA/Romania/Greece/Croatia/Italy). [VERIFY] specific test-lab names.
- Style: classic fruit-machine layouts, low-to-medium volatility; some higher-volatility titles (Age of Rome).
- Own-site signature titles: Wild Hot 40, Golden Crown, Very Hot 5, Age of Rome, Book of Luxor Double; DB titles Fruits and Stars, Jolly Poker.
- Explicit integrity: Fazi's own "Hot"/"Crown" family is NOT EGT's Super Hot / Shining Crown — different company.
- Jackpot: Gold/Diamond/Platinum mystery progressive, random, bet-funded, no edge improvement.
- RTP: cluster ~95.4–96.96%; outliers (some builds ~93% and lower); multi-build caveat; worked ~96% example.
- Practical close: check the info panel; demo; limits; RG.
