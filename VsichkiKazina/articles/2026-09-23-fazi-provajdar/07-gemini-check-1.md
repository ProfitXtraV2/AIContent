### 5. Repetitive "Not X, but Y" Framing
**The Passages:**
* *"Тези пари идват от залозите на самите играчи, **не от казиното**..."*
* *"Това е средна стойност за милиони завъртания, **не обещание за твоята сесия**..."*
* *"...това е въпрос на бюджет и търпение, **не белег за по-добра или по-лоша игра**."*
**The Pattern:** **Negative-Positive Signposting.** To sound authoritative and clarifying, the AI repeatedly uses a structure where it tells the reader what something *isn't* before (or after) telling them what it *is*. Used once, it's effective; used three or four times, it creates a highly robotic, rhythmic staccato.
**The Recommendation:** Break up this repetitive syntax. Rewrite at least two of these to be purely affirmative. For example, instead of "comes from players, not the casino," just say "The jackpot is funded entirely by a percentage of player bets."
