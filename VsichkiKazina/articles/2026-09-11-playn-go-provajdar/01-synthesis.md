# 01 — Synthesis (Play'n GO provider profile)

## Reader & intent
Bulgarian player who keeps seeing Book of Dead / Reactoonz and searches "play'n go" to learn who makes these games and whether they are worth it. Commercial-informational: wants a plain profile, not a sales page. Brand value-add over an aggregator: the configurable-RTP reality check (one game, several builds, operator chooses), certification ≠ player advantage, and supplier ≠ operator.

## Core message
Play'n GO is an independent Swedish, slots-focused B2B game maker (est. 1997, Växjö) behind several titles in almost every BG casino. Its games are lab-tested and its mechanics recur (expanding "book" symbol, cluster grid, charge-up meter). The honest catch: many titles ship in several RTP configurations the operator selects (Book of Dead 96.21 down to 84.18), so check the real RTP in the info panel.

## What to include (traceable facts)
- Sweden/Växjö 1997; own slots from mid-2000s; independent; Malta + Gibraltar offices.
- B2B supplier vs operator distinction; GLI + BMM Testlabs; MGA/UKGC/Gibraltar supplier licences (no numbers).
- Slots-focused; 400+ titles; ~2-3/month.
- Recurring mechanics: expanding book-symbol free spins; cluster pays + cascades; charge-up meter.
- Top titles: Book of Dead, Reactoonz, Rich Wilde series, Moon Princess, Fire Joker, Legacy of Dead, Rise of Olympus.
- Configurable RTP builds with Book of Dead ladder (96.21/94.25/91.25/87.25/84.18) + Reactoonz (96.51/94.51/91.49).
- Practical close: check the info panel; demo; limits; RG.

## What to exclude
No operator bonuses/terms, no НАП licence №, no affiliate link. No founder names (Törnqvist confirmed but not needed; Dahl unverified). No eCOGRA/iTech claim (unverified). No hype about "best provider".
