## H2 — „Един и същ общ залог, сглобен по два начина"
Direction: the counter-intuitive bit — because it is a product, the same total is reachable multiple ways. Worked: €0.02×1 монета×20 линии = €0.40 vs €0.01×2 монети×20 линии = €0.40; per-line stake identical, payouts identical. Consequence: the coin value shown alone tells you nothing; read the total. This is where the infographic sits.
Facts: both equal €0.40; per-line €0.02 both; same payouts. Illustrative.
Shape: one explanatory paragraph + the infographic + its caption. No mirrored „не X, а Y" closer.

## H2 — „По-голям залог не значи по-добри шансове"
Direction: RNG makes the result; the three dials only scale money, not odds. On a standard fixed-RTP slot, splitting the stake differently does not move RTP; bigger stake just means bigger payouts and faster budget burn. Route the lines-vs-odds deep-dive to the existing „линии на печалба" guide instead of re-teaching it. Mention the variable-RTP exception once, as a check-the-paytable nuance, no number. Internal link to линии guide and to /slot-igri/visok-rtp/ or /kak-ocenyavame/.
Facts: RNG; RTP unchanged by stake split on fixed-RTP slots; variable-RTP titles = check paytable (no figure). Cross-link vk-0008; link paytable guide vk-0058 for where to read coin value.
Shape: one flowing paragraph; end on the practical fact (bigger stake = bigger swings), not a bow.
