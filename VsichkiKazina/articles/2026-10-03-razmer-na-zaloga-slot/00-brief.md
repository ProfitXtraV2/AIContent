## CORE MECHANIC FACTS (universal, verified against reachable educational/game sources)
1. The total stake per spin is composed of three controls multiplied together:
   **стойност на монета (coin value) × ниво на залог / монети на линия (bet level) × брой активни линии/начини = общ залог.**
   (freeslots99: "Total spin cost = Coin Value × Coins Per Line × Number of Active Paylines"; ivycasino: "Total wager = coin value x bet level x number of paylines".)
2. Worked examples from sources (to be re-expressed in BG with € and labelled illustrative):
   - coin value £0.05 × bet level 3 × 25 lines = £3.75 per spin (ivycasino).
   - coin value £0.05 × bet level 2 × 20 lines = £2.00 (ivycasino).
   - Same total built two ways: $0.02 × 1 coin × 20 lines = $0.40 AND $0.01 × 2 coins × 20 lines = $0.40 — identical per-line stake, identical payouts (freeslots99).
3. The outcome of a spin is produced by the RNG; changing bet level or coin value does NOT change the odds of the result (ivycasino: "bet level does not affect the result of a spin… determined by the game's Random Number Generator (RNG)").
4. On a standard fixed-RTP slot, adjusting how you split the stake (coin value vs coins per line vs lines) does NOT change RTP — only the stake size, and therefore the size of the payouts, scales up or down (freeslots99: "Adjusting coins per line does not change RTP or odds"; ivycasino: higher stake → larger payouts on a win).
5. NUANCE / [VERIFY per game]: a small number of variable-RTP titles (e.g. NetEnt Mega Joker) publish an RTP *range* where the max coin level unlocks the higher RTP — this is a labelled exception that must be checked per game in its paytable, never assumed. Mark game-specific RTP figures [VERIFY]; cite none here.
6. Fixed vs selectable lines: some slots lock all lines active every spin; others let the player activate fewer. Activating fewer lines lowers the total bet but also removes win-check paths. At the same total cost, spreading across more lines gives more win-check paths (higher hit frequency) than fewer lines at a higher per-line stake (freeslots99).
7. The number that actually drains the budget is the ОБЩ ЗАЛОГ (total bet), not the coin value shown in the corner. Session length ≈ budget ÷ (общ залог × spins per minute) — tie to бюджет/лимити.
