However, there are a few minor stylistic quirks that occasionally appear when an LLM is prompted to "sound conversational," "be authoritative," or "write from personal experience."

Here are the specific passages that trigger a slight AI-detection radar and how to polish them.

### 1. Abrupt Point-of-View Shift (Forced Personalization)
* **The Pattern:** LLMs instructed to "sound human" or "add personal experience" will often abruptly inject first-person pronouns into a text that otherwise uses a second-person ("you") or third-person perspective.
* **The Quote:** *"Затова числото, което **гледам**, преди да завъртя, е общият залог, и спрямо него **си задавам** лимит на загубата предварително."*
* **The Recommendation:** The entire article speaks directly to the reader in the second person (*"Отваряте", "плащате", "Вдигнете ли"*). Suddenly switching to "I" (*гледам, си задавам*) in the final section feels bolted on. Align the POV. Change this sentence to address the reader directly (e.g., "Therefore, the number to look at..." or "This is why you should look at..."), keeping it consistent with the rest of the guide.

### 2. The "Hidden Secret" Cliché (Manufactured Drama)
* **The Pattern:** AI loves to frame standard information as a "secret" or something being hidden from the user to manufacture engagement or sound like an industry insider.
* **The Quote:** *"Има и обратна страна, **за която банерът мълчи**: средната очаквана загуба също расте с общия залог..."*
* **The Recommendation:** The phrase "за която банерът мълчи" (that the banner is silent about) is slightly melodramatic for a technical explanation of RTP and bet sizing. Remove this dramatic flourish. Simply state the fact directly: "Има и обратна страна: средната очаквана загуба също расте..." It sounds more professional and less like clickbait.

### 3. The Authoritative/Preachy Flourish
* **The Pattern:** AI frequently ends paragraphs with absolute, definitive clauses to create a sense of finality. It often comes across as slightly preachy or overly authoritative.
* **The Quote:** *"Трите контрола съществуват, за да наместите тази цена на ниво, което можете да загубите спокойно, **нищо повече**."*
* **The Recommendation:** The phrase "нищо повече" (nothing more) is unnecessary and gives the sentence a slightly lecturing tone. The sentence is actually a very strong, responsible statement on its own. Delete "нищо повече" and let the sentence end naturally at "спокойно". *(Note: Do not alter the 18+ or responsible gambling sentences immediately following this).*
