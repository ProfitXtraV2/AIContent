## FACTS (researched — see 06-verification.md)
WHEEL COMPOSITION (54 segments, Wizard of Odds): число 1 = 21; число 2 = 13; число 5 = 7; число 10 = 4;
Coin Flip = 4; Cash Hunt = 2; Pachinko = 2; Crazy Time = 1. Sum = 54. Numbers = 45 (~83%), bonus = 9 (~17%).
Bonus roughly once every 6 spins. Numbers pay X:1 (5 pays 5:1).
TOP SLOT: at round start, two reels above the wheel spin; left lands on one of the 8 bet spots, right on a multiplier
(up to ~50x [VERIFY]); if they align on a spot the player has, the multiplier applies to that spot for the round.
FOUR BONUS GAMES: Coin Flip (blue/red coin), Pachinko (puck down pegs, Double doubles all), Cash Hunt (108 hidden
multipliers, pick+shoot), Crazy Time (virtual wheel, 3 flappers, Double/Triple).
