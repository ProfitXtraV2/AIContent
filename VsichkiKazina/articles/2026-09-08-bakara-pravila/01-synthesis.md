- Card values A=1, 2–9 pip, 10/J/Q/K = 0; hand value = last digit of the sum. ✓✓
- Natural = 8 or 9 on the first two cards, ends the hand. ✓✓
- 5% commission on winning Banker bets (traditional table). ✓✓✓
- House edge: Banker ~1.06%, Player ~1.24%, Tie ~14.36% at 8:1. ✓✓ (Wizard, Casino.com)
- Tie is the worst bet; systems don't change a fixed-odds game. ✓✓
