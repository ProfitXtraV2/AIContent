## HONEST ANGLE (brand doctrine)
High volatility = long dry spells; the 10,000× max and the ×500 multipliers are ceilings, not expectations. Configurable RTP → operator picks the build → check the info panel (94.47% is meaningfully worse than 96.52% for the same game). Bonus buy (100×/500×) does NOT change the long-run house edge; you pay upfront for faster access to variance, not better odds. Cluster pays + persistent multipliers make the free-spins round the real engine, but it must actually trigger.
