### **Flagged Passages and AI Patterns**

**1. Pattern: Formulaic Signposting (The "Roadmap" Intro)**
> *"Тук ще намерите кой е зад студиото, как работят типичните му механики и какво показват числата на най-известните му слотове."*
**Why it flagged:** LLMs are programmed to be overly helpful and structured. They almost always end an introduction by explicitly telling the reader exactly what the next sections will cover, acting as a table of contents in sentence form.

**2. Pattern: Artificial Forward-Referencing**
> *"...което е важно за друг разговор по-надолу."*
**Why it flagged:** AI models often use clunky meta-commentary to build cohesion between paragraphs. Instead of just letting the information flow, they point to the structure of the article itself to prove they are connecting ideas.

**3. Pattern: Rigid Enumeration**
> *"Почеркът на Novomatic се крепи на две прости идеи... Първата е „книгата“... Втората идея е още по-стара: простите плодове."*
**Why it flagged:** While not exclusively an AI trait, LLMs love to organize concepts into highly rigid, numbered lists ("First is X, Second is Y") rather than weaving them together organically. It makes the text feel slightly robotic and overly symmetrical.

**4. Pattern: The "Neat Bow" Summary Conclusion**
> *"Novomatic е доставчик за играчи, които ценят познатата класика от залата повече от гонитбата на модерни рекорди. Числата му са трезви, механиката е ясна и точно затова си заслужава да проверявате коя RTP версия работи на вашето казино."*
**Why it flagged:** LLMs struggle to just *end* an article. They almost always generate a concluding paragraph that summarizes the main points already discussed in the body (classic hall feel, sober numbers, clear mechanics, RTP versions). It feels like a high-school essay wrap-up.

### **Concrete Recommendations**

*   **Fix the Intro:** Delete the roadmap sentence entirely (*"Тук ще намерите кой е зад студиото..."*). Your H2s already do the job of guiding the reader. Ending the introduction on the phrase *"който първо напълни залите с машини и чак след това излезе онлайн"* is a much stronger, punchier, and more human hook.
*   **Remove the Meta-Commentary:** In the Greentube section, delete *"което е важно за друг разговор по-надолу."* Just state the fact that Greentube holds the licenses and does the testing. When you get to the licensing section later, the reader will naturally make the connection without being spoon-fed.
*   **Soften the Enumeration:** In the mechanics section, remove the "First is / Second is" framing. You can simply introduce the "Book" mechanic, and then transition to the fruit slots by saying something like: *"Освен разширяващите се символи, студиото разчита и на още по-стара концепция: простите плодове."* This creates a narrative flow rather than a bulleted list in paragraph form.
*   **Cut the Summary Conclusion:** Delete the first two sentences of the final paragraph (the summary). Start that final paragraph directly with the actionable takeaway for the user: *"За българския играч решаващото остава едно: лицензът на казиното пред НАП..."* This avoids repeating yourself and ends the article on a sharp, authoritative note.
