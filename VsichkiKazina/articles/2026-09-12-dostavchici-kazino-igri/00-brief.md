## NOTES
Angle: education-first concept hub answering "who actually makes the games,
and does it matter". Core teaching points, honest and non-promotional:
1. Provider (studio) != operator: the studio builds the game math (RTP,
   волатилност, RNG); the casino licenses and hosts it. Readers conflate the
   two constantly.
2. Why it matters: the provider sets the game's math; the SAME title can ship
   in several certified RTP builds and the operator chooses which one to run
   - so certification is a fairness baseline, not a promise of a better game
   or better odds at any specific operator.
3. RNG + independent certification (GLI, eCOGRA, iTech Labs, BMM): what
   certification actually verifies (declared math matches real math) and
   what it does NOT verify (that the math favours the player, or that one
   certified provider is "better" than another).
4. How to spot a reputable studio: published RTP info per title, licensed/
   certified engine, transparent info panel in the game itself.
5. Brief factual tour of major studios seen in Bulgaria: Pragmatic Play,
   Amusnet/EGT (both link to their live vsichkikazina.bg profiles),
   NetEnt, Play'n GO, Novomatic (plain text, no link - no live profile).
6. Close: same studio, different RTP builds at different casinos - check the
   RTP shown in the info panel at the casino you actually play at, not the
   provider's marketing average.
