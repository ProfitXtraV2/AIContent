### 1. Pattern: Repetitive Authoritative Framing (The "Exactly X" Crutch)
**The Passages:**
* "...хазартните закони минават през **точно един** регулатор..."
* "Играчите обикновено прескачат **точно две** неща..."

**The Analysis:**
When LLMs are prompted to write in a "direct," "punchy," or "expert" tone, they often overcompensate by using absolute quantifiers to sound authoritative. Using the "точно [число]" (exactly [number]) construct twice in the same introductory paragraph creates a rhythmic repetition that feels slightly formulaic and engineered, rather than organically written.

**The Recommendation:**
Break the repetition in the opening paragraph. Keep one instance if you like the punchiness, but soften the other. For example, you could advise the writer to change the second instance to something more conversational, like focusing on *what* players skip rather than the exact number of things they skip.

### 2. Pattern: The Symmetrical "Wrap-Up" Summary
**The Passage:**
*"Лицензът и данъкът не се решават по един и същ начин - едното е механична проверка в публичен регистър, другото е лично задължение, което не изчезва просто защото сайтът е лицензиран. Провери първото при регистрацията, а второто изясни, преди да поискаш каквото и да е теглене."*

**The Analysis:**
This is the biggest AI footprint in the text. LLMs fundamentally struggle to just *stop* writing; they are programmed to provide a neat, symmetrical conclusion that summarizes the preceding points. Notice the perfectly balanced clauses: "едното е [Point A], другото е [Point B]" followed by "Провери първото [Action A], а второто [Action B]". This kind of perfectly mirrored, didactic summary is a classic AI structural pattern.

**The Recommendation:**
Advise the writer to dismantle the symmetry. A human writer would likely just end on a strong final thought rather than summarizing the two subheadings. Suggest dropping the "едното е... другото е..." mirroring entirely. The final sentence could be adjusted to simply deliver the final warning about withdrawals without neatly tying it back to the registration step.
