# 01 — Synthesis: Book of Ra

**Reader intent:** wants to know what Book of Ra is, how it plays, and its RTP before playing. Informational/commercial.

**Thesis:** Book of Ra is Novomatic/Greentube's archetypal Egyptian "book" slot whose entire
appeal rests on one mechanic — the expanding special symbol in the free games — played at high
volatility and at an RTP that sits clearly below the modern ~96% norm (original 92.13%, Deluxe 95.10%).

**Facts (verbatim, verified):**
- Provider Novomatic; online arm Greentube. Layout 5×3.
- Original: 9 lines, RTP **92.13%**. Deluxe: 10 lines, RTP **95.10%**. Both high volatility.
- Book = scatter+wild; **3+ Books → 10 free games**, retriggerable.
- Before free games one symbol is chosen at random to EXPAND across a whole reel and pay on any position.
- 5 explorer symbols pay **5,000× the line bet**.

**Honest angle:** below-average RTP (especially the original 92.13%), high variance, single-
mechanic game. €1000 at 95.10% → €951 back / €49 house; at 92.13% → €921.30 / €78.70 house.
