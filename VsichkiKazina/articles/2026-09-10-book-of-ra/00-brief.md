## Verified public game facts (web-verified 2026-09-10)
- Provider: **Novomatic**, online arm **Greentube** (Novomatic's digital gaming division).
- Layout 5×3. **Original: 9 paylines, RTP 92.13%. Deluxe: 10 paylines, RTP 95.10%.** Both HIGH volatility.
- **Book** = scatter AND wild. **3+ Books → 10 free games**, retriggerable (indefinitely).
- **Expanding special symbol:** before the free games one standard symbol is chosen at random;
  during the free games it expands to cover the whole reel and pays on any position (not just a line).
- **5 explorer (adventurer) symbols pay 5,000× the LINE bet** (state as line bet, not total).
- No single certified total max-win figure → do NOT invent one; describe the expanding-symbol
  free-games potential qualitatively.
