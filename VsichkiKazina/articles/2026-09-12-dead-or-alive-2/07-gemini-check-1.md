### 1. The "Duality Setup" (Formulaic Hook)
**The Pattern:** LLMs love to end introductory paragraphs by setting up a neat, balanced duality to outline the rest of the article.
**The Quote:** *"Играчите я помнят заради две неща, които вървят ръка за ръка: дълги серии без нищо и рядък, но огромен таван на печалбата."*

For example, suggest combining the thought to simply say that players remember it for its long dry spells that occasionally lead to massive payouts, dropping the "two things" framing.

### 2. The "False Lull" Transition (Signposting)
**The Pattern:** AI frequently describes basic features and then uses a dramatic pivot to signal that the "real" interesting part is coming. It’s a very common synthetic transition.
**The Quote:** *"Дотук нищо нестандартно. Разликата идва от това колко пари реално стоят в основната игра..."*
**The Recommendation:** Delete the signpost ("Дотук нищо нестандартно. Разликата идва от това..."). Suggest connecting the two thoughts directly—move straight from the scatter payouts into the explanation of how the money is distributed between the base game and the bonus.

### 3. The "Hidden Trap" & "In Other Words" (Narrated Emotion & Summarization)
**The Pattern:** To make dry statistics sound engaging, AI often dramatizes them (calling them a "trap" or "secret") and then uses a summarization marker like "In other words" or "Translated" to explain the math.
**The Quote:** *"Числото обаче крие капан."* AND *"Преведено: докато не хванеш бонуса..."*
**The Recommendation:**
*   Soften the dramatic "крие капан" (hides a trap). Suggest replacing it with a more objective phrasing, such as stating that the number requires a deeper look or is unevenly distributed.
*   Remove the word "Преведено:" (Translated:). Suggest letting the explanation stand on its own as a natural continuation of the previous sentence rather than announcing a summary.

### 4. The "If X, then Y" Alternative Block (Helpful Assistant Trope)
**The Pattern:** AI is hardcoded to be helpful. When wrapping up a review, it almost always provides alternatives using a perfectly balanced, dual-conditional sentence structure ("If you like X, play Y; but if you like Z, play W").

**The Recommendation:** Break this highly formulaic sentence apart. Suggest presenting these alternatives as separate, standalone thoughts rather than a perfectly mirrored "If/Then" compound sentence.
