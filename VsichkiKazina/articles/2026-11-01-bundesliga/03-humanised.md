# 03 — Humaniser pass (Бундеслига)

The author draft already applied the anti-AI discipline; this pass is a light read-through
against the BG AI-tell dictionary. Changes vs 02:

- Opening is a specific finding (голова-разлика тайбрек), not a „всички познават X, но…" pivot. Kept.
- No „не A, а B" antithesis used as a paragraph-ending mic-drop (the single most-flagged BG tell).
  The one „а не…" in the opening is a factual clause, not a closing aphorism; left as natural speech.
- No signposting lead-ins („Какво означава това:", „Нека сметнем:"). None present.
- No numbered-in-prose enumeration („Първата е… Втората е…"); the up/down section flows.
- Paragraph shapes vary: some single flowing analytical paragraphs (50+1), some short (нагоре/надолу),
  the trophy/attendance section is a mixed paragraph ending on a detail (not an aphorism).
- Headings mostly plain descriptive; „Билети за Европа" / „Нагоре и надолу" are light, not a repeated
  two-part tease across the whole piece.
- Rhythm varied: long sentences in the tiebreaker/50+1 sections, short ones in up/down.
- Asymmetric, opinionated close kept („Миналите титли… носят точни числа. Изходът от следващия
  мач не се знае…").
- „хазартът не е доходоносна стратегия" phrased as the house RG doctrine, not a sermon.

Em-dashes in body: 0. Banned connectives: 0. No narrated emotion, no false climax, no emoji checkboxes,
no Version A/B structure. Output carried to 04-seo unchanged in substance.
