## ANGLE (locked — universal slot-mechanics education, in scope)
Explain how stacked (наслагани) symbols work on slots. Cover:
- what a stacked symbol is: several IDENTICAL copies of one symbol pre-arranged as a block/stack on a single reel, so one column can show several (even all) positions with the same symbol at once.
- how the stack is fixed on the reel strip BEFORE the spin (falls already in this form; does not expand or draw itself in after the spin stops).
- effect on paylines/ways: a full stack on reel one gives every active line a matching starting symbol, so more lines pay at once when reels 2–3 repeat it; a stacked wild covers missing symbols across several lines in one hit.
- CENTRAL HONEST POINT: the effect is calibrated into the game's RTP (compensated via the paytable / hit-frequency / lower base values) → it MOVES variance and feel, NOT the long-run sum. Stacked wilds drive most of the „big win" moments.

## FACT INVENTORY (universal game-math — CORROBORATED, cite-ready)
1. Stacked symbol = identical icons landing in consecutive positions on the SAME reel, so one symbol covers two, three, or the full reel height instead of a single space; they arrive „pre-arranged on the reel strip" during a single spin. [vegasslotsonline; casinos.com]
2. On a five-row reel, up to five copies of the same icon can display after one spin; full stacks are common in modern video slots. [vegasslotsonline]
3. Payline effect: „a full stack on reel one gives you a matching starting symbol for every active line, which raises the number of paylines that pay when reels two through five deliver matches." On ways-to-win slots, stacked icons „multiply the number of paths through the grid." [vegasslotsonline]
4. Stacked wild: „A wild stack on a middle reel can complete several paylines at the same time by substituting for the icons the game was one short of." Stacking applies to ANY symbol; a stacked wild is just a wild that stacked. [vegasslotsonline; casinos.com]
5. DISTINCT FROM: expanding symbols „land as a single icon and then grow to cover more positions… after the spin stops" (stacked already arrive grouped); giant/colossal = ONE symbol blown up over a block of cells (stacking = several SEPARATE identical symbols). [casinos.com; covers.com]
6. RTP: „Stacked symbols are already priced into the game's advertised RTP by the developer. The feature changes how wins are distributed" but does not increase the long-run return. [vegasslotsonline]
