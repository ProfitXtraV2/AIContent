H1: Наслагани (stacked) символи в слотовете: как работят

SECTION 1 — Как пада стекът на барабана
Facts: stack fixed on the reel strip before the spin; up to 3 on a 3-row reel, more on taller screens; full stack on reel 1 = every active line starts matched; reels 2–3 repeat → several lines pay at once.
Terms: наслагани символи, стек, барабан, активна линия.
Direction: explain the mechanism and the reel-one effect. Cross-reference how lines read the stack via /blog/linii-na-pechalba/ (do NOT re-explain paylines in depth — anti-cannibalization). Route Buffalo-type high stacks to /kazino-igri/rotativki/ in prose, archetype only, no figure.
Shape: flowing analytical paragraph with embedded mechanism, longest-ish. Link: /blog/linii-na-pechalba/, /kazino-igri/rotativki/.

SECTION 2 — Къде се бърка с други механики
Facts: giant/colossal = ONE enlarged symbol over a block; expanding = single symbol that stretches AFTER the spin; stacked wild = just a wild that stacked (stacking applies to any symbol). Buffalo contrast: several separate bisons stacked, each counts on its own vs one enlarged icon.
Terms: гигантски/colossal, разширяващ се символ, наслаган wild.
Direction: the anti-cannibalization contrast section. Draw the three distinctions crisply; do NOT deep-dive any of them. End on the stacked-wild big-moment note, not an aphorism.
Shape: short crisp contrast build (distinct shape from S1).

SECTION 3 — Защо това не мести дългосрочната сметка
Facts: the feature is priced into RTP by the developer; more frequent/larger matches compensated via lower base payouts, rarer single symbols, tighter table; moves variance and feel, not the sum; stacked wilds drive most „big win" moments.
Terms: RTP, дисперсия, изплащателна таблица.
Direction: deliver the honest core directly, no signposting label. Link /slot-igri/visok-rtp/ for RTP-as-a-long-run-number. End on the „sum stays where the developer set it" fact.
Shape: analytical paragraph (distinct from S1/S2). Link: /slot-igri/visok-rtp/.

SECTION 4 — Пример с илюстративни числа
Facts: 5 reels, 3 rows, 10 lines (примерни); 3-high stack on reel 1 fills all rows → each of 10 lines starts matched; reels 2–3 repeat → several lines pay; stacked wild does it mid-screen; payout size comes from the paytable, already tuned to offset the higher hit frequency.
Terms: примерни числа, линии, изплащателна таблица.
Direction: start with the numbers, no „нека сметнем" label. Mark числата примерни. Worked example that doubles as reinforcement of the RTP-composition point.
