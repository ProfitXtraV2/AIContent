> **Quote:** *"Затова и изкушението е реално: вместо стотици завъртания в очакване, натискаш веднъж и си вътре."*
* **The Pattern:** AI models love to assign dramatic emotional weight to mechanical features. When introducing a feature, LLMs frequently use phrases like "The temptation is real," "The allure of X," or "It's easy to see why players are drawn to..." 
* **Recommendation:** Strip the narrated emotion. You don't need to tell the reader it's a temptation; just state the contrast. Suggestion: Start the sentence directly with the mechanical benefit (e.g., "Вместо стотици завъртания в очакване...").
