## ANGLE (honest, anti-hype — the spine)
"Купи бонус" lets you pay a fixed price (a multiple of your bet) to jump straight into the
bonus round instead of waiting for it to trigger naturally. It feels like a shortcut to the
"good part". The key honest point: studios price the buy to reflect the bonus's real
expected value, so the RTP of a bought bonus is roughly the SAME as playing normally
(sometimes a hair higher, sometimes a hair lower) — it is NOT a discount on the house edge.
What you actually buy is the MOMENT the bonus happens; what you pay in is far higher variance
per click (you stake ~100× your spin every time), so a cold run empties a budget fast. Not a
strategy, a convenience with a price tag.

## HOW IT WORKS (section spine — vary shapes)
1. WHAT IT IS — a separate „купи бонус"/„buy free spins" button beside spin; costs a ×bet
   multiple; drops you into the free-spins / feature round immediately.
2. THE PRICE — commonly around 100× the bet for a free-spins buy (varies widely by game and
   feature). Sweet Bonanza's buy = 100× total bet, guaranteeing 4+ scatters on the trigger.
3. RTP OF THE BUY — priced near the value of the natural bonus, so it is roughly RTP-neutral;
   e.g. Sweet Bonanza 1000 base RTP 94.51% vs BUY 94.53% (near-identical). The buy does not
   beat the game. [VERIFIED figures below]
4. VARIANCE / BANKROLL — €X buy at 100× a €0.50 bet = €50 per attempt (примерни); a losing
   sequence of buys burns a fixed budget much faster than base play. Real cost is variance.
5. WHERE IT IS ALLOWED — some markets restrict or ban the feature; the UK, for example,
   prohibits the buy on UK-licensed sites (regulators judged it accelerates spend and masks
   the higher per-session loss). BG availability depends on the operator and game build
   → [VERIFY: наличност на „купи бонус" при лицензирани в България оператори].
6. TRY BEFORE YOU PAY — the same feature can be sampled free in demo мод before spending.
