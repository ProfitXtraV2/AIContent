H1: Купуване на бонус (bonus buy): как работи и струва ли си

SECTION 1 — [opening, no heading] Какво е и как изглежда
Facts: separate „купи бонус"/„buy free spins"/„feature buy" button beside spin; pay ×bet multiple to enter the bonus round immediately; appears mostly on newer high-volatility slots; the appeal is skipping dry spells.
Terms/entities: купи бонус, buy free spins, feature buy, безплатни завъртания, ротативка, скатери, волатилност.
Direction: Open on the mechanism as a plain finding (it's a button that sells you the bonus round for a fixed price). Establish that the waiting you skip was never free. No definitional "in this article" intro.
Shape: two flowing paragraphs, uneven (a longer lead, a shorter follow). ~150 words.

SECTION 2 — [opening cont.] Цената
Facts: ~100× bet common for a free-spins buy, varies widely; Sweet Bonanza buy = 100× total bet, guarantees 4+ scatters on the triggering spin; multiple buy tiers exist on some titles; price computed on total bet.
Terms/entities: 100× общ залог, цена на бонуса, гаранция за скатери, Sweet Bonanza.
Direction: State the price plainly with the Sweet Bonanza figure; note the range and that pricier tiers are more volatile. First internal link → Sweet Bonanza.
Shape: single paragraph + INFOGRAPHIC 1 (bonus-buy-kak-raboti.svg: залог → ~100× цена → директно в бонуса). ~90 words.

SECTION 3 — Откупеният бонус не бие играта (RTP)
Facts: studios price the buy to the bonus's expected value → RTP near-identical to base; Sweet Bonanza 1000: 94.51% base vs 94.53% buy; not a discount on house edge; RTP is a long-run statistic, not a session promise; €5.50/€100 illustrative edge either way.
Terms/entities: RTP, домашно предимство, очаквана стойност, near-neutral.
Direction: Deliver the honest core: the buy does not beat the game. Use the 94.51/94.53 pair. Make "not a house-edge discount" concrete in €. Do NOT use the „Звучи като X… но на практика Y" debunk template.
Shape: narrative build (4-6 sentences) + INFOGRAPHIC 2 (bonus-buy-rtp-variance.svg: RTP 94.51/94.53 near-neutral + higher variance). ~130 words.

SECTION 4 — Стоте залога наведнъж (variance / bankroll)
Facts: 100× a €0.50 bet = €50 per attempt (примерни); base €50 = ~100 spins at €0.50; cold run empties budget fast; chasing risk on a losing series; wagering interaction (buy locked during active bonus; winnings on bonus-funded balance carry превъртане).
Terms/entities: волатилност, банкрол, превъртане/разиграване, бонус баланс.
Direction: The real cost is variance. Worked figure at a real BG bet size. Second internal link → волатилност glossary; third → разиграване. End on where the budget actually goes.
Shape: worked-example paragraph + a short practical-catch paragraph. ~160 words.
