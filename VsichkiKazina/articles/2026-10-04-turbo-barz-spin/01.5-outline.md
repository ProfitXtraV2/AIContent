## H2 — Скоростта не пипа RTP, шансовете или резултата
Shape: single flowing analytical explanation, two paragraphs.
Facts: RNG decides before reels move; animation is display only; RTP = long-run stat over millions of spins, independent of speed; turbo neither more generous nor stingier.
Terms: RNG, RTP, ротативка, бърз спин.
Links: /kazino-igri/rotativki/ (slots context), /slot-igri/visok-rtp/ (how to read RTP).

## H2 — Турбо не е същото като автоматичното завъртане
Shape: short contrast / disambiguation, two tight paragraphs.
Direction: ANTI-CANNIBALIZATION. Autoplay = series of spins without manual press; turbo = speed of the single spin; often combined, different functions; combined they multiply pace.
Terms: автоматично завъртане, турбо спин, turbo режим.
Link: /blog/rechnik-kazino-termini/ (glossary).

## H2 — Пример с илюстративни числа
Shape: worked figure, no takeaway one-liner. Numbers explicitly примерни.
Direction: ~8-10 spins/min normal vs ~20-25 turbo (2-3x), €0,50 stake; over one hour the exposed amount doubles/triples while per-spin odds are unchanged. End on the fact, not an aphorism.
Terms: скорост на игра, бързо завъртане, залог, оборот, домашно предимство.
