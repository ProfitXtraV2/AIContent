## ANGLE
Evergreen technology/mechanics explainer. Patient-teacher editorial voice. Explains HOW a live
dealer round works end to end, NOT where to play. Cover, in this rough order:
- what казино на живо is: real dealer in a studio, HD video streamed in real time, player bets through
  an on-screen interface; distinct from RNG games (ротативки), where software picks the result;
- the technology: multiple camera angles (overhead / close-up on cards or wheel / wider shot with the
  dealer); the GCU (Game Control Unit) attached to each table, which encodes the video and game data and
  keeps the stream and the result in sync; OCR (optical character recognition) reads the cards/numbers
  automatically, no manual dealer input; studio direction/lighting; RFID cross-check on some tables;
- table types: рулетка, блекджек, бакара, покер, game shows;
- latency and why there is a betting timer (bets close before the dealer acts);
- fairness and oversight: a real physical wheel/deck instead of an RNG, but still under licensing
  oversight; sessions recorded, cards/wheels inspected & replaced on schedule, independent audits;
  what makes a round verifiable; the house edge is still built into the rules.
