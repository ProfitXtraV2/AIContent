## VERIFIED GAME FACTS (every number traces to a reachable source; copy verbatim)

### Common to all table-game (табла) variants
- Each player has **15 пула** (checkers); board has **24 позиции/полета** (points),
  grouped in 4 groups of 6; **2 зара** (dice). [belot.bg/tabla/rules, bkgm.com]
- **Бар** (bar) separates the two halves of the board.
- First move: each rolls one зар; higher roll starts and plays using both dice;
  ties re-roll. [belot.bg, bkgm.com Gul Bara]
- Each зар is a separate move; one пул may play both dice combined only if the
  intermediate position is open. A **чифт** (doubles, e.g. 2-2) is played as
  **four** moves of that number. Must use all legal moves; if only one of two can
  be played, play the larger. [belot.bg/tabla/rules]
- **Капия** = a position held by **2 or more** opposing пула; you cannot land there.
- Bearing off (**изваждане/изкарване**): only after ALL 15 пула are in your
  **домашно поле** (home board, positions 1-6). Bear off from the point matching a
  зар; if none there, move from a higher point; if none higher, remove from the
  highest occupied point. First to bear off all 15 wins. [belot.bg, bkgm.com]
- **Марс** = double game: if the loser has borne off NONE of their 15 пула, the
  win counts double (2 points instead of 1). [bkgm.com Tapa/Gul Bara scoring; BG term "марс"]

### 1) ОБИКНОВЕНА ТАБЛА (прав backgammon)
- Подредба (per player, from own home board): **2 пула на поле 24, 5 на поле 13,
  3 на поле 8, 5 на поле 6** (= 15). [belot.bg/tabla/rules, bkgm.com/rules]
- The two players move in OPPOSITE directions, each toward their own home (high→low).
- A single пул is a **блот**; an opponent landing on it HITS it → it goes to the
  **бар** and must re-enter on the opponent's home side before any other move. If the
  entry points are blocked (капии), the turn is forfeited. [belot.bg, bkgm.com]

### 2) ТАПА (BG, Plakoto family)
- Layout effectively the same board; the aim is NOT to hit but to **затиснеш/
  похлупиш** (pin/cap) a single opponent пул: you place your пул ON TOP of it and
  it is trapped — it cannot move while your пул sits on it. "Тапа" = тапа/капачка
  (bottle cap). [en.wikipedia Tapa/Plakoto]
- NO hitting / no бар in тапа. Two own пула, or a pinning пул, make an anchor the
  opponent cannot land on.
- Players move in opposite (contrary) directions. Bear off after all 15 home.
- Match context: тапа is usually the THIRD game of a табла match, after прав табла
  and гюлбара; winner of the previous game moves first. [en.wikipedia, bkgm.com]

### 3) ГЮЛБАРА (Gul Bara / Crazy Narde / Rosespring)
- Подредба: all **15 пула on ONE position** — the far corner — with the two players'
  stacks at diagonally opposite corners. [en.wikipedia Gul bara, bkgm.com]
- Both players move in the SAME direction (parallel, anticlockwise).
- NO hitting: a single пул controls its position; the opponent cannot land there.
- Чифт rule is stronger than in прав табла: a doublet is played, and each SUCCEEDING
  doublet up to 6-6 is also played (so later in the game 2-2 can cascade: four 2s,
  four 3s … four 6s); not in the first few rolls. Unplayable parts are lost.
  [bkgm.com Gul Bara] — frame as a variant quirk, не като твърдо правило за всяка маса.
- Win = 1 point; 2 points (марс) if opponent has borne off none.
