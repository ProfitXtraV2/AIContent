SECTION 1 — Дъската, пуловете и подредбата
Facts: 24 полета в 4 групи по 6; бар дели дъската; 15 пула; 2 зара. Обикновена табла подредба: 2 на 24, 5 на 13, 3 на 8, 5 на 6 (=15); противоположни посоки, високи→ниски полета (всеки към своето домашно поле).
Terms: табла подредба, обикновена табла, поле, пул, зар, бар.
Direction: Lay out the board and the starting setup for ordinary backgammon concretely; make clear the two players mirror each other and head opposite ways. End on the direction of travel, not on an aphorism.
Shape: two flowing paragraphs (setup para is the numeric one). Leave a natural spot for the infographic here (no markdown).

SECTION 2 — Как върви ходът и какво значи чифт
Facts: first move each rolls one зар, higher starts & plays both, ties re-roll; each зар a separate move; one пул both numbers only if intermediate point open; чифт = four moves; must play all legal moves, if only one of two, play the larger; капия = 2+ opposing пула, cannot land.
Terms: игра на табла, как се играе табла, чифт, капия.
Direction: Walk the turn mechanics; put the "must use all legal moves / play the larger" rule as the counter-intuitive bit; introduce капия as tactic, not just definition. Vary sentence length hard here.
Shape: three short paragraphs of uneven length (first-move / чифт+forced-move / капия).

SECTION 3 — Удряне, блот и бар
Facts: ordinary backgammon only; блот = single пул; hit → бар → re-enter on opponent's home side before any other move; blocked entry (капии) forfeits the turn.
Terms: удряне, блот, бар.
Direction: Flag up front this is ordinary-табла territory. Explain hit/бар/re-entry as a chain. Close on the practical risk of leaving a блот, without a mirrored "not X but Y" mic-drop.
Shape: single tight paragraph + one blunt follow-up sentence.

SECTION 4 — Прибиране вкъщи и изваждане на пуловете
Facts: bearing off (изваждане/изкарване) only after ALL 15 in домашно поле (1-6); bear off from point matching the зар; if none there, move from higher; if none higher, remove from highest occupied; first to bear off all 15 wins.
Terms: изваждане, домашно поле, прибиране.
Direction: Make the "all 15 home first" gate explicit, then the three-step bear-off logic as plain conditionals (do not start three consecutive sentences with the same verb). End on who wins the game.
Shape: two paragraphs.

SECTION 5 — Кой печели и какво е марс
Facts: first to bear off all 15 wins (normally 1 point); марс = double game (2 points) if loser has borne off none.
Terms: марс, печалба.
Direction: Short. Define the win and марс; give the match-stakes consequence qualitatively (no invented point totals). No example numbers needed; if any illustrative figure appears it must be labelled примерни.
Shape: short blunt statement + one 2-sentence paragraph.

SECTION 6 — Обикновена табла, тапа и гюлбара: три игри под едно име
Facts: обикновена табла = opposite directions, hitting, бар. Тапа = pin/cap (затискане/похлупване), пул on top traps it, "тапа"=капачка, no hitting/no бар, two own пула or a pinning пул = anchor, opposite directions, bear off after all home, usually THIRD game of a match after права табла & гюлбара, winner of previous game moves first. Гюлбара = all 15 on one position (far corner), stacks diagonally opposite, both same direction (parallel, anticlockwise), no hitting (single пул controls its point), чифт stronger: dublet + each succeeding dublet up to 6-6 cascades (not first rolls, unplayable parts lost) — frame as variant quirk, not a hard rule; win 1 point, марс 2.
Terms: обикновена табла, тапа, гюлбара, игри на табла, чифт.
Direction: The longest section. One short lead placing ordinary табла, then a тапа paragraph, then a гюлбара paragraph. Keep the three sub-descriptions structurally different from each other so they don't read as a parallel triad. Explicitly soften the 6-6 cascade as a variant особеност.
Shape: narrative build, 3 paragraphs of unequal length.

SECTION 7 — Какво всъщност се уговаря на масата

Terms: правила, уговорка.
Direction: The honest de-AI angle. Say plainly that a lot of "rules" are house conventions; use the белот sibling link as the analogy (every company has small house rules). End on the practical advice to agree the version before the first roll.
Shape: single flowing paragraph. Place the belot sibling link here.

Terms: табла на пари, законно, НАП, отговорна игра.
