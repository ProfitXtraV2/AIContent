## FACTS (researched — see 06-verification.md)
GENERIC (state plainly): 5x5 = 25 tiles; player chooses number of mines; reveal one tile at a time; each safe tile raises
the multiplier; hitting a mine loses the stake; cash out any time. Multiplier = inverse survival probability minus house
edge; more mines = faster growth + higher risk. Provably fair (server seed + client seed + SHA-256 hash) is common.
PROVIDER-SPECIFIC (cite Spribe / flag): RTP ≈97% (Spribe-cited, third-party corroborated, NOT read from spribe.co →
[VERIFY]); house edge ≈3%; mines range 1–24 (some versions cap lower); max win ≈ x10,000 (version-specific).
