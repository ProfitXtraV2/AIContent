# 01-SYNTHESIS — chargeback-kazino
Core thesis: separate two things readers conflate — (a) a legitimate card dispute over a *defective payment*, and (b) an attempt to claw back a *voluntary gambling loss*. Only (a) is a chargeback right.
Angle: honest consumer-protection teacher voice; the value is telling the reader the uncomfortable truth other sites skip (losses are not chargeback-eligible) and redirecting to the real lever: a licensed operator + the complaints/НАП route.
Must-have beats: definition (bank-to-bank, reason code, representment, 60–120d window); valid grounds (3 concrete cases); invalid grounds (voluntary loss, remorse, misunderstanding); misuse risk (contest, account closure, blacklist, frozen winnings); licensed vs unlicensed difference; what to do instead (support → жалби → regulator). Licence-first throughout.
Numbers inventory (the ONLY figures in the piece): 60–120 days (card-scheme window). No RTP/€/bonus math. No operator facts.
RG touch: "хазартът не е финансова стратегия; chargeback не е предпазна мрежа"; budget = entertainment money; limits before first deposit.
