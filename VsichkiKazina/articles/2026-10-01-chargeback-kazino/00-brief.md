## Key facts carried into the draft (all general card-scheme / consumer rules, 0 operator facts)
- Chargeback = issuer-initiated reversal via the card scheme; reason code; merchant/acquirer can contest ("representment").
- Window typically 60–120 days from the transaction date (scheme + reason-code dependent).
- VALID: unauthorized/stolen card, money debited but deposit never credited, double-charge.
- NOT VALID: voluntary deposit + loss, buyer's remorse, "didn't understand the terms".
- Misuse consequences: casino contests & usually wins, account closure, PSP blacklist, frozen winnings.
- Licence-first: НАП-licensed → regulator + /zhalbi/ path is the real protection; offshore → no regulator, chargeback rarely works + risky.
