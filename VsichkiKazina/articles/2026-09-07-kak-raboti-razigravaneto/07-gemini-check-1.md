### 1. The "Mic-Drop" Fatigue (Over-polished rhetorical conclusions)
An LLM instructed to be persuasive will often use short, staccato sentences to create artificial gravity at the end of a paragraph. When used once, it’s good writing; when used in almost every section, it’s an AI pattern.

*   **Flagged text:** *"Затова нископриносните и изключените игри не са дребен шрифт встрани от важното. Те са важното."*

*   **Flagged text:** *"Превъртането не е начин да „победиш“ бонуса. Такава игра няма."*
*   **Recommendation:** Soften the rhythm. You don't need to end every section with a dramatic absolute. Combine some of these staccato sentences into natural, flowing prose. For example, instead of "Те са важното", integrate the thought into the previous sentence (e.g., "...не са дребен шрифт, а основен фактор за изпълнението на бонуса"). 

### 2. Didactic Signposting & Narrated Ignorance
AI loves to tell the reader what they are missing or how they are being tricked, using highly formulaic transition phrases that frame the information as a hidden secret.

*   **Flagged text:** *"Оттук идва уловката, която мнозина пропускат..."* (The classic AI "herein lies the catch" pattern).
*   **Flagged text:** *"Отвъд множителя и базата стоят няколко клаузи, които хващат играчите неподготвени по-често от всичко друго."*
*   **Recommendation:** Remove the narrative framing about what "many miss" or what "catches players off guard." Trust the information to be valuable on its own. Transition directly into the facts. For example, change the second flagged phrase to simply introduce the additional clauses without the dramatic "unprepared players" framing.

### 3. Metaphorical Overreach (Forced conversational tone)
To sound less robotic, AI often reaches for metaphors that sound slightly off or overly dramatic for a technical/financial topic.

*   **Flagged text:** *"Превъртането почти винаги идва с часовник."*
