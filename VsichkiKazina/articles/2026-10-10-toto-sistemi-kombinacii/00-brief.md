## INTENT (one line)
A Bulgarian тото player wants to know how much a „системен фиш" (пълно комбиниране) costs for 7, 8,
9 … 12 numbers in 6/49 (and 6/42, 5/35), and whether a system really increases the chance —
and whether there is a „най-добра система".

## ANGLE
Worked math, honest conclusion. A „system" of N numbers = ALL C(N,6) six-number combinations from
those N numbers. Cost = combinations × price of one combination. Jackpot probability for the whole
system = C(N,6) / 13 983 816. The system buys more combinations at strictly proportional cost →
probability PER EURO and expected value per euro are UNCHANGED vs single combinations. „Най-добрата
система" does not exist mathematically. Expected-value + RG framing. NO number picks, NO „hot/cold
numbers", NO „winning systems", NO predictions.

### PRE-COMPUTED MATH (python3, verified; the Author must use THESE numbers verbatim)
C(49,6) = 49·48·47·46·45·44 / 720 = 13 983 816. C(42,6) = 5 245 786. C(35,5) = 324 632.
C(N,6) = N! / (6!·(N−6)!). E.g. C(8,6) = C(8,2) = 8·7/2 = 28.

6/49 at 0,90 €/combination:
| N | комбинации | цена | шанс за джакпот |
| 6 | 1 | 0,90 € | 1 към 13 983 816 |
| 7 | 7 | 6,30 € | 1 към 1 997 688 |
| 8 | 28 | 25,20 € | 1 към 499 422 |
| 9 | 84 | 75,60 € | 1 към 166 474 |
| 10 | 210 | 189,00 € | ≈ 1 към 66 590 |
| 11 | 462 | 415,80 € | 1 към 30 268 |
| 12 | 924 | 831,60 € | 1 към 15 134 |
(13 → 1716 комб., 1 544,40 €; 14 → 3003 комб., 2 702,70 € — only if mentioning larger systems.)

6/49 prize-group odds for ONE combination: 6 — 1 към 13 983 816; 5 — 258 комб. → ≈1 към 54 201;
4 — 13 545 → ≈1 към 1 032; 3 — 246 820 → ≈1 към 57; any ≥3 — 260 624 → ≈1 към 54.

„Multiple wins" inside a system (6/49): if your N numbers contain h of the 6 drawn:
- система 8, h = 6 → 1×„6", 12×„5", 15×„4"; h = 5 → 3×„5", 15×„4", 10×„3"; h = 4 → 6×„4", 16×„3".
- система 10, h = 6 → 1×6, 24×5, 90×4, 80×3.
- система 12, h = 6 → 1×6, 36×5, 225×4, 400×3.
  (Formula: C(h,j)·C(N−h,6−j).) This is why systems „печелят по много групи наведнъж" — but each
  of those combinations was paid for.

Per-euro equivalence (the key point):
- 1 комбинация = 0,90 € → шанс 1/13 983 816. Система 10 = 210 комб. = 189,00 € → шанс 210/13 983 816.
  Шанс на всеки похарчен евро = същият (210/189 = 1/0,90). Същото важи за всяка система.
- Система 10 всеки тираж: средно 13 983 816 / 210 ≈ 66 590 тиража до джакпот; при 2 тиража
  седмично (104 годишно) ≈ 640 години; общ разход ≈ 66 590 × 189 € ≈ 12 585 434 € — точно колкото
  да купите всички 13 983 816 комбинации наведнъж (13 983 816 × 0,90 € = 12 585 434,40 €).
- Expected value: every combination has the same EV; a system is just N·combos → EV per € identical;
  lottery EV per € is negative (prize fund < stakes) — state qualitatively, no % (prize-fund share
  unverified).
- Practical difference of a system → only real effect: concentration (variance) + convenience
  (one slip instead of many); a system of
  overlapping combinations vs. the same number of random different combinations has the SAME jackpot
  probability (each combination is distinct) — but lower-tier wins cluster (when you hit, you hit
  several groups; when you miss, you miss them all).
