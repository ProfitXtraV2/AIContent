SECTION 6 — Система или същия брой отделни фишове
Facts: two differences, neither a better chance. (a) Convenience: one slip instead of 28 or 210 hand-filled combinations, no risk of repeating a six. (b) Concentration/variance: the jackpot chance is identical for 210 system combinations and 210 different random sixes (each combination is distinct), but small wins cluster. When you hit, several groups come at once. When you miss, the whole slip loses. Spread combinations give more even small wins. Same mean, different distribution. Reduced systems (съкратени системи): generic maths only, they cover part of a full system's sixes, cost less, and the per-€ chance is still unchanged. Do NOT say БСТ offers them.
Terms: съкратени системи; дисперсия; пълно комбиниране тото.
