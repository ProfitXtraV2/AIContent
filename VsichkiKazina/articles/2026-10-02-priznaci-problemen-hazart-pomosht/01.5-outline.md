## Защо гоненето на загубите е капан — SHAPE: short worked logic paragraph with the one illustrative €200 example, blunt close
Direction: isolate chasing because its internal logic is convincing. €200 lost, the next big bet „could" return it. Blunt close: if you are betting to fix your finances, that is the moment to stop.
