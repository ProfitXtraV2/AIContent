## VERIFIED FRAMING FACTS (from reputable health/education sources — NOT operator data)
- Lie/Bet screen: two questions drawn from the DSM criteria because they best predict a gambling problem: (1) felt the need to bet more and more money; (2) had to lie to people important to you about your gambling. A „yes" to either is a positive screen warranting a closer look (NOT a diagnosis). [NCPG Screening Standards Manual]
- DSM-5 Gambling Disorder: 9 criteria, diagnosis = 4+ met within a 12-month period (mild 4–5 / moderate 6–7 / severe 8–9). Criteria include: tolerance (needing larger bets), withdrawal-like restlessness/irritability when cutting down, repeated failed attempts to stop, preoccupation, gambling to escape, chasing losses, lying to conceal, jeopardised relationships, relying on others for money. [Gateway Foundation / DSM-5-TR summary]
- Gamblers Anonymous „20 Questions": a longer self-screen on the same principle; general description only.
- Warning-sign categories (behavioural / emotional / financial): chasing losses, secrecy & lying, borrowing money, loss of control, mood changes/anxiety/sleep problems, using gambling to escape. [GamCare — Signs of Gambling Harm]
- Entertainment vs problem distinguished by impact/consequences, not frequency or stake size. [HelpGuide]
- Self-exclusion in BG: per-operator or via the national регистър на уязвимите лица (НАП), filed in writing by the affected person only, not reversible early by calling support (brand canon + /otgovorna-igra/).
- International bodies mentioned generally: GamCare, Анонимните комарджии (Gamblers Anonymous).
