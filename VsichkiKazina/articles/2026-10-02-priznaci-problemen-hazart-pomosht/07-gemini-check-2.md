### 1. The "Not X, but Y" Contrast Pattern (Rhetorical Balancing)
AI models love to sound profound by telling the reader what something *isn't* before telling them what it *is*. This creates a seesaw rhythm that becomes very noticeable when used repeatedly.

*   **Flagged Passage 1:** *"Въпросът, който има значение, не е колко често или колко голямо залагаш, а дали още държиш контрол..."* (The question that matters is not how often or how much you bet, but whether you still have control...)
*   **Flagged Passage 2:** *"Разпознаването на няколко от тези неща у себе си не е признание за слабост. То е първата полезна стъпка."* (Recognizing these things is not an admission of weakness. It is the first useful step.)
*   **Flagged Passage 3:** *"Нито един от тях не е изпит, който можеш да си „издържиш“ или „скъсаш“ сам у дома, и попълването му не е диагноза."* (None of them is an exam you can pass or fail at home, and filling it out is not a diagnosis.)
*   **Recommendation:** Flatten these contrasts into direct, authoritative statements. For Passage 1, remove the "not X" part and just state the core issue: *"Основният въпрос е дали още държиш контрол..."*. For Passage 2, drop the cliché about weakness and just state: *"Разпознаването на тези сигнали е първата полезна стъпка."*

### 2. Narrated Empathy & Therapeutic Platitudes
When prompted to write about sensitive topics like addiction, AI adopts a highly specific "therapist" persona. It tries to manufacture empathy through sweeping, reassuring generalizations, especially at the end of sections.

*   **Flagged Passage 1:** *"Ако си стигнал дотам да четеш признаците и да се разпознаваш в тях, най-трудната част вече е зад гърба ти."* (If you've gotten to the point of reading the signs... the hardest part is already behind you.)
*   **Flagged Passage 2:** *"Първата конкретна стъпка не струва нищо и не изисква да решиш нещо предварително."* (The first concrete step costs nothing and requires no prior decision.)
*   **Recommendation:** Cut the forced reassurance. Telling an addict that "the hardest part is behind you" just because they read an article is a classic AI hallucination of empathy (and psychologically inaccurate). Delete that sentence entirely and let the final paragraph focus purely on the practical next step: calling the "Solidarnost" hotline.
