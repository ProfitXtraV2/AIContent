## Search intent (one line)
The searcher is looking at / considering a newly-launched online casino and wants to know how to
judge whether it's safe and worth it. Deliver a reusable evaluation framework, not a ranked list.

## Gaps competitors leave (our differentiator)
1. Listing pages rank "new casinos"; we teach how to JUDGE one, which is what a careful reader
   actually needs at launch, when the crowd wisdom that normally protects them doesn't exist yet.
2. The withdrawal-track-record point: stated payout speeds are promises with zero history behind
   them at a new site → the honest move is a small self-test before committing.
3. Novelty framed correctly: "нов" is a marketing tag, not a score; the launch bonus is bait.
