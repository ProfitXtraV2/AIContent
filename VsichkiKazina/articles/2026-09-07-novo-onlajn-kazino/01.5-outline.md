H1: **Ново онлайн казино: как да оцените новооткрит оператор**

OPENING (no heading) — SHAPE: the framing move, flowing. Direction: a new casino raises the same
questions as any other, minus the ready answers (no payout history, terms may still shift, launch
hype fills the gap) → same checks, applied with MORE care because nobody made them before you.
Avoid „не A, а B". Terms: ново онлайн казино, новооткрит оператор.

§1 — "Има ли лиценз от НАП" — SHAPE: single firm paragraph, the non-negotiable first check.
Direction: licence doesn't change with the site's age; check the name in the public НАП register,
not the "лицензирано" label; no licence → stop, however good the bonus. Link: /zakonno-li-e/.

§2 — "Какво пише в общите условия" — SHAPE: worked € example delivered directly (NO command-verb
bridge). Direction: new operators launch aggressive bonuses to gather players fast; the banner is
bait, the terms say if it's real. "150% до 300 €": deposit €200 → base депозит+бонус €500 → 35×
= €17,500 turnover in 14 дни — a flashy number as a near-impossible task. New site = reading the
T&C matters double (no one else's experience to warn you).

§3 — "Кой стои зад игрите" — SHAPE: short analytical (providers/RTP). Direction: games come from
providers; the PROVIDER sets RTP and the maths, independently tested; a portfolio of only unknown
providers is a reason for more questions. Check whether RTP is published and providers are ones
you recognise from other licensed sites.

§4 — "Тегленето без история" — SHAPE: the core point + a concrete self-test. Direction: hardest to
verify at a new site; stated timings are promises with no history behind them; check methods,
timing, caps, KYC policy — then test small yourself (deposit €20, clear KYC, request a €20
withdrawal before committing more). Link: /depoziti-i-teglenia/.

§5 — "Кой стои зад сайта" — SHAPE: transparency checklist as prose (NOT a bullet list).
Direction: a licensed operator is a company with a name, address, accountability; a new site
hiding who runs it / where registered / how to complain says something; check support (live chat,
sensible answers, Bulgarian, reasonable time). Often visible in the first ten minutes.

§6 — "Първият месец като тест" — SHAPE: practical + the method pointer. Direction: treat the first
weeks as a trial, not a final — small deposits, one tested withdrawal, watch how it behaves before
moving your main budget or chasing the launch bonus. We run a new operator through six weighted
criteria (законност heaviest); the reader can borrow the same lens. Link: /kak-ocenyavame/.

CLOSING (no heading) — SHAPE: asymmetric verdict + mandatory RG. Direction: novelty is neither an
advantage nor a flaw; "нов" is a marketing tag, the launch bonus is bait; judge a new operator by
the same things (licence, terms, games, withdrawals, transparency) with more patience, because the
history that usually protects you isn't there yet. Verbatim 18+ line + /otgovorna-igra/; set
лимити before the first deposit.
