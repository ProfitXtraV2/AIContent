## FACTS (sourced — every figure traces to a source below)
- Deck: 24 cards — во всяка боя: Асо (A), Десятка (10), Поп (K), Дама (Q), Вале (J), Девятка (9).
  No cards below the nine. [pagat; belot.bg]
- Card point values: Асо 11, Десятка 10, Поп 4, Дама 3, Вале 2, Девятка 0. [pagat; belot.bg]
- Per-suit total = 11+10+4+3+2+0 = 30 points. Deck total = 30 × 4 = 120 points. [pagat; arithmetic]
- Last-trick bonus = 10 points (only when the hand is played to the end without anyone declaring 66). [pagat]
- Players: 2. Deal: 3 cards each, then 1 card turned face up to set the trump (коз), then 3 more
  each → 6 cards each; the rest form the talon (талон). [pagat; belot.bg]
- Trick-taking order within a suit, high→low: Асо, Десятка, Поп, Дама, Вале, Девятка
  (десятката бие попа, дамата и валето). Trump beats any non-trump. [pagat]
- Marriages / обявявания (Дама + Поп от една боя): 20 точки обикновено, 40 в козовата боя.
  Declared on lead, by playing one of the pair and showing the other. Not after the talon is
  closed/exhausted. [pagat; belot.bg]
- Trump-nine exchange (смяна на девятка коз): the holder of the trump 9 may swap it for the
  face-up trump card, on their turn to lead, after winning at least one trick, while cards
  remain in the talon. [pagat; belot.bg]
- Phase 1 (talon not exhausted): no obligation to follow suit; after each trick winner draws
  first, then loser, back to 6 cards. [pagat; belot.bg]
- Phase 2 (talon exhausted OR closed): must follow suit; second player must beat the lead if
  able, else play a lower card of the suit; if cannot follow, must trump if able. [pagat; belot.bg]
- Closing the talon / затваряне (капак): the player on lead may close the talon, turning the
  trump face down; switches to Phase 2; closer commits to reach 66 without further draws. [pagat; belot.bg]
- Declaring 66 (излизане): possible only immediately after winning a trick or an обявяване;
  if declarer has 66+ they win the hand; declaring without 66 loses 2 game points. [pagat; belot.bg]
- Game-point (печеливши точки) scoring for a won hand: opponent 33+ → 1; opponent under 33
  ("шнайдер") → 2; opponent 0 tricks ("шварц") → 3. [pagat; belot.bg]
- Match target: 11 game points (Bulgarian standard per belot.bg). Regional/app variant: some
  play to 7 (Austrian Schnapsen root) or a set number of hands — agreed before play. [belot.bg; pagat — mark as вариант]
