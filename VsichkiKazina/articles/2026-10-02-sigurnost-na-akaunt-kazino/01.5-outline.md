H1: Сигурност на акаунта в онлайн казино: паролата, 2FA и фишингът
(compelling, carries primary keyword + the three sub-topics; distinct from title tag)

OPENING DIRECTION: lead with WHY the account is a target (money + KYC documents + saved payment method). Frame the split: operator secures its side, the login is yours. State scope and the two one-sentence out-of-scope references (transaction security; data privacy). No rhetorical-question opener. Shape: two flowing paragraphs.

1. ## Какво пази казиното и какво оставате да пазите вие
   Facts: operator secures servers/storage/anomaly detection; licensed operator obliged (LINK /zakonno-li-e/). Player owns the identity-at-login. Introduce the layered model as a roadmap (4 layers) -> INFOGRAPHIC placement here (sigurnost-akaunt-sloeve.svg).
   Terms: лицензиран оператор, превземане на акаунт, слоеве.
   Shape: analytical split + single roadmap sentence listing the 4 layers (as prose, not numbered). Infographic + caption.

2. ## Паролата: дълга, уникална и скрита в мениджър
   Facts: reused password = top vector (credential stuffing); length > exotic chars; 12 chars min; unique-per-site; password manager generates/stores/autofills-on-correct-domain + one master password.
   Terms: силна парола, уникална парола, повторно използвана парола, мениджър на пароли.
   Shape: cause-first narrative (the leak->stuffing story), then two practical paragraphs. HOLDS the single „не A, а B" antithesis ("не е хитра атака, а повторно използвана парола").

3. ## Двуфакторна автентикация: приложение или SMS
   Facts: 2FA = second one-time code; app (TOTP, local, non-interceptable) > SMS (SIM swap); backup codes offline, not in the same email.
   Terms: двуфакторна автентикация, приложение за автентикация, SMS, SIM swap, резервни кодове.
   Shape: definition -> comparison -> short practical tail. No table (prose comparison).

4. ## Как изглежда фишингът при казино
   Facts: urgency bait (frozen/verify/bonus-expires), mirror/lookalike domain, fake login page harvests creds + live 2FA; defences (no password by email, don't use message links, bookmark/type address, check sender+link domain, verify by logging in directly).
   Terms: фишинг, огледален сайт, фалшива страница за вход, спешност, подател/линк.
   Shape: mechanism paragraph + anatomy paragraph + habits paragraph (flowing, not a bullet checklist).

5. ## Устройството и имейлът ви са част от акаунта
   Facts: recovery email = backstage key (secure with own password + 2FA); device hygiene (lock screen, updates, avoid shared/public machines, public Wi-Fi caution, log out).
   Terms: имейл, възстановяване на парола, заключен екран, публична Wi-Fi.
   Shape: two tight paragraphs; ends on a concrete detail, not an aphorism.

6. ## Ако акаунтът ви вече е компрометиран
   Facts: warning signs (unknown login alert, changed contact, drained balance, unknown withdrawal); steps (change password + email password, revoke sessions / „излез от всички устройства", enable 2FA, contact support -> temporary lock + withdrawals freeze + re-KYC; LINK /depoziti-i-teglenia/). RG TOUCH here: account security underpins лимити/самоизключване (LINK /otgovorna-igra/).
   Shape: signs paragraph + action paragraph (sequential but fluid, no numbered counting) + RG paragraph.

7. ## Струва ли си усилието (CLOSING)
   Direction: asymmetric opinionated verdict. If only two things: unique password in a manager + app-based 2FA. Most takeovers ride reused password + missing 2FA; phishing breaks on the same two habits. Everything else is a useful add-on, but not the thing holding the door. NOT a balanced both-sides bow.
