## RESEARCHED REFERENCE FACTS (security best-practice sources — NOT operator T&C)
- Authenticator app (TOTP) is safer than SMS 2FA: codes are generated locally on the device and are not transmitted, so they cannot be intercepted in transit; SMS is vulnerable to SIM swapping and to codes entered on a fake page. Save single-use backup codes offline. Pair 2FA with a strong, unique password from a manager. [Keeper Security]
- Phishing red flags: sender address that looks similar but has extra characters / subtle changes (0 vs o), urgency/panic tactics ("account will be closed"), requests to confirm password or details. A fake login page can look identical; hover/check the real link and domain; navigate via bookmarks, not links in messages/search ads/email. [Kaspersky; Microsoft]
- Account takeover (ATO): attackers exploit weak and REUSED passwords plus missing MFA (credential stuffing / brute force). Unique passwords limit blast radius of any single leak; MFA blocks login even with a correct password; a hardware key will not authenticate on a fake site. Go directly to the official site/app to check alerts rather than clicking email links. [IC3/FBI; Kaspersky]
