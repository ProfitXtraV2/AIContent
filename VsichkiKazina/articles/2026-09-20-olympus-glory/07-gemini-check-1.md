### 1. Pattern: Symmetrical / Antithetical Phrasing
LLMs are mathematically wired to balance their sentences. When presenting a concept with two sides, AI almost always uses a perfectly symmetrical "If X, then Y; if A, then B" rhythm. This creates a pendulum-like, slightly robotic cadence.

*   **Flagged Quote 1:** *"Отгоре е класическа гръцка митология, а отдолу работи висока волатилност..."*
*   **Flagged Quote 2:** *"Уцели ли се стойностен символ, серията може да върне добра сума; падне ли някой от дребните, ефектът е скромен."*
*   **Flagged Quote 3:** *"Позна ли, продължаваш нагоре; сгрешиш ли, кръгът отива цял."*

**Recommendation:** Break the symmetry. You don't always need to state both the positive and the negative outcomes in perfectly balanced clauses, as the reader can infer the opposite. 
*   For Quote 1, consider dropping the "top/bottom" metaphor and just stating the theme and mechanics directly.
*   For Quote 2, focus only on the impact of the high-value symbol, or merge the two thoughts into a single, asymmetrical sentence about how the symbol's value dictates the payout.
*   For Quote 3, state the rule of the gamble feature without the rhythmic "guess right / guess wrong" seesaw. 

### 2. Pattern: The Paragraph Wrap-Up (Summary Bow)
AI struggles to simply end a thought. It usually feels compelled to add a final sentence that summarizes the paragraph it just wrote, often using the formula: *"This [adjective] [noun] makes it [superlative/conclusion]."*

*   **Flagged Quote 1:** *"Тази двойна функция го прави най-важното нещо в играта: и попълва редовите печалби, и е единственият вход към бонуса."* (Summarizes the preceding sentences about Zeus being wild and scatter).
*   **Flagged Quote 2:** *"Тегленето е ново всеки път, затова два бонуса почти никога не изглеждат еднакво."* (Summarizes the preceding explanation of the random symbol draw).

**Recommendation:** Delete the summary bows or integrate their information earlier. 
*   For Quote 1, you already explained what the Wild and Scatter do in the previous sentences. You can simply end the paragraph after explaining the Scatter function. If you must keep the "most important thing" concept, move it to the very first sentence of the paragraph as a topic sentence, rather than a concluding summary.
*   For Quote 2, the fact that the draw is random already implies bonuses look different. You can safely cut the second half of the sentence ("затова два бонуса...") to make the paragraph end on a sharper, more confident note.

### 3. Pattern: Narrated Emotion / Hypothetical Didacticism
AI often tries to relate to the reader by painting a mini-narrative of how a feature "feels" or "looks" during a hypothetical session, usually framing it as a cautionary tale.

*   **Flagged Quote:** *"Няколко удвоявания подред изглеждат чудесно, докато едно грешно познаване не изтрие всичко натрупано."*

**Recommendation:** Remove the emotional framing ("изглеждат чудесно, докато..."). State the mechanical reality of the gamble feature directly—that consecutive wins compound, but a single loss forfeits the entire sequence.
