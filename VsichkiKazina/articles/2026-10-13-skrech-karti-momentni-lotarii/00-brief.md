## TOPIC / ANGLE
Educational explainer: what scratch cards (скреч карти) / instant lotteries (моментна
лотария) are, how they work, and what the real chance of winning is. The STRONG,
distinct hook is legal: under the Закон за хазарта, the моментна лотария is one of only
two game types (with томболата) that **cannot** be organised online in Bulgaria
(чл. 41, ал. 2 ЗХ). So a legitimate Bulgarian scratch card is a PHYSICAL ticket bought
at a counter; any site advertising „онлайн скреч карти" for real money is either (a) a
casino scratch-style slot running on an RNG under a casino licence (a different game,
чл. 41 ал. 1 — the medium does not change the game type), or (b) an unlicensed site —
which НАП blocks and where the player also breaks the law. Educational, no operator
promotion, no venue/retail promotion.

### Evergreen mechanics (general knowledge — NOT operator-specific; explain, don't invent BG numbers)
- A scratch/instant lottery has a FIXED prize structure decided when the series is printed:
  the total number of tickets and how many winners of each tier already exist in the batch.
- There is NO draw and NO skill — the result is pre-determined at print; scratching only
  reveals it. Buying „more strategically" cannot change odds.
- Overall odds of winning ANY prize are typically printed on the ticket/series rules
  (commonly roughly 1 in 3 to 1 in 4 for the lowest tier across international instant games —
  [VERIFY] exact BG odds per game from the official series rules; label as примерни if used).
- Expected value is always below the ticket price (that gap funds prizes + operator + the
  cause/budget); „near-miss" designs are psychological, not a signal.
- Any specific BG ticket price, top prize, or per-game odds → [VERIFY] from the official
  game rules (toto.bg is CAPTCHA/Radware-gated at runtime; not fabricated here).
