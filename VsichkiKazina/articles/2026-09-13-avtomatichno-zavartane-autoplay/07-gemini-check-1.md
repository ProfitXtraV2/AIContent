### Passages and Patterns Flagged

* **The Pattern:** LLMs are programmed to be highly relevant. When they introduce a specific case study or a regional fact (like the UKGC ban in a Bulgarian article), they often feel the need to explicitly justify *why* they are telling the reader this, using first-person meta-commentary. Human writers usually just state the fact and trust the reader to understand its relevance.

**2. Thesis Repetition (Echoing)**

* **The Pattern:** AI struggles with narrative memory over the course of an article. It often generates a strong hook in the intro, but then regurgitates the exact same concept (using slightly different vocabulary) in the body paragraphs because the core prompt instructions are still driving its output. 

**3. The "Tidy Bow" Antithesis Conclusion**
