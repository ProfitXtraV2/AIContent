- Provider: Nolimit City. Release: 11.01.2022 (direct successor to Tombstone).
- Grid: 2-3-3-3-1 (5 barabana), 108 default win ways (ways grow via xSplit wilds).
- RTP: 96.08% default (house 3.92%). Lower build: 94.08% (some operators; DX1) — operator picks, check the info panel.
- Volatility: Nolimit rates it "insane" / very high. Hit frequency 9.08%.

- Min bet: €0.10 (max varies by operator/currency — not stated as a hard figure).
- Core wild mechanics: xNudge wilds (nudge until fully visible, +1 to the wild multiplier per nudge step); xSplit wild (reel 5, counts as 2 symbols, splits/duplicates symbols to the left, raising the number of ways).
- xBet (enhanced bet): +10% stake guarantees at least one scatter.
- Free spins: Hang 'em High = 3 scatters -> 8 free spins, multiplier accumulates per wild; Boothill Freespins = 10 spins, cowboy character multipliers from 5x to 999x — the 300 000x ceiling lives here.
- Feature buy: from 70x (Hang 'em High) to 3000x (Boothill) the base bet. Availability varies by market/operator.

NOTE ON MECHANICS: verified sources (official + aboutslots + slotcatalog) consistently list xNudge + xSplit wilds. The game does NOT use xWays/xBomb here (one outlier source, slotark, confused it with another Nolimit title). Do not claim xWays.

## HONEST ANGLE (brand doctrine)
"Insane" volatility = long dry stretches (hit frequency under 1 in 10 spins) broken by rare large hits; the 300 000x is a statistical ceiling, not an expectation. Configurable RTP -> the operator picks 96.08% or 94.08% -> check the info panel. xBet and feature buy do NOT change the long-run house edge — you pay for faster access to variance, not better odds.
