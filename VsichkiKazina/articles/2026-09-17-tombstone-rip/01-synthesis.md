## Core message
Tombstone R.I.P. is a very-high-volatility Nolimit City slot on an unusual 2-3-3-3-1 grid (108 ways to start) built around xNudge and xSplit wilds. Default RTP 96.08%, but it ships in a lower 94.08% build the operator can choose. "Insane" volatility (hit frequency 9.08%) means long losing stretches; the 300 000x ceiling lives in Boothill Freespins and is a rare statistical event, not an expectation. Buying the feature or using xBet buys faster access to variance, not a better long-run edge.
