## Core message
Playtech is one of the oldest and largest B2B suppliers in online gambling: founded 1999 in Tartu, Estonia, by Teddy Sagi, now Isle-of-Man-based and listed on the London Stock Exchange (FTSE 250). It is infrastructure — it licenses casino, live casino, poker, bingo and sports software to operators, it does not take your bets. Its signature is the Age of the Gods progressive jackpot network (four shared tiers). The honest catches: the jackpot pool is funded from every player's turnover and a hit is astronomically unlikely; RTP ships in several certified builds the operator picks (so databases disagree); max-win figures are theoretical ceilings and vary by source; what matters legally for the BG player is the casino's НАП licence, not Playtech's B2B licence.
