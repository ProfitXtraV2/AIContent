## PROVIDER / COMPANY FACTS (web-verified — see SOURCES)
- Company: Playtech plc. Founded 1999 in Tartu, Estonia, by Teddy Sagi. Headquarters now Isle of Man (Douglas).
- Listing: floated on the London Stock Exchange in 2006 (AIM March 2006, later main market); constituent of the FTSE 250; ticker PTEC. Publicly traded, independent B2B supplier.
- Founder: Teddy Sagi reduced his stake from 2017 and sold his remaining Playtech shares by November 2018 — no longer a shareholder.
- Aristocrat bid: in October 2021 Aristocrat Leisure agreed terms to buy Playtech (~£2.7bn / ~$3.7bn); the deal DID NOT PROCEED. Playtech remained an independent listed company. CORRECTION: a headline framed it as "will be sold to Aristocrat for €3.2bn" — that 2021 offer fell through; do NOT state Playtech was sold.
- Product suite (B2B): online casino/slots (700+ titles per racingpost), live casino (studios incl. Latvia and the Philippines), poker (iPoker network), bingo (Virtue Fusion platform, acquired 2010), sports betting, plus Snaitech (Italian retail/online, majority stake 2018). Licenses software to major operators.
- Signature product: Age of the Gods — a progressive jackpot NETWORK, four tiers (Power, Extra Power, Super Power, Ultimate Power), shared pool across many linked games, triggered at random. Age of the Gods slot: RTP 95.02% (headline RTP already includes the jackpot contribution, ~0.99%), max win 10,000x, 5x3 grid, 20 paylines, released March 2016.
- RTP policy: Playtech ships multiple certified RTP builds per title; the operator chooses which to load — databases disagree as a result (e.g. Great Blue reported 94.3% on one page and 96.03% on another within the same house). Check the game's info panel.

## HONEST ANGLE (brand doctrine)
Playtech = breadth and infrastructure, not extreme max-win caps. Its identity is the Age of the Gods progressive jackpot network. The honest catches: the jackpot pool fills from every player's turnover, the headline RTP already skims the jackpot slice, a jackpot hit is astronomically unlikely; RTP ships in several builds so the operator picks the number (check the info panel); max-win figures vary by source and are theoretical ceilings; the studio's B2B licence is not the casino's licence — for the BG player what matters is the operator's НАП licence.
