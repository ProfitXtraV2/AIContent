## FACTS (all researched — see 06-verification.md)
- Single 52-card deck. Middle/"game" card revealed first (NOT a wild). Andar=inside, Bahar=outside.
- Alternate deal until a card matches the Middle card's RANK (any suit); that side wins. Avg ~13 cards to resolve.
- First-card side wins 51.5006%; other 48.4994%. Andar wins on odd count, Bahar on even (Andar usually dealt first).
- First-card assignment VARIES by casino (some colour-dependent, some always Andar). The ~51.5% edge follows whichever side is dealt first.
- Common payouts: first-card side 0.90:1 → edge 2.15%; other side 1:1 → edge 3.00%. Stingier: 0.85:1 → 4.72% / 0.95:1 → 5.43%. Vary by casino.
- Side bets (count-to-match, middle-card colour/suit/rank) higher & provider-specific edges (e.g. 1–5 cards 2.50:1 ~5%).
- RTP derived: 0.90:1 → ~97.85%; evens → ~97.00%.
