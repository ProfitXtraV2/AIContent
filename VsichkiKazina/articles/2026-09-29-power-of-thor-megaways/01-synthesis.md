# 01 — SYNTHESIS · Power of Thor Megaways (Pragmatic Play)

INTENT: informational / branded Megaways slot explainer. Reader wants how it plays, the RTP, the
volatility, and what the "no-cap multiplier" really means.

HONEST CORE (locked):
- The progressive multiplier lives ONLY in free spins (starts 1x, +1 per tumble, persists across the
  round). The base game has no multiplier build-up, so meaningful wins realistically require the
  bonus — which is rare.
- "No-cap multiplier" is theoretical: any win is hard-capped at 5000x; hitting it ends the round.
- RTP default 96,55% is a long-run average; Pragmatic ships lower builds (95,81%, 94,77%) and the
  operator picks which runs. The player rarely sees the active build.
- The buy feature (100x, RTP 96,97%) guarantees entry but does not improve the edge; it buys tempo,
  not value; blocked in some jurisdictions.
- Maximum volatility 5/5: long dry spells by design.

FACTS CONFIRMED (≥2 sources): Pragmatic Play; 2021; 6 reels + top reel; up to 117 649 ways (7^6, NOT
46 656); tumble/cascade; FS-only progressive multiplier (+1 per tumble, persists); FS trigger 4+ THOR
= 10 spins, +4 each, max 30; buy feature 100x (RTP 96,97%); max win 5000x hard cap (round ends);
Hammer symbol converts to wilds; volatility 5/5; RTP default 96,55% with lower builds 95,81%/94,77%.
