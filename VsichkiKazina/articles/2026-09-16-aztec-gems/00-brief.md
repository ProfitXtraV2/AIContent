## GAME FACTS (from provider site + international game databases ONLY — no BG operator T&C, no НАП)
- Game: **Aztec Gems** · Provider: **Pragmatic Play** · Released: 2018
- Grid: **3 reels × 3 rows (3×3)**, **5 фиксирани линии** (fixed paylines)
- Fourth reel: a separate **множителна ролка / Win Multiplier reel** to the right of the 3×3 grid
- Multiplier reel values: **1x, 2x, 3x, 5x, 10x, 15x** (provider's own site lists the full set incl. 5x)
- Mechanic: on every spin the fourth reel lands on ONE multiplier; any winning line on the 3×3 grid is multiplied by that value; 1x = no boost. Base-game only, resets every spin.
- **No free spins, no bonus rounds** (the multiplier reel is the only "feature")
- Wild: golden Aztec mask, substitutes for all symbols; symbols stacked on all reels
- RTP: **96.52%** (default/published build) → house edge **3.48%**
- Max win: **375x** the bet (top line payout × 15x multiplier)
- Volatility: **средна (medium)** — sits at the calmer/simpler end
- Worked €: €1000 оборот при 96.52% → средно ~€965.20 обратно, ~€34.80 за казиното; €1 залог × 375x = €375 максимум
