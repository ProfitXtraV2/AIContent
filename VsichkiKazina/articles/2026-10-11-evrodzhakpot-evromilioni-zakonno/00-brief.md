## SEARCH INTENT (one line)
A BG player has heard of the huge pan-European jackpots (Eurojackpot / EuroMillions) and
wants to know: can I legally play them from Bulgaria, how would I even do it, and is it safe —
honest legal answer, not a sales pitch for a reseller site.

## ANGLE / STRATEGY
- Honest legal explainer. The truthful answer is the strongest content: **there is no
  НАП-licensed domestic route to buy an official Eurojackpot or EuroMillions ticket in
  Bulgaria** — БСТ (toto.bg) does not participate in either game; it runs its own ТОТО games.
- Explain the two ways people try to play from BG and what each legally is:
  1. **Reseller / messenger / concierge sites** (buy a real ticket abroad on your behalf).
  2. **Betting-on-lottery-outcome sites** (you bet on which numbers come out; you never own a
     real ticket — a different product: „залагания върху случайни събития").
  Neither is offered to BG players by a НАП-licensed organiser in the general case → using
  them = playing with an operator the Bulgarian state does not licence or oversee.
- Risks: no legal protection / no НАП recourse, НАП site-blocking (ISP blocks), tax ambiguity
  on winnings from an unlicensed source, look-alike/scam sites impersonating the games.
- The legal, in-scope alternative: the state's own НАП-licensed games on toto.bg (ТОТО 6/49,
  5/35, 6/42) — real jackpots, legal, with recourse. Link, don't oversell.
- Anti-cannibalization: DISTINCT from the BG-lottery hub vk-0273 (what lotteries exist IN BG)
  and the toto cluster — this is specifically the FOREIGN pan-EU games + legality-from-BG intent.
  No existing/queued article targets „евро джакпот".

- Draws: **Tuesday 21:15 and Friday 21:00, Helsinki local time** (Tuesday draw added March 2022).

- Jackpot: starts at **€10 000 000**, caps at **€120 000 000** (cap raised from €90M in March 2022). [S1]

### BG legal framework (Закон за хазарта — public law; НАП supervises gambling since 8 Aug 2020)
- **чл. 3 ЗХ** — every gambling game is organised only with a licence from the executive
  director of НАП; the licence covers only the game explicitly named in it. (Established
  framework, same as sister article vk-0273.)
- **чл. 4, ал. 3 ЗХ** — a licence for lottery games may be issued only to the state (except
  томбола, бинго and their variants); **чл. 13а** — the state organises lotteries through ДП
  „Български спортен тотализатор" (БСТ). → Foreign lottery operators cannot be licensed for
  lotteries toward BG players.
- **чл. 41 ЗХ** — game types; „залагания върху случайни събития" (betting on random events,
  which covers betting on lottery-draw outcomes) is its own licensed game type.
- **НАП site-blocking procedure** [S3]: НАП keeps a public list of sites organising gambling
  without a valid ЗХ licence. The НАП executive director issues a decision per site; after it
  is published on nra.bg the site must stop within **3 days**; if it stays reachable, НАП asks
  the **Sofia District Court (Софийски районен съд)**, which orders Bulgarian ISPs to block the
  site and its subdomains. Decisions + court orders are published in the „Онлайн хазарт"
  section of nra.bg. [VERIFY: exact ЗХ article(s) for the blocking mechanism — likely чл. 86–87;
  not confirmed in source text]
- Tax: winnings from licensed BG gambling are treated under ЗДДФЛ differently from income from
  an unlicensed/foreign source — point to the tax guide, do not state a rate. [VERIFY: precise
  ЗДДФЛ treatment of a foreign-lottery win for a BG resident]

## HONEST CONCLUSION (the spine of the article)
Playing Eurojackpot/EuroMillions „from Bulgaria" means going through a site the Bulgarian state
neither licenses nor supervises — so there is no НАП recourse if something goes wrong, the site
may be on the НАП block list, and the win's legal/tax status is murky. The clean, legal route to
a big draw jackpot in BG is the state's own НАП-licensed ТОТО on toto.bg.
