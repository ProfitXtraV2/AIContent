## PROVIDER FACTS (from provider/international game DBs + Wikipedia/trade press — NOT operator T&C/НАП)
- Company: Microgaming, privately held, based on the Isle of Man; founded 1994.
- Historic claim: developed the first online casino software (1994) — framed as widely credited / "смята се за".
- Cash Splash (1998): first networked progressive jackpot slot.
- Mega Moolah (2006): four-tier progressive jackpot network (Mini/Minor/Major/Mega); base-game RTP 88.12% (low because turnover funds the pool); 5 reels, 25 lines.
- Mega Moolah Guinness World Record: Jon Heywood, 6 October 2015, £13,213,838.68 = €17,879,645.12 (largest jackpot payout in an online slot machine game, nominal, per Guinness World Records); later surpassed in 2019.
- 2022: sold Quickfire distribution business + online games portfolio + jackpot network to Games Global (founded 2021, private capital, CEO Walter Bugno ex-IGT). Announced 15.11.2021, closed May 2022 (Q2 2022).
- B2B licences on the studio (now Games Global): MGA, UKGC etc. Independent RNG/RTP testing.
