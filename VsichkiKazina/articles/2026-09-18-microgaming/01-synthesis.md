## SYNTHESIS REPORT

### Core message
Microgaming is the studio that started online casino gaming: it is widely credited with the first real-money online casino software in 1994 (Isle of Man). Its historic firsts (Cash Splash, 1998 = first networked progressive jackpot; Mega Moolah, 2006 = the record-setting progressive network) define its identity far more than any single modern theme. The crucial, easily-mis-stated corporate fact: in 2022 Microgaming transferred its distribution business (Quickfire) plus its online games portfolio and jackpot network to Games Global. So the games a Bulgarian player sees "by Microgaming" are today distributed and owned by Games Global; Microgaming the company kept its platform and sports-betting technology. This must be stated accurately and, where the boundary is fuzzy (what "Microgaming" now means on a game tile), hedged, not asserted with false precision.

### Entity union covered
Microgaming; Isle of Man; 1994 first online casino software; Cash Splash (1998, first networked progressive); Mega Moolah (2006, four-tier progressive Mini/Minor/Major/Mega, base RTP 88.12%); Mega Moolah Guinness World Record (Jon Heywood, 06.10.2015, £13,213,838.68 = €17,879,645.12); 243-ways engine; aggregation/distribution (Quickfire); Games Global (founded 2021, closed acquisition Q2/May 2022, CEO Walter Bugno); B2B licensing + independent testing; top slots Immortal Romance / Thunderstruck II / Break da Bank Again / Mega Moolah; RTP-version reality; studio B2B licence vs operator НАП licence; progressive pool funded by turnover.
