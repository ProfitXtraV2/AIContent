**Verdict: Shows AI patterns, 75% confidence.**

While this article is highly informative, factually dense, and well-structured, it exhibits several classic LLM writing patterns. The text relies heavily on conversational signposting, hypothetical framing ("Imagine..."), and a slightly moralizing, over-polished concluding thought. It reads like a high-quality AI output that has been lightly edited but still retains its robotic "tells."

Here is the breakdown of the specific patterns that triggered this assessment, along with actionable recommendations to humanize the text.

### 1. Conversational Filler / Narrated Emotion
**The Flagged Text:** *"Тук е честната част."* (Here is the honest part.)
**The Pattern:** AI models frequently use conversational filler to artificially inject "voice" or signal a shift in tone. Real writers rarely announce that they are about to be honest; they just state the facts. It comes across as patronizing.
**The Recommendation:** Delete this sentence entirely. Start the paragraph directly with the core point: *"Голям символ, който пълни половината екран, изглежда като по-щедра игра, но размерът му е просто визуален..."*

### 2. Signposting
**The Flagged Text:** *"Правилото е просто:"* (The rule is simple:)
**The Pattern:** LLMs love to announce the complexity (or simplicity) of what they are about to explain before actually explaining it. It’s a classic structural crutch used to transition between paragraphs.
**The Recommendation:** Remove the signpost. Begin the explanation immediately: *"Гигантският символ не е отделна награда, а начин..."*

### 3. Hypothetical Framing ("The 'Imagine' Crutch")
**The Flagged Text:** *"Представете си блок 2x2, който пада в лявата част на барабаните."* (Imagine a 2x2 block that falls on the left side of the reels.)
**The Pattern:** When asked to provide an example, AI almost universally defaults to commanding the reader to "Imagine [Scenario]." It is one of the most recognizable AI footprints in modern copywriting.
**The Recommendation:** Convert the hypothetical command into a direct, concrete conditional statement. For example: *"Ако блок 2x2 падне в лявата част на барабаните, той покрива..."* (If a 2x2 block falls...)

### 4. Tautological / Clunky Phrasing (Translation Artifact)
**The Flagged Text:** *"Колкото по-ефектен е ефектът на екрана..."* (The more spectacular the effect on the screen...)
**The Pattern:** AI often struggles with word repetition when generating text in languages other than English, leading to clunky tautologies (ефектен / ефектът) that a native human editor would usually catch.
**The Recommendation:** Swap one of the words to remove the repetition. For example: *"Колкото по-зрелищно е събитието на екрана..."* or *"Колкото по-впечатляваща е анимацията..."*

### 5. The Over-Polished, Moralizing Conclusion
**The Flagged Text:** *"Игра, избрана защото символите ѝ са едри и ефектни, е избрана по усещане, не по число."* (A game chosen because its symbols are large and spectacular is chosen by feeling, not by number.)
**The Pattern:** AI models are programmed to wrap up articles with a neat, philosophical bow that summarizes the "lesson" of the text. This sentence feels overly poetic and slightly preachy for a technical explanation of a slot mechanic.
**The Recommendation:** Delete this specific sentence. The preceding sentence (*"Гигантските символи правят екрана по-зрелищен... но колко връща играта... е решено от RTP..."*) already perfectly summarizes the point without sounding like a proverb.
