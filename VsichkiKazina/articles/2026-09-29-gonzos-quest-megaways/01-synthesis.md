# 01 — SYNTHESIS · Gonzo's Quest Megaways (Red Tiger)

INTENT: informational / branded Megaways slot explainer. Reader wants how it plays, the RTP, and
what the advertised max win really means.

HONEST CORE (locked):
- Multipliers ARE the volatility engine: base game capped at 5x, the real potential lives in free
  spins (3x→6x→9x→15x) combined with high Megaways ways — and those long max-multiplier chains are
  uncommon.
- RTP 95,77% default is BELOW a round 96% and is a long-run average, not a session promise. Worse,
  operators can deploy lower-RTP configurations (down toward ~90%); the player rarely sees which
  build is active.
- Max win ~20 972x (21 000x) is a HARD CAP and astronomically rare, not an expectation.
- High volatility, medium hit rate: frequent small returns, rare big hits.

FACTS CONFIRMED (≥2 sources): Red Tiger build on NetEnt IP (both Evolution); Megaways engine from
BTG; 6 reels, 2–7 rows, up to 117 649 ways; Avalanche/tumbling; base ladder 1x→2x→3x→5x; free-spins
ladder 3x→6x→9x→15x; Free Falls trigger 3+ scatters (or 2 + wild), 3 spins per symbol / 9 to start;
Earthquake feature; max win 20 972x (~21 000x); release 2020; high volatility.

FLAGGED [VERIFY]: lower configurable RTP variant table (single-source); free-spins award count
nuance. OMITTED: exact bet range (operator/currency-dependent).
