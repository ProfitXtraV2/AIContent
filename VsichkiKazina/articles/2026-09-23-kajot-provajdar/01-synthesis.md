## SYNTHESIS REPORT

### Verified entity facts (from fetched pages)
- Kajot / Kajot Games: Czech-rooted maker of slot games; began in hardware (slot terminals, video lottery systems, gaming-hall machines), history traced by databases to 1996. [SlotCatalog]
- Online arm Kajot Games is registered in Malta; holds B2B licence MGA/B2B/397/2017 (Type 1); Gaming Labs certified; B2B model (supplies casinos, does not take player bets directly). [kajotgames.com]
- Additional certification/markets: Czech Republic and Spain noted; active in ~31 markets. [SlotCatalog]
- Catalogue: 113 titles (112 video slots + 1 roulette); 110+ commonly cited. [SlotCatalog]
- Profile: predominantly classic fruit / Joker slots, plus a few themed video slots and a dice-slot range. [SlotCatalog, kajotgames.com, AboutSlots]
- Flagship titles with verified specs:
  - Joker 27 Plus — 3x3 grid, 27 betways, RTP 95%, Joker wild, Double Win when all lines hit. [SlotCatalog]
  - Simply the Best 81 — 4 reels, 3 rows, 81 paylines, RTP 95%, 648x max win, classic fruit symbols (cherries, oranges, plums, bells, BAR). [SlotCatalog]
- Other recognisable titles: Multi Fruits 81, Joker 81, Neon Fruits, Vikings, Secrets of Egypt. [SlotCatalog, kajotgames.com]
