# 01 — SYNTHESIS · vk-0189 · Casino Hold'em

Sourced facts (each with source; [VERIFY] where sources disagree or a figure could not be pinned).

## RULES / SEQUENCE
- Player places Ante; player + dealer each get 2 hole cards; 3 community cards (flop) dealt face
  up immediately. — Wizard of Odds (WoO), casino-hold-em. [solid]
- After the flop: fold (lose Ante) OR make a Call bet = exactly 2× the Ante. Then the last 2
  community cards (turn + river) are dealt together (5 community total); best 5-card hand wins.
  No separate turn/river betting round. — WoO; Wikipedia. [solid]

## DEALER QUALIFICATION
- Dealer qualifies with a pair of fours or better. — WoO; Wikipedia. [solid]
- Not qualify → Call pushes (returned), Ante pays per the Ante paytable. — WoO. [solid]
- Qualify + player wins → Ante pays per paytable, Call pays 1:1. — WoO. [solid]
- Qualify + dealer wins → lose both Ante and Call. Tie → both push. — WoO. [solid]

## ANTE BONUS PAYTABLE (WoO Pay Table 3 — most common)
- Royal flush 100:1 · Straight flush 20:1 · Four of a kind 10:1 · Full house 3:1 · Flush 2:1 ·
  Straight or less 1:1. — WoO; Wikipedia (identical). [solid for this table; other tables exist → VERIFY per version]

## HOUSE EDGE
- Standard house edge on the Ante ≈ 2,16% (Pay Table 3). — WoO. [solid; version-dependent → VERIFY]
- "Zero house edge" variant exists (~0,35% edge but 10% commission on net session win). — WoO. [solid]
- Element-of-risk figure (~0,82%) NOT confirmed on WoO for this game → OMITTED from body (not stated).

## AA / AA+ SIDE BET
- Resolved only on the player's first 5 cards (2 hole + 3 flop), independent of dealer/outcome;
  pays on a pair of aces or better. — Wikipedia. [solid]
- Paytables vary by version; WoO Pay Table 1 house edge = 6,40%. Body states ≈6% + [VERIFY per table]. [solid direction]

## BASIC STRATEGY
- Optimal player raises (Calls) ~82% of hands; folds only the worst ~18%. — WoO. [solid]
- (The "~30% fold" figure sometimes quoted is NOT supported by WoO — not used.)

## DISTINCTION
- vs Ultimate Texas Hold'em: also 2 hole + 5 community, but variable-size single raise chosen from
  several stages + a separate Blind bet; dealer qualifies with a pair. — WoO/UTH. [solid on structure]
- vs Three Card Poker: 3 cards, no community, Play = 1× Ante, dealer qualifies Queen-high or better. — WoO. [solid]
