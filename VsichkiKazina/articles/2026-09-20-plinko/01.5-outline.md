H1: Plinko: как работи и какво значат нивата на риск

- **Intro** (flowing para): not a slot/roulette; ball down a pegboard into a multiplier slot; came from TV shows, rebuilt online by BGaming, Spribe, Stake; simple look, pure maths underneath. Set honest tone.
- **## Топчето, щифтовете и защо центърът печели най-често** — binomial/Pascal; 16 rows = 16 L/R decisions; centre paths far more numerous → small multipliers common, edge max rare BY DESIGN. (analytical single para, no takeaway line)
- **## Редовете: от 8 до 16** — BGaming 8–16; Spribe 12/14/16; more rows = more slots, longer path, higher edge max; fewer rows = narrower spread. (short, spec-in-prose)
- **## Нивата на риск сменят волатилността, не шанса** — BGaming Low/Normal/High; Spribe colours; redistributes multipliers, RTP unchanged; high → centre below 1x; low → compressed near 1x. Follow with a plain-language "risk = volatility choice" para. (two paras, different shape)
- **## RTP и домашно предимство** — BGaming 99%, Stake 99%, Spribe 97%; house edge 1%/3%; €1000 → ~€990 / ~€970; fixed per title, check info panel. INFOGRAPHIC beside. (worked figures, no punchy outro)
- **## Доказуемо честна игра (provably fair)** — server+client seed; verify round; proves fairness of the round, NOT removes house edge / not profitable.
- **## Какво значи това за банката ти** — high volatility + small budget = harsh; long play → low risk + fewer rows; the 1000x/555x tail is an extreme, not a plan.
