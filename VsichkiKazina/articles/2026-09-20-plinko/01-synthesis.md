# 01 — Synthesis (Plinko)

## Core proposition
Plinko is a provably-fair instant game (a game TYPE, not one title). The honest, differentiating angle for a BG player: the **risk-level button changes volatility, not RTP**, and the **shape of the pegboard** (binomial distribution) is why most balls land in the low-multiplier centre. House edge (1–3%) is always present; provably fair proves round integrity, not profitability.

## Verified fact spine (all from provider + game-DB, NOT operator T&C/НАП)
- Mechanics: ball drops a triangular pegboard, bounces L/R each row, lands in a multiplier slot. Binomial / Pascal's triangle → centre most probable (small mult), edges rarest (max mult).
- Providers: BGaming, Spribe; platform originals Stake, BC.Game. Plinko = type.
- Rows: BGaming/Stake 8–16 selectable; Spribe 12/14/16.
- Risk: BGaming Low/Normal/High; Spribe green/yellow/red. Redistributes multipliers, RTP unchanged. High → centre below 1x; low → compressed near 1x.
- RTP: BGaming 99%, Stake original 99%, Spribe 97%. House edge 1% / 3%. Fixed per title; check info panel for operator versions.
- Max multiplier: BGaming 1000x; Spribe 555x (16 rows, red).
- Provably fair: server+client seed, verify per round.
- Bet range: operator-dependent → not asserted.
