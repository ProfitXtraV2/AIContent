## GAME FACTS (public provider + game-DB data — NOT operator T&C / НАП)
- Plinko is a game TYPE / provably-fair "instant" (crash-adjacent) game, NOT a single branded title. Made by multiple studios: BGaming, Spribe, plus platform "originals" (Stake, BC.Game).
- Mechanics: a ball drops down a triangular pegboard; at each row it bounces left/right; lands in a bottom slot with a multiplier. Follows a binomial distribution / Pascal's triangle — centre slots most probable (small multipliers), edge slots rarest (highest multipliers).
- Risk levels: BGaming = Low / Normal / High; Spribe = green / yellow / red. Risk redistributes the multipliers, does NOT change the theoretical RTP (high risk = edge multipliers bigger, centre drops below 1x; low risk = all multipliers compressed near 1x). Risk = volatility choice.
- Rows: BGaming/Stake/BC.Game selectable 8–16; Spribe 12/14/16 only. More rows = more slots, longer path, higher max multiplier at edges.
- RTP: BGaming 99% (official page); Stake original 99%; Spribe 97% (house edge 1% / 3%). RTP fixed per branded title; risk/rows don't change it. Operator may run alternate versions → check info panel.
- Max multiplier: BGaming up to 1000x; Spribe up to 555x (16 rows, red). (Stake standard 1000x.)
- Provably fair: BGaming/Spribe/Stake use server+client seed verification per round. Proves round fairness; does NOT remove house edge.
- Bet range: operator/provider dependent → do NOT assert a specific min/max.
