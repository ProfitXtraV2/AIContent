## Key facts carried into the draft (all general; 0 operator facts, 0 fabricated figures)
- Licensed BG (НАП) operators work in EUR via regulated, traceable methods (card/bank/e-wallet) tied to AML + KYC; crypto is pseudonymous, outside the banking system → not part of the licensed flow.
- A casino offering crypto to a BG player is almost certainly outside the BG licence (offshore).
- Crypto transactions are irreversible: no chargeback; wrong address = lost funds; dishonest operator = no card scheme to dispute.

- Offshore (Curaçao etc.): no payout standard, not audited on player-protection tools; БГ regulator has no leverage; limits/self-exclusion/complaints vary or absent.
- Red flags: no visible НАП licence №, offshore seal, EUR-card hidden behind crypto, bigger bonus for crypto deposits.
