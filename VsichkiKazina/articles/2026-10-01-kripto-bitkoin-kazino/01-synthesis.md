# 01-SYNTHESIS — kripto-bitkoin-kazino
Thesis: the honest answer has two halves — at НАП-licensed operators crypto effectively isn't offered (EUR + regulated, AML/KYC-bound methods; crypto is pseudonymous/out-of-band), and where crypto IS offered the site is almost certainly offshore, bringing irreversibility, volatility and (worst) no regulator.
Angle: education, not promotion; never recommend a crypto operator. Payment method as a signal of regulatory status.
Beats: licensed = EUR/regulated; necessity-not-whim reasoning (AML/KYC vs pseudonymity); irreversibility/no-chargeback; volatility = second bet; no-regulator = the real cost; red flags; decision rule (licence-first). One natural RG touch (casino = paid entertainment, adding currency risk + no regulator makes the bet strictly worse).
Numbers inventory: NONE asserted (no price, RTP, %). Jurisdiction named (Кюрасао as example of offshore).
