### **Flagged Passages and AI Patterns**

**1. Meta-Discourse / Signposting**

**The Pattern:** LLMs have a strong tendency to announce the purpose of the text to the reader (e.g., "In this article, we will explore...", "The main focus here is..."). Humans usually just dive into the topic without narrating the fact that they are writing an article about it.

**2. Formulaic / Dramatic Transitions**
> *"Има и втора уловка, по-малко известна."*
> *"Тук е разликата, която пази парите ви."*
**The Pattern:** AI models struggle with organic paragraph transitions. To move from one concept to another, they frequently rely on dramatic, standalone "hook" sentences that sound like a TV presenter introducing the next segment.
