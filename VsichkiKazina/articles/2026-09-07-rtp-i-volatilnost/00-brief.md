## ANGLE (editorial concept-education, no operator facts)
- RTP is a LONG-RUN statistic computed over millions of spins, never a session promise.
- Worked ILLUSTRATIVE € example: at 96% RTP, across €1 000 total wagered, the long-run EXPECTED
  house take ≈ €40 (house edge 4%). But session variance/дисперсия dwarfs that ±€40 — a single
  session can land far above or below.
- Волатилност = frequency-vs-size of payouts. Ниска = чести малки печалби; висока = редки големи.
  A temperament/budget fit, NOT „по-добра/по-лоша". Two games at identical RTP feel completely
  different because of волатилност.
- Домашно предимство = обратната страна на RTP (100% − RTP); публично изчислимо за игри с фиксирани
  правила (блекджек с вярна базова стратегия под 1%).
- Демо режим = honest way to FEEL волатилност before real money.
- Distinguish RTP (дългосрочно) from вашата конкретна сесия (дисперсия).

## ILLUSTRATIVE FIGURES (all hypothetical — NO operator, NO real game named)
- RTP 96% → house edge 4%.
- €1 000 total wagered @ 96% RTP → expected long-run house take ≈ €40 (expected return ≈ €960).
- Typical RTP framing: 96%+ = high, 95–96% = average, under 94% = low. RTP to two decimals where a
  game states it (illustrative example 96.82%).
- Ниска волатилност illustrative shape: payout frequency ~70%, average prize ~60% of stake.
- Висока волатилност illustrative shape: payout frequency ~40%, average prize ~200% of stake.
- Блекджек с базова стратегия: домашно предимство под 1% (fixed-rules, publicly computable).
NB: these percentages are GENERAL/ILLUSTRATIVE educational figures, not claims about any specific game.
