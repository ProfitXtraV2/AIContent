# 01 — Synthesis (facts dossier)

## Angle
National-team history guide. The core and emotional centre is the 1994 World Cup
fourth place (USA '94), but the piece traces the whole arc: origins (1924, БФС 1923),
the long pre-1994 drought, the golden run, and where the team sits in world football
by achievement (not by current form). Strictly evergreen: no current squads, standings,
fixtures, odds or predictions. Signed Георги Тодоров, editorial neutral voice.

## Confirmed facts (with source tags)
- **Nickname**: Лъвовете; alt Трикольорите. [wiki-nt]
- **БФС**: founded 1923. [wiki-nt]
- **Stadium**: Национален стадион „Васил Левски", София. [wiki-nt]
- **First official international**: 21.05.1924, Виена, Австрия 6:0 България (loss). [wiki-nt]
- **World Cup finals (7)**: 1962, 1966, 1970, 1974, 1986, 1994, 1998. [wiki-wc]
- **Pre-1994 drought**: no win in World Cup finals across the 5 prior appearances. [wiki-wc / search]
- **Qualification for 1994**: 17.11.1993, Париж; Емил Костадинов гол в 90' eliminates France. [wiki-nt]
- **USA '94 path**:
  - R16: Мексико 1:1 (aet), 3:1 на дузпи. [wiki-wc]
  - QF: Германия 2:1, 10.07.1994 (Giants Stadium); Стоичков (свободен удар) 76', Лечков (глава) 79'; Матеус дузпа за Германия. Победа над действащия световен шампион. [bta][espn]
  - SF: Италия 1:2; два гола Роберто Баджо. [wiki-wc]
  - 3rd place: Швеция 4:0 (loss). [wiki-wc]
  - Final placing: **4th** — best ever. [wiki-wc]
- **Стоичков**: Golden Boot СП '94, 6 гола (shared w/ Oleg Salenko); Ballon d'Or 1994. [wiki-nt]
- **Euro finals**: 1996, 2004 — group stage both times. [wiki-nt]
- **Most caps**: Стилиян Петров, 105. [wiki-nt]
- **Top scorer**: Димитър Бербатов, 48 — infobox shows Berbatov & Bonev both near 48;
  exact tally ambiguous → [VERIFY: точен брой/дали наравно с Христо Бонев]. [wiki-nt]
- **Olympic football silver**: 1968. [search]
