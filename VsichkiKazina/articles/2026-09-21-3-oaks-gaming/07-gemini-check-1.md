### Flagged Passages & AI Patterns

**1. Pattern: Formulaic Benefit Translation (The "What this means for you" trope)**
> **Quote:** *"За теб това означава, че ще срещнеш едни и същи слотове на 3 Oaks в сайтове, които иначе нямат нищо общо помежду си."*
*   **Why it flagged:** LLMs are hard-coded to be "helpful" and user-centric. When fed facts (like aggregator partnerships), they almost always use the exact transitional formula: *"For you, this means that..."* (За теб това означава, че...). It feels like a mechanical pivot from B2B facts to B2C benefits.
*   **Recommendation:** Remove the formulaic pivot and state the outcome directly as a natural continuation of the thought. For example, you could merge the idea into the previous sentence by simply stating that because of these integrations, players will find these exact same slots across completely unrelated casino sites.

**2. Pattern: The Robotic Data-Dump (Over-stuffed spec sentence)**
> **Quote:** *"Следващите части вдигат тавана: Sun of Egypt 3 е с RTP 95.61%, 5x3 барабана, 25 начина за печалба и максимум 10 000 пъти залога, а Sun of Egypt 4 върви с RTP 95.47%, решетка 5x4 и същия таван от 10 000 пъти залога."*
*   **Why it flagged:** When AI is asked to compare two products, it tends to cram all the numerical specs into a single, heavily comma-spliced sentence. It reads like a database readout rather than a narrative comparison.
*   **Recommendation:** Break this into two distinct, conversational sentences. Let the text breathe. You might introduce Sun of Egypt 3's specs first, and then use a separate sentence to highlight exactly what Sun of Egypt 4 changed (the grid size and the slight RTP drop) while keeping the max win the same. 

**3. Pattern: The "Wrap-Up" Summary Sentence (Micro-conclusion)**
> **Quote:** *"Общото остава принципът: залепени символи, повторни завъртания и таван, който се стига само при пълен екран."*
*   **Why it flagged:** LLMs have a strong structural bias toward summarizing the paragraph they just wrote. After explaining the mechanics and the jackpots, this sentence acts as a neat, slightly redundant bow tying up the paragraph. 
*   **Recommendation:** You can make this read less like a summary by integrating it into the opening of that paragraph rather than leaving it at the end, or by rephrasing it to focus on *why* players chase this mechanic, rather than just summarizing the mechanics you just explained.

**4. Pattern: Didactic Signposting**
> **Quote:** *"Точната стойност стои в информацията на самата игра, преди да заложиш. А RTP е дългосрочна статистика върху милиони завъртания, не обещание какво ще стане в твоята сесия."*
*   **Why it flagged:** While the information is excellent and necessary, starting the second sentence with "А RTP е..." after a direct instruction feels slightly preachy—a common tone LLMs adopt when explaining gambling concepts. 
*   **Recommendation:** Connect the two thoughts more fluidly. Instead of a hard stop and a new sentence starting with "A", consider linking the location of the RTP directly to its definition as a long-term metric, softening the didactic tone while preserving the exact meaning.
