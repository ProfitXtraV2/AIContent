# 01 — SYNTHESIS · vk-0189 · Cash or Crash (Evolution)

## BALL MACHINE (drum composition)
- 28 balls total: 19 green + 8 red + 1 gold. (19+8+1 = 28.) — livecasinocomparer; crashgamesplay;
  crashgames.guide; Evolution press ("20 balls advancing... just 8 red balls" + gold). [solid]
- Green climbs one rung; red crashes the round (loses stake unless already cashed / shielded);
  gold = shield absorbing one red + boosts remaining ladder payouts. — Evolution; livecasinocomparer. [solid]
- Derived first-draw odds: red 8/28 ≈ 28,6%; green 19/28 ≈ 67,9%; gold 1/28 ≈ 3,6%. [derived]
- Balls are NOT returned → red share rises as you climb; after 10 greens: 9 green + 8 red + 1 gold = 18 left,
  red ≈ 8/18 ≈ 44%. [derived]

## LADDER & DECISIONS
- 20-level ladder; multiplier rises each rung. — livecasinocomparer; crashgamesplay; crashgames.guide. [solid]
- Three decisions each step: "Take All" (cash out) / "Take Half" (bank 50%, continue) / "Continue" (pass).
  — Evolution; livecasinocomparer; crashgamesplay. [solid]

## RTP (strategy-dependent — the article's core honest angle)
- Optimal / disciplined early cash-out ≈ 99,59% (Evolution: "99,6% when played optimally"). — Evolution;
  crashgamesplay; livecasinocomparer; crashgames.guide. [solid on 99,59% figure]
- Always chasing the top / max risk ≈ 94,51% (lowest). — crashgamesplay; americancasinoguide. [VERIFY]
