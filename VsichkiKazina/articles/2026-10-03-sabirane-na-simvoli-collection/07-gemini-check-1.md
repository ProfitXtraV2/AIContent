### **Flagged Passages, Patterns, and Recommendations**

#### 1. Pattern: Defensive Signposting / Meta-commentary
AI models often generate paragraphs that explicitly state what the text *is* or *isn't* doing, acting as a defensive boundary for the prompt they were given. 

*   **Flagged Text:** 
    *   *Heading:* `## Това е класът механики, не разбор на отделна игра`
    *   *Passage:* `Тук говоря за принципа, общ за всички игри, които го ползват. Конкретни заглавия като Duck Hunters, Gems Bonanza и Pirots 2 имат свои отделни разбори... Тази страница е общият „как работи", а не ревю на нито една от тях.`
*   **Recommendation:** This entire H2 section interrupts the flow and reads like an AI explaining its own constraints to the user. You don't need a dedicated subheading to justify the article's scope. **Recommendation:** Remove the H2 entirely and weave this disclaimer naturally into the introductory paragraph (e.g., just mention in passing that while games like Duck Hunters have specific rules, this guide covers the universal mechanics). 

#### 2. Pattern: The "Dictionary Definition" Opening
LLMs love to start explanations with a clinical, direct definition ("X works like this: [Definition]"). It creates a slightly robotic, textbook-like rhythm right out of the gate.

*   **Flagged Text:** `Механиката на събиране работи така: определени символи или монети падат по време на завъртанията и вместо да платят веднага самостоятелно, се трупат в брояч на екрана.`
*   **Recommendation:** To make the hook more engaging and human, shift the focus from a mechanical definition to the player's sensory experience. **Recommendation:** Suggest rephrasing the opening to start with what the player actually sees happening on the screen (e.g., "You spin the reels, a special coin drops, and instead of paying out, it flies into a meter at the top of the screen..."). 

#### 3. Pattern: The "Philosophical Summary" Conclusion
AI struggles to end articles naturally. It usually resorts to a sweeping, philosophical summary that tries to tie a neat bow around the topic, often shifting into a slightly preachy tone.

*   **Flagged Text:** `Хазартът не е финансова стратегия, а ротативката е платено забавление с предварително известна дългосрочна цена. Пълнещият се бар прави това забавление по-въвличащо; колко струва то обаче, е решено далеч преди да падне и първият символ за събиране.`
*   **Recommendation:** While the responsible gambling sentiment here is excellent (and must remain), the sudden shift to "Gambling is not a financial strategy" feels disconnected from the previous paragraph about game mechanics. **Recommendation:** Suggest adding a brief transitional sentence at the start of this paragraph that bridges the gap between the mechanics discussed above and the reality of the math. (e.g., "Because these mechanics are designed to keep you engaged, it's vital to remember the math behind them: gambling is not a financial strategy..."). 

#### 4. Pattern: Staccato / Symmetrical Paragraph Sizing
If you look at the text visually, almost all the paragraphs under the H2s are roughly the same length (4-5 sentences). AI tends to generate highly symmetrical text blocks, whereas human writing usually features a mix of very short, punchy paragraphs and longer, explanatory ones.
*   **Recommendation:** Break up the text visually. For example, in the `## Какво всъщност мести натрупването` section, suggest hitting "Enter" before `Оттук идва и практичната предпазливост...` to create a standalone, punchy paragraph that emphasizes the practical advice.
