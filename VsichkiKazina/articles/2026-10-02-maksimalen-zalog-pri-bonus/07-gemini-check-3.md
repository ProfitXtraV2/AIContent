### 1. Pattern: Formulaic Subheading Structure (The "Troubleshooting" Tail)
**The Flagged Text:** 
> *H2: Ако вече сте го нарушили*
> (followed immediately by)
> *H2: Как да не го нарушите*

**Why it reads as AI:** 
This is a classic LLM structural pattern. When asked to explain a concept, AI almost always structures the final third of the article as a troubleshooting guide: "What to do if X happens" followed by "How to prevent X." It creates a predictable, textbook-like rhythm that feels slightly disconnected from the engaging narrative of the earlier sections.

**Recommendation:** 
Break the formula by combining these two sections into a single, more organically titled H2 (e.g., something like "Стратегии за запазване на бонуса" or "Какво следва при нарушение"). Alternatively, weave the "How not to break it" advice directly into the earlier section (*"Колко лесно се нарушава по невнимание"*), so the article flows as a continuous narrative rather than a list of scenarios.

### 2. Pattern: The Didactic / Preachy Wrap-up (The "Moral of the Story")
**The Flagged Text:** 
> *"Правило, което може да изтрие печалбата ви с едно завъртане, се чете, преди да депозирате."*

**Why it reads as AI:** 
LLMs struggle to simply *end* an article. They are programmed to provide a helpful, conclusive summary, which often manifests as a slightly preachy, moralizing lesson or universal advice at the end of a paragraph. While the phrasing here is good, the tone suddenly shifts from objective/analytical to a scolding teacher.

**Recommendation:** 
Soften the didactic tone. Instead of telling the reader what "must be read" as a universal truth, frame it as a strategic advantage. For example, you could recommend changing it to something like: *"Проверката на това правило преди депозит е най-сигурният начин да избегнете неприятни изненади."* (Note: Do not use this exact rewrite, but guide the author to adjust the tone to be less commanding).

### 3. Pattern: Textbook Transition (Signposting)
**The Flagged Text:** 
> *"Към всичко това се добавя и приносът на игрите."* (Start of the 3rd paragraph under "Сметката")

**Why it reads as AI:** 
AI frequently uses explicit, additive signposting to glue two related concepts together (e.g., "Adding to this," "Furthermore," "Another factor to consider"). It feels a bit like a high-school essay transition rather than a natural progression of thought.

**Recommendation:** 
Advise the writer to delete the signpost entirely and jump straight into the action. The paragraph can simply start with the core subject: *"Приносът на различните игри допълнително усложнява сметката. Ротативките обикновено броят 100%..."* This creates a punchier, more confident flow.

### 4. Pattern: The "List-in-a-Paragraph" (Imperative Stacking)
**The Flagged Text:** 
> *"Преди първото завъртане отворете информацията... Нагласете залога на или под тази граница... Оставете купуването на бонус..."*

**Why it reads as AI:** 
When AI is told not to use bullet points, it often compensates by writing a dense paragraph made entirely of imperative commands stacked back-to-back. It creates a staccato, robotic rhythm.

**Recommendation:** 
Suggest breaking up the rhythm by varying the sentence structures. Instead of starting every sentence with a command (Open, Adjust, Leave), the writer can introduce conditional clauses or explain the *why* before the *what*.
