**Verdict: Shows AI patterns, 75% confidence.**

While the article is highly informative, logically structured, and grammatically flawless, its rhetorical style heavily relies on classic Large Language Model (LLM) writing tropes. Specifically, the text suffers from an over-reliance on **rhetorical contrasts (antithesis)**, **narrated emotion**, and **poetic/moralizing wrap-ups**. AI models, particularly GPT-4, use these patterns to simulate an "engaging" or "authoritative" tone, but to a trained eye, they make the text feel overly polished, slightly preachy, and distinctly synthetic. 

Here is the breakdown of the specific patterns that triggered this assessment, along with actionable recommendations.

---

### 1. Pattern: The "X, not Y" Rhetorical Contrast Overuse
LLMs love to create punchy, balanced sentences by contrasting two ideas. Doing this once is fine; doing it five times in a short article is a massive AI fingerprint. 

**Flagged Passages:**
*   *"За казиното това е застраховка, не наказание."* (For the casino this is insurance, not a punishment.)
*   *"Един голям залог е чист късмет... Хиляда малки залога са статистика..."* (One big bet is pure luck... A thousand small bets are statistics...)
*   *"...границата се движи, а вие не."* (...the boundary moves, but you don't.)
*   *"Бонусът е забавление с условия, не безплатни пари..."* (A bonus is entertainment with conditions, not free money...)
*   *"Максималният залог е коланът на казиното, не вашият."* (The maximum bet is the casino's belt, not yours.)

**Recommendation:** 
Break the symmetry. Human writers rarely speak in constant dualities. 
*   Change *"За казиното това е застраховка, не наказание"* to a direct statement of function (e.g., "Казината използват това правило, за да ограничат риска си"). 
*   Remove the poetic *"границата се движи, а вие не"* and just explain the mechanical danger of floating ceilings. 
*   Cut the *"забавление с условия, не безплатни пари"* cliché entirely—it sounds like a corporate PSA.

### 2. Pattern: Narrated Emotion & Conversational Filler
AI struggles to just state a negative fact; it feels the need to cushion it by telling the reader how to feel about it or by using conversational filler to introduce the paragraph.

**Flagged Passages:**
*   *"Най-неприятното в правилото е, че рядко го нарушавате нарочно."* (The most unpleasant thing about the rule is...)
*   *"Честният отговор рядко е приятен: при автоматично анулиране..."* (The honest answer is rarely pleasant...)

**Recommendation:**
Delete the emotional framing and start directly with the substantive point. 
*   Instead of *"Най-неприятното в правилото е, че..."*, start with: *"Играчите най-често нарушават правилото без да осъзнават."* 
*   Instead of *"Честният отговор рядко е приятен: при автоматично..."*, just state the reality: *"При автоматично анулиране печалбата от бонуса обикновено не се връща..."*

### 3. Pattern: The "Silver-Lining" / Didactic Conclusion
LLMs are programmed to be helpful and positive, which means they almost never end an article abruptly. They tend to wrap up negative scenarios with a "lesson learned" or a philosophical takeaway.

**Flagged Passage:**
*   *"Най-ценното, което остава след такава вечер, е навикът да отваряте условията преди, а не след заявката за теглене."* (The most valuable thing left after such an evening is the habit of opening the terms...)

**Recommendation:**
Kill the silver lining. It sounds patronizing. End the paragraph on the practical advice about contacting support or opting out of the bonus, without trying to teach the reader a life lesson about "building habits."

### 4. Pattern: The Metaphorical Over-Polish
AI often tries to end articles with a sweeping, dramatic metaphor to sound authoritative. 

**Flagged Passage:**
*   *"Максималният залог е коланът на казиното, не вашият. Пази неговите пари от едрия ви замах, а вашата единствена защита срещу него е да знаете числото, преди жетонът да е на масата."*

**Recommendation:**
This reads like the ending of a movie monologue, not a casino guide. Delete the "casino's belt" metaphor and the "big swing" imagery. Replace it with a dry, factual concluding sentence about checking the specific limit before placing the first bet.
