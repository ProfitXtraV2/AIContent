### 1. The "Not X, but Y" Antithesis (Formulaic Conclusions)
Advanced AI models love to wrap up paragraphs with a perfectly symmetrical, punchy contrast. When used once, it’s great copywriting. When used in almost every section, it creates a predictable, robotic rhythm.

*   **Flagged Passages:**
    *   *"Числото описва казиното в мащаб, не теб тази вечер."* (The number describes the casino at scale, not you tonight.)
    *   *"Бонусът мени размера на забавлението, не математиката под него."* (The bonus changes the size of the entertainment, not the math beneath it.)
    *   *"Безплатното в бонуса е шансът за повече игра, не изключение от предимството."* (The free part of the bonus is the chance for more play, not an exception to the edge.)
    *   *"...това не е песимизъм, а аритметика."* (...this is not pessimism, but arithmetic.)
    *   *"...третираш хазарта като платено забавление с познаваема цена, не като начин да правиш пари."* (...treat gambling as paid entertainment with a knowable price, not as a way to make money.)
*   **Recommendation:** Break the symmetry. You don't need to end every thought with a mic-drop contrast. For example, instead of the "pessimism/arithmetic" line, just end on the mathematical reality. Instead of the "entertainment/math" contrast for bonuses, integrate the concept into the previous sentence (e.g., explain that bonuses extend playtime but are still subject to the same mathematical edge, without phrasing it as a catchy slogan). 

### 2. Dramatic Signposting and Colons
AI frequently uses a specific setup-and-reveal structure, often utilizing a colon or a numbered metaphor to hold the reader's hand through the logic.

*   **Flagged Passages:**
    *   *"Това число има име: домашно предимство."* (This number has a name: house edge.)
    *   *"На масата тази логика се разбива в две стени. Първата е... Втората е..."* (On the table this logic crashes into two walls. The first is... The second is...)
*   **Recommendation:** Flatten the dramatic setups. Instead of "This number has a name: house edge," weave the term naturally into the sentence (e.g., "This is due to the house edge, which ensures..."). For the Martingale section, remove the "crashes into two walls" metaphor. Just state directly that the system fails in practice due to table limits and bankroll constraints, without numbering them as "The first is / The second is".

### 3. Narrated Evaluation / Over-polished Transitions
AI often tells the reader how to perceive a concept before actually explaining it, or uses heavy-handed transitional sentences to pivot between ideas.

*   **Flagged Passages:**
    *   *"Идеята звучи неустоимо логично."* (The idea sounds irresistibly logical.)
    *   *"Дълбокият проблем обаче е математически, не практически."* (The deep problem, however, is mathematical, not practical.)
*   **Recommendation:** Delete the narrated evaluations. You don't need to tell the reader that Martingale sounds "irresistibly logical"—just explain the €1, €2, €4 progression and let the apparent logic speak for itself. Remove the "mathematical, not practical" transition; just move straight into explaining how the expected loss is calculated.

### 4. The "Honest Answer" Hook
AI frequently opens articles by contrasting what people *want* to hear with the "honest" or "harsh" reality.

*   **Flagged Passage:**
    *   *"Търсенето „как да спечелим от казино" очаква трик. Честният отговор е, че такъв няма..."* (The search "how to win at a casino" expects a trick. The honest answer is that there isn't one...)
*   **Recommendation:** While this is a decent hook, it's a known AI trope. Consider rephrasing the opening to be slightly more direct. Start immediately with the reality of how casino games are built, rather than narrating the psychology of the Google search.
