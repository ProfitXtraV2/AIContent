INTRO (no heading, before the first H2)
Facts: чл. 49 ЗХ sets out 4 types of lottery games. Under чл. 4, ал. 3 + чл. 13а, a licence for lottery games can go only to the state, which runs them through ДП БСТ. The exceptions are томбола and бинго (and their variants). The monopoly dates from 2020 (§ 9 ПЗР, ДВ бр. 14/2020).
Terms: лотария (first sentence), Закон за хазарта, БСТ
Direction: Open on the finding itself: the law defines four kinds of lottery, and almost all of them may be run only by the state. Name the two exceptions and stop. Do not add a wrap-up sentence about the rest of the article.

SECTION 1 — Четирите вида лотарии според Закона за хазарта
Facts:
- чл. 41, ал. 1: lottery games are one of the 5 permitted kinds of gambling. Name the other four in one parenthesis.
- чл. 41, ал. 1, last sentence: the method or technical means of offering a game does not change its type. Plant this here because Section 4 relies on it.
- чл. 49: the four types. Then one definition per type:
  - традиционна лотария (чл. 50, 51): every issued ticket enters the draw; fixed prizes on a scheme approved by the ИД на НАП; ticket = number, series, nominal; ≥50% of the value of ALL tickets is paid out as cash and/or goods.
  - томбола (чл. 52): only sold tickets enter; public draw; fixed prizes, goods only; ≥50% of ticket value.
  - числова лотарийна игра (чл. 54–57): тото / лото / бинго / кено.
    - тото: prizes are a pre-set % of receipts, ≥50% (чл. 55, ал. 2; чл. 56).
    - лото and кено: pre-set coefficients that do not depend on receipts (чл. 55, ал. 3).
    - бинго: pre-printed combinations; ≥50% of receipts; paid out immediately after they are announced (чл. 57).
  - моментна лотария (чл. 49 т. 4, чл. 59): scratch-off cover; prize revealed when the combination is uncovered; fixed prizes; ≥50% of the value of all tickets.
- Comparison table, 4 rows × 5 columns (Вид / Кои билети участват / Как се определя печалбата / Онлайн / Кой организира). The brief explicitly allows this table, and it is the most useful thing on the page.
Terms: видове лотарии, лотария, числова лотарийна игра, тото, лото, бинго, кено, томбола, моментна лотария, скреч
Direction: Frame all four types around two questions only: which tickets take part in the draw, and what determines the prize. Give each type a definition of uneven length. Томбола can be two sentences. Числова gets the short sub-list because its three payout logics really do differ. Then the table.
Shape: definitions of deliberately uneven length, then the table. Longest section, ~420 words including the table.

SECTION 2 — Кой има право да организира лотария
Facts:
- чл. 3: every gambling game needs a licence from ИД на НАП, and the licence covers only the game named in it.
- The state may hold a licence only to support sport, culture, health, education and social causes.
- НАП has supervised gambling since 8 август 2020 г.
- чл. 4, ал. 3 (ред. ДВ бр. 14/2021): lottery licences go only to the state, except томбола, бинго and their variants.
- чл. 13а: the state runs them through ДП БСТ, attached to the министър на младежта и спорта.
- 2020: § 9 ПЗР ended non-БСТ lottery licences, except томбола/бинго/кено. Unpaid prizes remain payable.
- One sentence saying a separate guide covers Национална лотария and the history of private lotteries. NO link (vk-0271 is not live).
- томбола: only ЮЛНЦ (чл. 53), charitable purpose only, one-off (чл. 15). Contrast a charity-ball tombola with a weekly cash „томбола" run by a company.
- кено: one sentence that an active private licence could not be confirmed, plus the link.
Terms: лотария, НАП, ДП „Български спортен тотализатор", ЮЛНЦ, томбола, кено
Direction: Build the legal chain step by step: licence from НАП, then state-only for lotteries, then БСТ as the vehicle, then the 2020 cut-off. End on the two real exceptions, with the томбола contrast as the concrete image. Keep the 2020 history to two sentences at most.
Shape: narrative build, 3 short paragraphs + 1 two-sentence кено paragraph, ~260 words.
Internal link: **/blog/keno-pravila/**, anchored on the кено rules mention.
FLAGS: [VERIFY: active private кено licence after 2020 — НАП licensed-organiser lists] (tied to the [CONFLICT] in Section 1).

SECTION 4 — Онлайн лотария: законно е само през toto.bg
Facts:
- чл. 41, ал. 2: everything may be offered online except томбола and моментна лотария, so online scratch is not legal.
- Only БСТ holds the licence, which makes toto.bg the legal route.
- toto.bg:
  - registration and identification under the Наредба (ПМС № 50 от 15.02.2021 г.), with data sent to a НАП server;
  - ePay.bg or card via БОРИКА;
  - winnings from online ТОТО 2 and Тото Джокер bets are transferred to the account automatically;
  - the site is unavailable Thu/Sun 18:40–19:10.
- Foreign or unlicensed „лотарийни" sites: the medium does not change the type of game (callback to Section 1), and without a БГ licence the game is illegal.
- From 01.08.2026, НАП blocks access to unlicensed sites by its own decision within 24 hours (чл. 17, ал. 6).
- The player is also liable: чл. 9, ал. 14 + чл. 97а, ал. 2, fine 500–2000 лв.
- НАП publishes lists of licensed and unlicensed sites.
Terms: онлайн лотария (H2), лотария онлайн (once in the body, e.g. the first sentence of the foreign-sites paragraph), toto.bg, ePay.bg, БОРИКА, НАП, ПМС № 50
Direction: Answer the legal question in the first sentence (what can be played online and where), then describe toto.bg as a flowing paragraph, NOT bullets, because Sections 3 and 5 are already lists. End on the player's own liability. That is the point most readers will not expect.
Shape: blunt one-sentence answer, then a flowing practical paragraph (4–5 sentences), then a narrative paragraph on foreign sites, ~260 words.

SECTION 5 — Как да познаете незаконна лотария
Facts:

Terms: лотария, незаконна лотария, НАП, томбола, 18+
