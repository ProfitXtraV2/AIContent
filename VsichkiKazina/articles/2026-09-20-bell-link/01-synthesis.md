# 01 — Synthesis (Bell Link)

## Core proposition
Bell Link is EGT Digital's hold-and-win LINKED jackpot layer bolted onto classic EGT slots. Honest angle: it adds volatility and four jackpot tiers but does NOT change the base-game odds; only Grand+Major are progressive/pooled, Minor+Mini are fixed multiples of the bet; RTP is per-title and operator-configurable → check the info panel. No fabricated seeds/contributions.

## Verified fact spine (provider + game-DB, NOT operator T&C/НАП)
- Bell Link = EGT Digital hold-and-win jackpot on a family of EGT slots (suffix "... Bell Link"); land-based origin → online via EGT Digital (arm of EGT / Euro Games Technology). Distinct from Amusnet.
- 4 levels Mini/Minor/Major/Grand. Grand+Major progressive & pooled network-wide; Minor+Mini fixed. Fixed ~50×/10× bet [VERIFY per title].

- Games: 40 Super Hot Bell Link, 40 Burning Hot Bell Link, Flaming Hot Bell Link, 40 Shining Crown Bell Link, Burning Hot Bell Link.
- RTP title-dependent/configurable; 40 Super Hot Bell Link ~96.5% [VERIFY]; no system-wide RTP.
