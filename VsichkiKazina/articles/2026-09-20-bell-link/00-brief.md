## FACTS (public provider + game-DB data — NOT operator T&C / НАП)
- Bell Link = EGT Digital "hold-and-win" (Hold & Win) linked jackpot feature added to a family of EGT slots (suffix "... Bell Link"). Land-based origin, brought online by EGT Digital (the online/digital arm of EGT / Euro Games Technology). Keep EGT Digital distinct from Amusnet (the separate rebrand of EGT Interactive).
- 4 levels: Mini, Minor, Major, Grand. Grand + Major = PROGRESSIVE, pooled/linked across bets & games in the network. Minor + Mini = FIXED (non-progressive).
- Fixed values reported ~Minor 50× bet, Mini 10× bet (game-DB, per-title) → include with [VERIFY] (varies by title/config).
- Trigger: land 5+ Bell symbols in one spin (any mix). Starts a Hold & Win: bells lock/stick, other cells respin. Start = 3 respins; each new bell RESETS respins to 3; ends when respins run out or grid fills.
- Bells carry random cash values ~1×–100× bet; special/coloured bells map to the 4 jackpot levels. Full-grid multiplier exists in some titles (do NOT pin a number; note it does NOT apply to Grand/Major).
- Games: 40 Super Hot Bell Link, 40 Burning Hot Bell Link, Flaming Hot Bell Link, 40 Shining Crown Bell Link, Burning Hot Bell Link, etc.
- RTP: title-dependent, often operator-configurable. 40 Super Hot Bell Link ~96.5% (game-DB) → include as one title's figure with [VERIFY], NOT a system-wide RTP.
- Distinct from Jackpot Cards (card-suit collection) and Clover Chance (clover mystery jackpot).
