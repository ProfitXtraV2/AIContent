- ## Четирите нива: кои растат и кои са фиксирани — Mini/Minor/Major/Grand; Grand+Major progressive & pooled; Minor+Mini fixed (~50×/10× [VERIFY]); seeds/contributions not published → no invented numbers. INFOGRAPHIC here.
