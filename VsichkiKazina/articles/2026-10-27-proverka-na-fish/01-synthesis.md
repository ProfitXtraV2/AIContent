# 01 — Synthesis · vk-0297 (проверка на фиш Тото)

Byline: Георги Тодоров (editorial „ние" register) · Market: BG · Type: guide
Intent: a player with a фиш wants to know, fast and free, if it won — online, in the app, or at a пункт.

## Fact inventory (every item traces to 00-brief.md; source in brackets)
1. Online check is per game, via a „Проверка на фиш" form: `/check/6x49`, `/check/6x42`, `/check/5x35`, `/check/zodiak`, `/check/rojdenden` [info.toto.bg].
2. On that form you mark the numbers you played **по области** and pick your **ТИРАЖ** from a dropdown, then press **„ПРОВЕРИ ФИШ"** („Изчисти" clears it). No barcode / QR / serial — manual numbers [info.toto.bg/check/6x49].
3. For a subscription (абонамент) фиш you pick the last тираж included in the abonament [verbatim: „Ако си играл с абонамент, тогава от полето „ТИРАЖ“ избираш последния, включен в абонамента, тираж."].
4. Тото Джокер is checked together with the main ticket number, not as a stand-alone number grid [info.toto.bg].
5. Official results live on `/results/<game>` (6x49, 6x42, 5x35, joker, zodiak, rojdenden; Тото 1: 13t1/12t1/10t1; second-chance has its own pages) — winning numbers + prize groups per тираж [info.toto.bg].
6. Instant games („моментни" / стъргалки) use a separate „ПРОВЕРКА НА МОМЕНТНИ ИГРИ" path on toto.bg, not the number form [info.toto.bg].
7. The official „Спорт Тото" app for Android/iOS is linked from info.toto.bg (App Store + Google Play) [info.toto.bg]. Its exact ticket-check capability → [VERIFY].
8. At a тото пункт the terminal checks the physical фиш; the пункт-issued бон is what is valid for the payout [Capital.bg 2013 — [VERIFY current]].
9. Втори тото шанс is a free raffle among receipts, drawn by lot and published as press releases — not checked with the number form [carried from vk-0268].
