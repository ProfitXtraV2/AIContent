## Original draft (facts only, pre-voice)
Cleocatra is a 2022 Pragmatic Play slot on a 5x4 grid with 40 fixed paylines, Egyptian cat theme. High volatility (5/5). Default RTP 96.20% (house edge 3.80%); Pragmatic also ships 95.50% and 94.50% builds. Max win 5000x; bet 0.20–100. Base game: a respin triggers when a full vertical stack of matching cats lands on reel 1 — the stack and any wilds lock, other reels respin until a winning combination forms. Wilds carry a x2 or x3 multiplier. Free spins: 3/4/5 scatters award 8/12/16 spins; in the bonus all wilds turn sticky until the round ends and keep their x2/x3 multipliers, and a full stack of cats on reel 1 can add spins. Bonus buy costs 100x the current bet and jumps straight to the free spins (availability varies by market). No ante bet. Worked example at 96.20%: €1000 turnover returns ~€962 to the player long-run, ~€38 to the casino.
