## FACTS (all researched — see 06-verification.md)
- 6 decks standard; one card each, higher wins even money; ace high; suits irrelevant. Player & dealer each win 46.3% on first card; tie ~7.4%.
- Tie → surrender (lose half ante) OR go to war (raise = ante; dealer burns 3; one more card each). War: player card ≥ dealer → raise wins 1:1, original ante PUSHES; dealer higher → lose both.
- House edge (6 decks): always war (no bonus) 2.88% (per ante); element of risk 2.70%; always surrender 3.70%; with 1x tie-after-tie bonus 2.33%; super-liberal 3x bonus ~1.24%.
- Going to war is ALWAYS the lower-edge play vs surrender.
- Tie side bet: pays 10:1; house edge 18.65% (6 decks); 11:1 variant → 11.25%.
- More decks → slightly higher base edge, lower Tie-bet edge.
- RTP derived: war ~97.12%; surrender ~96.30%; 1x bonus ~97.67%.
