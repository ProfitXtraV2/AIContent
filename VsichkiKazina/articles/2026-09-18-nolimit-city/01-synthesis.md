## Reader & intent

Commercial-informational. ## Core message
Nolimit City is a Stockholm-founded (2014), now Evolution-owned game maker known for extreme volatility, distinctive "x" mechanics (xWays, xSplit, xNudge, xBomb, Enhancer Cells) and very high max-win caps. The honest catch: those huge caps are theoretical ceilings, the games burn a bankroll fast, RTP ships in several builds the operator picks (Fire in the Hole 2: 96.07% down to 87.05%), and the feature buy is restricted in some regulated markets. Check the real RTP in the info panel; what matters legally for the BG player is the casino's НАП licence, not the studio's B2B licence.
