## PROVIDER FACTS (web-verified — see SOURCES)
- Company: Nolimit City. Founded 2014, Stockholm, Sweden, by former NetEnt developers. Teams also in Malta and India. 100+ HTML5 video slots.
- Acquired by Evolution AB: agreement announced 22 June 2022, completed 8 August 2022; total consideration up to €340M (upfront €200M + earn-outs up to €140M). CORRECTION: repo note said 01.07.2022 — primary press release (Cision) gives completion 08.08.2022. Uses the verified date; the article says "обявена през юни и завършена през август 2022 г."
- Group siblings after acquisition: NetEnt, Red Tiger, Big Time Gaming; studio keeps its own name/identity.
- Signature mechanics (the "x" family): xWays (one symbol → stack of identical, more ways), xSplit (splits symbols to its left in two), xNudge (wild nudges to fill reel, +1 multiplier per nudge), xBomb (wild explodes, removes adjacent symbols, +1x), Enhancer Cells (locked cells under middle reels, unlock on cascades).
- Identity: extreme/high volatility, dark provocative themes (prison/crime/war/mining), very high max-win caps; feature-buy (bonus-buy) culture.
- RTP policy: multiple certified RTP builds per title; operator chooses which to load. Example: Fire in the Hole 2 = 96.07% default, plus 94.08%, 92.07%, 87.05%.
- Feature buy restricted/banned in some regulated markets: UK bonus-buy banned for players since 31 Oct 2021; titles remain on UKGC sites in compliant builds with the buy button removed.

## HONEST ANGLE (brand doctrine)
Extreme volatility presented as RISK and a budget/temperament fit, never as a promise. Max-win caps are theoretical ceilings, not expected outcomes. RTP is a long-run statistic over millions of spins; multiple builds exist so the operator picks the number → check the info panel. Feature buy is expensive up-front and eases loss-chasing → restricted in some markets. Studio licences ≠ casino licence; what matters for the BG player is the operator's НАП licence.
