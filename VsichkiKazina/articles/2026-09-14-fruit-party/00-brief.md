## GAME FACTS (provider + game-database sourced)
- Game: Fruit Party · Provider: Pragmatic Play
- Release: May 2020 (SlotCatalog: 21.05.2020 exclusive / 28.05.2020 wide)
- Grid: 7×7 (49 клетки)
- Mechanic: cluster pays — win when 5 or more matching symbols touch horizontally or vertically anywhere on the grid (no paylines)
- Tumble / cascading wins: winning symbols disappear, new ones fall, chain continues while wins keep forming
- Random multipliers: in the base game winning symbols can carry a random 2x multiplier; multiple multipliers on one win COMBINE multiplicatively up to a max of 256x. In free spins the random multipliers are 2x or 4x, same combine-up-to-256x cap. (Not persistent marked-cell spots like Sugar Rush — random per spin/tumble.)
- Free spins: 3 or more golden-peach scatters award 10 free spins; retriggerable (10-14). A Buy Feature lets the player start free spins for 100x the stake.
- RTP: default/headline 96.50% (Pragmatic Play official game page; house edge 3.50%). Offered in lower configurable builds too — around 95.50% and 94.50% (game databases list the precise three-tier as 96.47% / 95.48% / 94.45%). Operator chooses which build to load → check the info panel.
- Max win: 5,000x the stake
- Volatility: rated inconsistently across databases — medium (SlotCatalog, 3.5/5) to high/very high (AskGamblers). → [VERIFY] band; frame practically (multipliers concentrate the payout).
- Bet range: €0.20 to €100 (per spin)

## HONEST ANGLE (this brand always takes)

- Random multipliers scale the VARIANCE, not the long-run edge — they are already inside the RTP.
