## Entity union (concepts to cover)
- Заблуда на комарджията / gambler's fallacy (a.k.a. Monte Carlo fallacy) — independence of RNG events; „ред е на другото" is false.
- „Дължи ми се" / „I'm due" — the fallacy pointed at oneself.
- Ефект на почти-печалбата / near-miss — win-related reward circuitry fires on an objective loss; pushes continued play.
- Илюзия за контрол — instrumental responses/ritual (button, stop-reel, chosen numbers) create false sense of skill over pure chance.
- Гонене на загуби / loss chasing — intensifying play to recoup; marker of problem gambling; collects the other three.
- Frame: house edge is permanent; these distortions make a fixed game feel beatable; knowing them is protection.

## Verified facts (sourced — see 00-brief)
- Gambler's fallacy: past independent outcomes do not change future odds; the wheel/RNG „has no memory". (addictionhelp; Wikipedia)
- Monte Carlo 18.08.1913: black 26× in a row (~1 in 66.6M); players lost fortunes betting red as „overdue" — origin of the „Monte Carlo fallacy" name. Use as a concrete, non-fabricated historical anchor. (Wikipedia; casinoedges)
- Near-miss recruits win-related brain circuitry (ventral striatum) despite being a loss; increases desire to play though rated more unpleasant; designed in deliberately. (Clark et al. 2009, Neuron; frontiersin; teachboston)
- Illusion of control: Langer — instrumental/choice features create perceived skill over chance outcomes; does not change RNG odds. (UBC Clark & Wohl 2021; medium; rg.org)
- Loss chasing: continue/intensify to recoup; hallmark of transition to problem gambling. (ScienceDirect; PMC10628006; gamblingharm)
