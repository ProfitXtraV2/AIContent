## FACTS (all researched — see 06-verification.md for URLs)
- Single 52-card deck; player vs dealer; Ante + Blind must be equal; Trips optional (on player's own 5-card hand).
- Player raises ONCE (Play bet): pre-flop 4x (or 3x) the ante; after the flop 2x; after the river 1x or fold.
- Dealer qualifies with a pair or better. Dealer not qualified → ANTE PUSHES (returned); Play & Blind still resolve.
- Blind pay table: royal 500:1, straight flush 50:1, four of a kind 10:1, full house 3:1, flush 3:2, straight 1:1, below straight push. Blind pays only when the player WINS with a straight or better.
- Trips pay table (most generous / "9-7"): royal 50:1, straight flush 40:1, quads 30:1, full house 9:1, flush 7:1, straight 4:1, three of a kind 3:1 → house edge ~0.90%. Stingier tables → up to ~6.18%. Pays regardless of dealer / result. Tables VARY by casino.
- House edge: 2.185% of the ante (optimal); element of risk 0.526% of total wagered (optimal). Simplified strategy: 2.43% / 0.58%. RTP ≈ 99.47% vs turnover (long run).
- Basic strategy: pre-flop raise 4x with pairs 3-3+, suited A-x/K-x/Q-6+/J-8+, offsuit A-x/K-5+/Q-8+/J-T; else check. Flop 2x with two pair+ / hidden pair (not pocket 2s). River 1x unless ≥21 dealer outs beat you → fold.
