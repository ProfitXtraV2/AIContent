FACT INVENTORY (all corroborated A+B unless noted):
- Single 52-card deck; player vs dealer only; community-card game (2 hole + 5 community). [A,B]
- Mandatory Ante + Blind, MUST be equal; optional Trips (on player's own final 5 cards). [A,B]
- One Play raise per hand; earlier = larger: pre-flop 4x (or 3x), post-flop 2x, post-river 1x or fold. [A,B]
- Dealer qualifies with a pair or better. Not qualified → ANTE PUSHES; Play & Blind still resolve. [A,B]
- Blind pay table: royal 500:1, SF 50:1, quads 10:1, full house 3:1, flush 3:2, straight 1:1, below straight push. Pays only on a player win with a straight or better. [A,B]
- Trips PT1 (9-7, most generous): royal 50, SF 40, quads 30, full house 9, flush 7, straight 4, trips 3 (all :1) → house edge 0.9018%. Stingier tables to 6.1808%. Pays regardless of dealer. [A]
- House edge: 2.185% of ante (optimal); element of risk 0.526% of total wagered (optimal). Simplified strategy 2.43% / 0.58%. [A]
- RTP derived ≈ 99.47% vs turnover (optimal), ≈ 97.82% vs ante. [derived from A]
- Basic strategy thresholds (pre-flop 4x, flop 2x, river 1x/fold "21 outs"). [A /strategy/]
