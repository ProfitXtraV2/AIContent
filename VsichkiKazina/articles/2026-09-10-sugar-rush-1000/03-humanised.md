# 03 — Humaniser pass (anti-AI style)

Applied to 02-draft → prose in 05b. Changes (numbers untouched, all facts identical):
- Broke uniform section shape: intro and RTP section are flowing analytical paragraphs; FS
  section is a compact list-in-prose; max-win section ends on a plain fact, no takeaway line.
- Removed every "не X, а Y" paragraph-ending antithesis (the top BG tell). Affirmatives stated
  directly. The one contrast ("не описва по-добра игра, а по-екстремна") is used once, in the
  intro only.
- No signposting lead-ins; calculations start with the numbers.
- Em-dashes within budget (≤1 per ~400 words); replaced with commas/colons/full stops.
- No demonstrative intensifiers ("именно/точно тази"), no "причината е проста:" reveals.
- Asymmetric ending: takes a side (1000 is a deliberate chase-the-rare-hit build; the calmer
  original exists for a steadier ritmo).
- Native vocabulary: множители по клетките, безплатни завъртания, оборот, домашно предимство,
  инфо-панел, демо режим.

Self-check: em-dash budget OK; no signposting; ≥3 varied paragraph shapes; asymmetric outro;
