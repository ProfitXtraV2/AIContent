# 01 — Synthesis: Sugar Rush 1000

**Reader intent:** someone who knows (or has seen) Sugar Rush and wants to know what the
"1000" version changes before playing. Searching "sugar rush 1000" — commercial/informational.

**One-sentence thesis:** Sugar Rush 1000 keeps the 7×7 cluster-and-tumble skeleton of the
original but pushes the multiplier ceiling from x128 to x1,024 and the top win from 5,000× to
25,000×, which is exactly why it is more volatile and returns the same or less — not more —
per lev on average.

**Facts to carry (verbatim, verified):**
- 7×7 grid, cluster pays (5+ touching), tumble.
- Per-spot multiplier x2 doubling → cap **x1,024** (base game x128). No reset in free spins.
- Scatters 3/4/5/6/7 → **10/12/15/20/30** FS.
- RTP configurable: **97.50% / 96.53% / 95.50%**; commonly listed default **96.53%**.
- Max win **25,000×**; top hit ~**1 in 12,800,000**.
- Volatility **5/5**.
- €1000 turnover at 96.53% → ~**€965.30** back to player long-run, ~**€34.70** house.

**Honest angle:** the "1000" markets a bigger dream, not better odds. Higher cap = rarer big
hit; RTP is set by the operator and the top 97.50% build is rarely the one you get. Check the
info panel. Very high volatility eats bankroll in long dry stretches.
