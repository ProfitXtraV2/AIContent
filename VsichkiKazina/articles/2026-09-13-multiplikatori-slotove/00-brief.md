## ANGLE (honest, anti-hype — the pillar's spine)
A multiplier multiplies a win by a stated factor (x2, x3, x100). It feels like "free extra
money", but the game's RTP already accounts for every multiplier the maths can produce. A
big multiplier ceiling does not lower the house edge — it concentrates the return into
rarer, larger hits, i.e. HIGHER волатилност. Multiplier applies to the WIN/bet, so x100 on a
€0.20 win is €20, not a life-changing sum. Distinct from a progressive jackpot (a separate
prize pool), and multiplier winnings from a bonus are still subject to превъртане.

## MULTIPLIER TYPES TO COVER (the section spine — vary shapes, no uniform template)
1. Множител на печалбата (win multiplier) — multiplies a spin/combination win by a factor.
2. Множител в таблицата / символ-множител — a symbol (often a wild) that carries a fixed
   ×N and multiplies the combination it completes.
3. Нарастващ (прогресивен) множител — increases step by step within a feature/cascade chain.
4. Случаен множител — lands at random (orbs/bombs), typically in free spins.
5. Множител в бонуса / безплатните завъртания — usually higher or accumulating vs base game.
