### **Flagged Passages, Patterns & Recommendations**

#### 1. Pattern: Symmetrical / Formulaic Contrasts
AI models love to explain concepts using perfectly balanced, mirror-image sentences (If X does Y, then A does B). It creates a robotic, pendulum-like rhythm.
* **The Quote:** *"При голямо попадение x100 качва баланса чувствително; при дребно попадение същият x100 добавя малко, понеже базата под него е малка."*
* **The Recommendation:** Break the symmetry. Instead of a perfectly balanced contrast, combine the thought into a single, flowing sentence. For example, you could suggest explaining that the x100 multiplier's impact is entirely dependent on the base win, meaning a massive multiplier on a tiny hit still yields a modest result, rather than splitting it into two identical clauses.

#### 2. Pattern: Over-Polished / Sterile Lists
LLMs default to highly uniform bullet points. Notice how every bullet in this list starts with a bolded term, followed by a colon, followed by exactly one concise sentence of explanation. 
* **The Quote:** 
  > "- **Множител на печалбата:** умножава резултата от едно завъртане или от отделна комбинация.
  > - **Символ-множител (wild ×N):** обикновено wild с надпис ×N, който допълва комбинацията и вдига стойността ѝ.
  > - **Нарастващ (прогресивен):** качва се стъпка по стъпка..." *(and so on)*
* **The Recommendation:** Introduce structural variety (human asymmetry). Make one bullet point two sentences long. Make another one slightly more conversational. You could also remove the bolding from the text inside the parentheses to make it look less like a machine-generated glossary.

#### 3. Pattern: Didactic Staccato Rhythm
When AI explains math or logic, it often reverts to short, punchy, definitive sentences grouped together. This creates a "textbook" or "lecturer" tone.
* **The Quote:** *"Голямото число не струпва повече стойност в играта. То я преразпределя: печалбите идват по-рядко и на по-едри порции, значи волатилността се качва."*
* **The Recommendation:** Smooth out the staccato rhythm by connecting these thoughts. Suggest using conjunctions to link the idea of value redistribution directly to the increase in volatility, turning two abrupt sentences into one fluid, conversational explanation.

#### 4. Pattern: Forced Signposting (SEO Injection)
AI is often prompted to "include internal links naturally." It usually responds by stopping the narrative to explicitly point the reader to another page, rather than weaving the link into the actual subject matter.
* **The Quotes:**

* **The Recommendation:** Suggest integrating these links contextually. Instead of a dedicated "read more here" sentence, advise hyperlinking the actual concepts (like the word "превъртане" or specific casino terms) earlier in the paragraphs where they are naturally being discussed.
