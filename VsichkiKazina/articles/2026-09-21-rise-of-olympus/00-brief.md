- Provider: Play'n GO. Release: 2018 (август 2018).
- Grid: 5×5. Wins: 3+ matching symbols in a horizontal OR vertical line (up/down/left/right), then the winning symbols clear and new ones fall (cascade/tumble).
- Cascade: each consecutive cascade raises the win multiplier by +1.
- RTP: 96.50% default (house ~3.50%). Play'n GO ships lower configs — 94.51%, 91.49%, 87.50%, 84.50% — the operator picks which to run. [VERIFY the live per-casino build via the in-game info panel].
- Volatility: HIGH (10/10 on Play'n GO's own scale). Max win: 5,000× stake (ceiling).
- Hand of God powers: Zeus removes/destroys two sets of symbols; Poseidon adds 1–2 wilds; Hades transforms one set of symbols into another.
- Wrath of Olympus meter: charges from god-symbol wins (5-symbol win = 3 sections, 4 = 2, 3 = 1). Full meter → one free respin then all three Hand of God powers fire in sequence.

## HONEST ANGLE (brand doctrine)
The god powers and the cascade multiplier do NOT change the house edge — they redistribute variance, not odds.
