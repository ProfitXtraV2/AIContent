## ANGLE
The entire high-end payout power of this game sits in the multiplying (not additive) wild multipliers during the bonus — ×2/×3/×5 per wild, compounding up to ×3125 — combined with a scatter-triggered free-spins structure that can retrigger up to 200 spins. The 93,750× max win is a mathematical extreme, reachable only through the rare stacking of high wild multipliers inside a big free-spins round; high (5/5) volatility means long stretches with no meaningful return are normal, not a malfunction.
