# 01 — Synthesis (Book of Dead)

## Reader & intent
BG player searching "book of dead" / "book of dead rtp" to learn how it plays and whether it is worth it. Commercial-informational. Brand value-add: the configurable-RTP reality check (down to 84.18%), high-volatility framing, and that the expanding symbol in the free spins carries nearly all the win weight.

## Core message
Book of Dead (Play'n GO, 2016) is a 5x3 / 10-line Egyptian slot whose whole payoff sits in the 10 free spins, where one randomly chosen symbol expands to fill a reel. High volatility, 5000x max (rare), and — the catch — several RTP builds the operator selects (96.21% down to 84.18%). Check the info panel.

## What to include
Theme/Rich Wilde; 5x3/10 lines; book = wild+scatter; free-spins expanding symbol + retrigger; RTP ladder; high volatility + 5000x; gamble feature; demo/limits/RG.

## What to exclude
No operator terms/bonuses, no НАП №, no affiliate link. No faces in imagery (Rich Wilde is a person). No invented max-win or RTP.

## Persona / voice
