## GAME FACTS (verified — see 06-verification.md for source URLs)
- Slot: Jack and the Beanstalk (NetEnt), released 12.12.2011 (5×3 classic).
- RTP: 96.28% (NetEnt official default build). Two decimals, confirmed.
- Reels/rows: 5×3. Paylines: 20 (fixed).
- Max win: 7,100× залога (NetEnt official).
- Volatility: medium (classifications vary medium ↔ medium-high across sources — state honestly).
- Hit frequency ~34%.
- Walking Wilds: a wild appears on a reel, all wins containing it are TRIPLED (×3), then a re-spin fires and the wild shifts ONE reel to the LEFT; repeats until it walks off reel 1. Works in base game and free spins.
- Free spins: 3+ scatters (treasure chest) → 10 free spins; 3 more scatters mid-round add spins (re-trigger, some builds).
- Treasure Collection: collect keys from reel 5 during free spins → progressively upgraded stacked/expanding wilds.
- NetEnt commonly ships operator-configurable RTP builds → the casino's info panel is authoritative (verify default vs lower build).
- Remastered edition exists (2022+, different RTP builds ~94–96%); this piece covers the classic original.
