## Search intent (one line)
The searcher wants to play casino games for free — but "free" splits into two intents:
(a) demo/free-play to try slots without money, and (b) "free" bonuses (no-deposit / free
spins). A useful pillar serves both and names the catch in (b).

## Entity / term union to cover
демо режим · виртуален (условен) баланс · RTP (дългосрочна статистика, не обещание за сесия)
· домашно предимство · волатилност (ниска/висока) · ротативки (демо мирори реалния RTP) ·
игри на маса / казино на живо (демо ограничено) · безплатни завъртания (FS) · бонус без
депозит · превъртане с БАЗА (тук: върху печалбата от FS) · максимална изтегляема сума ·
„социални казина"/нелицензирани безплатни игри с нагласени печалби · лиценз от НАП ·
лимит на депозита/загубата.

## Gaps no competitor covers well (our differentiator)
1. Most "безплатни казино игри" pages are just a grid of playable demos with zero education.
   We explain that the big virtual balance teaches TEMPO/волатилност, not luck.
2. Nobody links the two "frees": demo (truly free) vs bonus (marketed free, carries
   превъртане + cap). We compute the bonus one in €.
3. The trust angle: a demo you can trust is a НАП-licensed operator's demo, not a random
   "social casino" app that inflates win frequency to convert you.

## Facts / figures (ALL illustrative — no operator sourced)
- RTP framing: at ~96% RTP the game returns, averaged over a huge number of bets, 96 of every
  100 € staked; the other 4 are the house edge. Long-run only; a 100-spin demo says nothing.
- Free-spins worked example (illustrative): 50 FS × €0.20 = €10 of play you didn't pay for;
  a €15 win from them carrying 40× wagering on the winnings = 40 × €15 = €600 turnover before
  any withdrawal; plus a max-cashout cap (e.g. €50).
- Slots contribute ~100% to wagering; demo mirrors a slot's fixed math (RTP/волатилност/
  bonus-feature frequency) at a licensed operator; table/live demo is limited/absent.
