### 1. The "Philosophical Hook" Opening
**The Pattern:** AI struggles to just start an article. It usually opens by defining a concept, presenting a dramatic dichotomy, or making a sweeping philosophical statement about the topic before getting to the point.
**Flagged Passage:** *"Думата „безплатно" в казиното крие уловка, защото се лепи върху две несвързани неща и объркването между тях излиза скъпо."*
**Recommendation:** Drop the dramatic framing about the "word 'free' hiding a catch." Start the article directly with the practical reality: players often confuse demo modes with free spins, and explain immediately how they differ. 

### 2. The "Setup and Reveal" (Narrated Terminology)
**The Pattern:** When explaining a concept, AI often describes the mechanics in one sentence, and then uses the next sentence as a dramatic reveal to name the term. 
**Flagged Passage:** *"Смисълът е в поведението на конкретната ротативка: колко често плаща, по колко, дали дълго мълчи и после удря голямо, или капе постоянно по малко. Това е волатилността, и демото е най-честният начин..."*
**Recommendation:** Invert this structure. Introduce the term "волатилност" (volatility) right at the beginning of the explanation, rather than treating it as a punchline at the end of the description. 

### 3. Aphoristic / Didactic Topic Sentences
**The Pattern:** AI loves to start paragraphs with short, punchy, almost proverb-like sentences that summarize the moral of the paragraph before explaining it.
**Flagged Passages:** 
* *"Големият условен баланс подвежда точно защото е голям."*
* *"Другото „безплатно" е доста по-скъпо, отколкото изглежда."*
**Recommendation:** Soften these transitions. Instead of these fortune-cookie-style declarations, blend the transition directly into the mechanics. For example, move straight into how a 20-spin session with a massive virtual balance creates a false sense of security, without the preachy preamble.

### 4. The "Neat Bow" Paragraph Conclusions
**The Pattern:** AI rarely leaves a thought open or lets the reader draw their own conclusions. It ends almost every paragraph with a summarizing sentence that reiterates the main point, creating a repetitive, staccato rhythm across the article.
**Flagged Passages:**
* *"Това се вижда в информацията на самата игра, а в демо не се усеща."*
* *"Демо, на което да се доверяваш, идва от оператор с валиден лиценз от НАП; произволната безплатна игра от реклама не носи такава гаранция."*
* *"„Безплатното" тук е само входът; истинската цена излиза от изискването за оборот и от срока, в който трябва да го покриеш."*
**Recommendation:** Delete at least two of these summarizing sentences. Trust that the reader understood the math and the examples provided in the body of the paragraph. Let the paragraphs end on the concrete data/examples rather than a summary statement.

### 5. The Preachy Sign-off
**The Pattern:** AI frequently ends articles with a standalone, imperative command telling the user exactly what to do, acting as a final "moral of the story."
**Flagged Passage:** *"Пусни демото, преди да депозираш, а не след като реалният баланс вече е тръгнал надолу."*
**Recommendation:** Remove this standalone sentence entirely, or merge its sentiment organically into the preceding paragraph about budget checking. Leaving it isolated at the bottom makes it feel like an AI-generated concluding thought.
