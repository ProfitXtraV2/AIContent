SECTION 1 — Какво е хазарт според закона
Facts: definition quoted verbatim from чл. 2, ал. 1. The three cumulative elements are случайност, залог and печалба/загуба на залога. Two contrast cases: a free school томбола (no залог) and a chess tournament with an entry fee (no случайност). „Залог" is defined in § 1, т. 1 ДР, and „под друга форма" covers жетони, кредити and виртуална валута bought with real money. Участник is defined in чл. 2, ал. 2 (optional, one clause).
Terms/entities: хазарт, залог, участник, Закон за хазарта, хазарт игри.
Direction: quote the definition, then test it against the two contrast cases so the reader can classify an edge case themselves. End on the „под друга форма" point, because that is the clause that catches tokens and credits.
Shape: narrative build of 2 paragraphs, ~150 words. This is the only section in the piece that reasons through counter-examples.

The 4 sub-types under чл. 49: традиционна лотария (всички издадени билети), томбола (само продадените), числова лотарийна игра (тото, лото, бинго, кено per НАП) and моментна лотария (скреч под защитно покритие, еднократно). The state monopoly under чл. 4, ал. 3 exempts only томбола and бинго. Contrast: a private firm may run bingo but not classic лото.
  Terms/entities: лотарийни игри, традиционна лотария, томбола, числова лотарийна игра, тото, лото, бинго, кено, моментна лотария / скреч, държавен монопол.

Two payout methods (чл. 60, ал. 4): a pre-set коефициент (€10 × 2,50 = €25) or the тотализаторен принцип (pool share, sum unknown until the end).

SECTION 3 — Кои хазартни игри са разрешени онлайн
Facts: under чл. 41, ал. 2 all five types are allowed online (internet, mobile/stationary devices, radio, TV, satellite), except томбола and моментна лотария (both tied to physical tickets; моментна is one-off per чл. 49, т. 4). The key player rule in чл. 41, ал. 1 is that the channel does not change the type. Three examples: рулетка in an app = игра в казино, live покер from a studio = игра в казино, лото via phone = числова лотарийна игра. Operator duties under чл. 6 (ЦКС at БИМ, player ID, real-time data to the НАП server) are reduced to ONE clause.
Terms/entities: хазартни игри онлайн (Ahrefs parent topic, so it goes in the heading), онлайн хазарт, НАП.
Direction: open with the one-word answer, give the two exceptions, then make the "channel doesn't change the type" rule the centre of the section with the 3 examples. The чл. 6 clause comes last and is subordinate.
Shape: short paragraph + 3-item list + 1 closing sentence, ~150 words.

SECTION 4 — Един лиценз покрива една игра
Facts: under чл. 3, ал. 3 a licence covers only the game it names and cannot be transferred. НАП issues a separate licence per type, for 5 or 10 years [VERIFY]. Practical consequence: a sports-betting licence does not make the same site's casino section legal, because the online casino needs its own licence. Check WHICH games are listed in the НАП register, not only the operator's name.
Terms/entities: лиценз НАП, лиценз (5/10 г.).
Direction: state the rule, then go straight to the one concrete consequence for the player. Give no hypothetical operator name. End on the check-which-games instruction.
Shape: short blunt section of 2 tight paragraphs, ~80 words. It contrasts with the list-heavy sections around it.

SECTION 5 — Кой издава лицензите и как да разпознаете законната оферта
Facts: since 08.08.2020 (ДВ бр. 69 от 04.08.2020) the licensor is the изпълнителен директор на НАП or an authorised deputy (чл. 3, ал. 1). Before that it was the Държавна комисия по хазарта. Three recognition signs: (1) the site appears in the НАП list of licensed sites, there is a separate list of unlicensed ones, and access to those is blocked by order of Софийски районен съд (чл. 17, ал. 6). (2) Stakes and winnings are only in € since 01.01.2026 (чл. 3, ал. 4); another currency is allowed only in land-based casinos after it is entered in the certificate, so dollars or crypto on an online site is a red flag. (3) A visible warning about gambling addiction plus info on counselling and therapy (чл. 10б). Report unlicensed sites to illegal_gambling@nra.bg; anonymous reports are not reviewed.
Terms/entities: НАП / изпълнителен директор, Държавна комисия по хазарта (historical), списък на лицензираните сайтове, Софийски районен съд, евро, чл. 10б, illegal_gambling@nra.bg.
Internal links: /blog/proverka-licenz-kazino/ (step-by-step check) and /zakonno-li-e/ (legality overview). Summarise, don't duplicate.
Direction: give the authority in 2 sentences, then the 3 signs as a numbered checklist. The euro sign gets the most weight because it is the newest and least known. Close with the links + reporting e-mail.
Shape: 2-sentence intro + numbered checklist (3 items) + 2-sentence close, ~190 words. This is the longest section and the practical payoff of the article.

SECTION 6 — От колко години може да се играе хазарт
Facts: under чл. 2, ал. 3 only adult, legally capable natural persons without restrictions under this or other laws may play. Adulthood = 18 years [VERIFY statutory source]. The rule applies to all 5 types, including lottery tickets from a kiosk [VERIFY чл. 10, ал. 11]. "Restrictions under other laws" includes people on the регистър на уязвимите лица (чл. 10г): it is not public, and the term is chosen by the person but is at least 12 months.
Terms/entities: 18+, пълнолетие, регистър на уязвимите лица, чл. 10г.
Direction: answer the age question in the first sentence, then use the "restrictions in other laws" phrase as the bridge to the vulnerable-persons register. This sets up the RG close without repeating it.
Shape: single flowing paragraph (can split into 2), ~90 words.

SECTION 7 — Платено забавление, не източник на доходи
