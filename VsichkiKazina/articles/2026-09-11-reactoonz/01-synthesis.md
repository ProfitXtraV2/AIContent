# 01 — Synthesis (Reactoonz)

## Reader & intent
BG player searching "reactoonz" / "клъстер печалби" to learn how it plays. Commercial-informational. Brand value-add: it has NO free-spins round (surprises players), very high volatility, configurable RTP, and the Gargantoon/Quantum Leap system explained plainly.

## Core message
Reactoonz (Play'n GO, 2017) is a 7x7 cluster-pays grid slot: win with 5+ touching symbols, cascades charge the Quantum Leap meter that triggers random features and eventually the Gargantoon giant wild. No free-spins round. Very high volatility, 4570x max (rare), RTP 96.51% in several builds. Check the info panel.

## What to include
7x7 + cluster pays; cascades + Quantum Leap; Gargantoon 3x3→2x2→1x1; no free spins; RTP ladder + very high volatility; max 4570x; one-eyed vs two-eyed symbols; demo/limits/RG.

## What to exclude
No operator terms, no НАП №, no affiliate link. No human faces in imagery. No shaky ~750x single-symbol figure (hedged). No invented RTP/max win.
