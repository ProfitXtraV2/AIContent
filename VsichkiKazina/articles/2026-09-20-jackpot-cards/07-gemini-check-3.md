This article is exceptionally clean and avoids the most egregious AI clichés (there are no "В днешния дигитален свят" or "Нека се потопим" intros). ### 1. Pattern: Symmetrical / Contrasting Pairs (The "AI Seesaw")
LLMs love to create perfectly balanced, contrasting sentences to sound authoritative and comprehensive. When used repeatedly, it creates a robotic, predictable rhythm.

*   **Flagged Passages:**
    *   *"Може да дойде и след голяма печалба, и след напълно празно завъртане."*
    *   *"Три спатии дават най-малкия джакпот, три пики най-големия."*
    *   *"Изборът на такъв слот опира до тема и волатилност, не до джакпота."*
    *   *"...играй заради самата игра, не заради джакпота."*
*   **Recommendation:** Break the symmetry. Human writers rarely speak in perfectly balanced scales. Suggest combining these thoughts into more fluid, asymmetrical sentences. For example, instead of the neat "this, not that" structure, advise the writer to state the primary fact and append the secondary thought as a casual aside or a separate, shorter sentence to disrupt the staccato rhythm.

### 2. Pattern: Didactic Bridging ("This [Noun]" Transitions)
AI models frequently use demonstrative pronouns paired with a summary noun to bridge paragraphs. It reads like a textbook explaining a concept rather than a natural article flow.

*   **Flagged Passages:**
    *   *"Заради тази мрежова природа джакпотът може да натрупа големи числа..."*
    *   *"Тази вноска към пула е причината прогресивните игри често да имат..."*
*   **Recommendation:** Remove the "This [Concept]" crutch. Advise the writer to connect these ideas using cause-and-effect verbs or by simply stating the next fact without explicitly summarizing the previous paragraph's concept (e.g., dropping "тази мрежова природа" and just explaining how the network size directly impacts the numbers).

### 3. Pattern: Formulaic / Interrogative Subheading Structure
The H2s read exactly like an AI-generated SEO outline. They follow a rigid, predictable Q&A format that marches down a logical checklist. 

*   **Flagged Passages:**
    *   *"Кога се пуска бонусът"*
    *   *"Откъде идват парите в пула"*
    *   *"Мени ли шансовете ти в основната игра"*
    *   *"В кои игри ще го срещнеш"*
*   **Recommendation:** Suggest blending or rephrasing the subheadings so they don't read like a FAQ schema. Advise the writer to use more descriptive, punchy H2s rather than direct questions or sterile categories. 

### 4. Pattern: Over-Explanatory / Mechanical Phrasing
When explaining mechanics, the AI defaults to a highly sterile, step-by-step instructional tone that lacks conversational warmth.

*   **Flagged Passage:** *"Щом събереш три карти от една и съща боя, печелиш нивото на тази боя и кръгът приключва веднага."*
*   **Recommendation:** Advise the writer to inject a bit more conversational pacing here. Suggest breaking this mechanical explanation into two distinct beats—one focusing on the action of matching the cards, and the other on the immediate payout/end of the round, rather than cramming it into one highly efficient, robotic sentence.
