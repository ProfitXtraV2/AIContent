## MECHANIC FACTS (provider + game-DB data — NOT operator T&C / НАП)
- Jackpot Cards = Amusnet's (ex-EGT) signature cross-game MYSTERY progressive.
- FOUR levels named after card suits, smallest→largest: Спатия (Clubs) < Каро (Diamonds) < Купа (Hearts) < Пика (Spades). [egt.com/jackpot/jackpot-cards, egt.rs — provider wording: Spades highest, Clubs lowest]
- Trigger: random after any spin, independent of base-game result; higher base bet raises the chance of triggering and of higher levels. [egt.com, amusnet.com/jackpots]
- Bonus mini-game: field of 12 face-down cards; flip one by one; collect 3 of the same suit → win that suit's level; round ends when the third matching suit is revealed. [egt.com, amusnet.com/jackpots]
  - Plus variant: Jackpot Cards Plus = 15 cards + Wheel/Key Cards feature. [amusnet.com/jackpots]
- Funding: pooled/network progressive — a small % of every bet across all games running it feeds a shared pool. Exact % NOT published → [VERIFY], state no number.
- Effect on RTP/odds: contribution is funded from wagers (standard for progressives), so it does not improve base-game odds; jackpot is a redistribution to a rare outcome. No specific "RTP incl jackpot" figure published → do not state one.
- Games carrying it: 20 Super Hot, 40 Super Hot, Burning Hot, Rise of Ra, Olympus Glory, Versailles Gold, Extra Stars (EGT/Amusnet classic library). [amusnet game pages + game DBs]
- Distinct from EGT Digital's Bell Link and Clover Chance (separate jackpot systems). Cash Bomb (2025) is an add-on to Jackpot Cards, not a replacement. [amusnet.com/jackpots, eegaming.org]
- No published seed/reset/average amounts (operator/currency-configurable) → do NOT invent figures.
