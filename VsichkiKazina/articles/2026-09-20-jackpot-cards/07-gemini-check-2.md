### 1. Pattern: The Formulaic "If/Then" Hook
AI models struggle to start articles organically. They almost always rely on a conditional hook ("If you are looking for X, then Y" or "Whether you are a beginner or a pro...") to establish a connection with the reader. 
*   **Flagged text:** *"Ако си въртял класика на EGT, почти сигурно си виждал лентата на Jackpot Cards да свети над барабаните."*
*   **Recommendation:** Drop the conditional "If you've..." setup. Start directly with a punchy, authoritative statement about what the feature is. For example, you could suggest starting immediately with the fact that Jackpot Cards is the signature progressive jackpot of Amusnet, visible across dozens of their classic games.

### 2. Pattern: Uniform Block Structure (Symmetrical Paragraphs)
Look at the visual rhythm of the article. Every single H2 section consists of exactly one paragraph, and almost all of them are exactly 3 to 5 sentences long. AI loves symmetry and tends to generate perfectly balanced "blocks" of text. Human writers naturally vary their paragraph lengths—sometimes using a single sentence for emphasis, sometimes writing a longer block.
*   **Flagged text:** The entire article's H2 structure (One H2 = One paragraph). 
*   **Recommendation:** Break up the visual monotony. In sections like **"Бонусът с обърнатите карти"** or **"Откъде идват парите в пула"**, suggest splitting the text into two paragraphs. You can also suggest pulling out a key sentence to stand alone for emphasis (e.g., making *"Три спатии дават най-малкия джакпот, три пики най-големия."* its own line).

### 3. Pattern: Staccato Rhythm & Rigid Parallelism
AI often tries to sound authoritative by using short, perfectly parallel sentences. In Bulgarian, this can occasionally sound slightly robotic or overly clinical, lacking the natural connective tissue a human would use.
*   **Flagged text:** *"Може да дойде след голяма печалба, може и след празно завъртане."*
*   **Flagged text:** *"Точен алгоритъм производителят не публикува. Моментът не се предвижда и не се предизвиква."*
*   **Recommendation:** Suggest combining these staccato sentences using natural conjunctions, or softening the phrasing so it doesn't read like a bulleted list forced into paragraph form. For example, suggest blending the idea that the algorithm is secret with the fact that the moment can't be predicted into a single, more fluid sentence.

### 4. Pattern: The Didactic "Hand-Holding" Wrap-up
LLMs have a habit of over-explaining the *implication* of a fact, treating the reader a bit like a student who needs the lesson summarized. 
*   **Flagged text:** *"Тази стълбица е една и съща във всяка игра с Jackpot Cards, така че щом веднъж я разбереш, я разпознаваш навсякъде из каталога на Amusnet."*
*   **Recommendation:** Suggest trimming the second half of this sentence. The reader doesn't need to be told that understanding it once means they will recognize it everywhere—that is implied. Ending the section simply on the fact that the ladder is identical across all games is punchier and respects the reader's intelligence.
