# 01 — Synthesis (concise)

## Intent
Searcher wants to understand what "каскадни/падащи символи" (tumble / cascade / Avalanche)
means: why one spin can pay several times, whether that makes the game "better", and how it
differs from ordinary payline slots. Informational, evergreen.

## Entity union (covered)
tumble/cascade/Avalanche mechanic · remove-and-refill within one paid spin · chained wins ·
rising win-multiplier (base vs free spins) · cluster pays / pay-anywhere pairing · RTP is
unchanged (redistribution, not extra money) · high/streaky volatility · contrast with fixed
paylines & ways-to-win (one evaluation per spin) · branded examples (Sweet Bonanza, Sugar
Rush, Gonzo's Quest, Reactoonz).

## Key verified facts (see 00-brief SOURCES / 06-verification)
- Cascades do NOT raise theoretical RTP; they reshape when/how it pays → streaky volatility.
- Gonzo's Quest Avalanche ladder: base 1×→2×→3×→5×; Free Fall (10 spins) 3×→6×→9×→15×; RTP 95.97%.
- Sweet Bonanza: pay-anywhere + tumble; multiplier bombs 2×–100× only in free spins.
- Reactoonz: cluster pays + cascades, RTP 96.51%.

## Conflicts / excluded claims
- No "tumbling reels = higher RTP / consistent wins" framing (marketing claim on some
  affiliate pages) — excluded; cascades don't change theoretical RTP.
- No operator-specific bonus/T&C or НАП facts (out of scope).
- Exact multiplier caps differ per game → described generically, one verified concrete
  example (Gonzo's Quest) cited.

## Suggested persona
Editorial-register education, signed Георги Тодоров. Numbers-first, dry, sceptical-but-fair.
Illustrative € figures labelled примерни.
