## ANGLE
Evergreen game-MECHANIC explainer. NOT a listing, NOT operator-specific. Explain the
tumble/cascade/Avalanche mechanic: a win forms → winning symbols are REMOVED → new symbols
DROP into the emptied positions within the SAME paid spin → chained wins from one bet.
Often paired with cluster-pays / pay-anywhere grids and with a rising win-MULTIPLIER that
climbs with each consecutive tumble (especially in free spins).

## HONEST CORE (must land)
Cascades feel generous because one spin can pay several times, but the average return is
baked into the game's normal RTP — the mechanic REDISTRIBUTES wins into streaks, it does
NOT beat the house edge. The rising multiplier mostly lives in the free-spins round and
drives HIGH volatility (long dry stretches, rare big hits). Distinguish from fixed
paylines / ways-to-win (each spin = one evaluation).

## VERIFIED FACTS (web-reachable, non-operator, non-НАП)
- Cascading/tumbling reels do NOT change theoretical RTP; they reshape how/when the RTP is
  paid out → higher, streakier volatility. (gamblingzone, casinobloke, ACRpoker, drupaldocs)
- Gonzo's Quest (NetEnt, Avalanche): consecutive base-game avalanches raise the win
  multiplier 1× → 2× → 3× → 5×; in the Free Fall (10 free spins) round the ladder is
  3× → 6× → 9× → 15×. RTP 95.97%. (netent games site; slotcatalog; askgamblers) — matches
  vk-0044 established facts.
- Sweet Bonanza (Pragmatic Play): pay-anywhere, tumble; multiplier "bombs" 2×–100× only in
  the free-spins round; RTP default 96.48%. (verified in vk-0008-adjacent article; own site)
- Reactoonz (Play'n GO): cluster pays + cascades; RTP 96.51%. (PokerNews)
- Sugar Rush (Pragmatic Play): cluster pays + tumble + multiplier spots — plain-text mention.
