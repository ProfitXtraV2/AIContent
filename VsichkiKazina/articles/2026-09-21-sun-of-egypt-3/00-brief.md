- 5×3, 25 fixed paylines. RTP 95.61% (consistent). Volatility High. Max win 10,000× (= Royal jackpot, full 15-cell grid).
  Bet range varies by operator → NOT hard-pinned (sources $0.25–$20, currency varies).
- Hold & Win: trigger 6+ Sun symbols; cleared 15-position grid (5×3); starts 3 respins, resets to 3 on each new symbol;
  ends when respins run out or grid fills. Coin values 1×–15× stake, summed and paid.
- Jackpots: FIVE fixed-multiplier tiers — Mini 20×, Minor 50×, Major 150×, Grand 2,000×, Royal 10,000× (full grid=max win).

- Series: SoE1 (3 tiers, 1,000× max), SoE2 (4 tiers, 5,000× max), SoE3 (5 tiers, 10,000× max).
