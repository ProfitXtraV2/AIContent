## HONEST ANGLE (brand doctrine)
High volatility = long dry spells between hits; the 500× Mega jackpot and the 4,608× max are ceilings, not expectations. Configurable RTP → the operator picks the build → check the info panel (the 90% build is meaningfully worse for the same game). Ante bet buys feature frequency, not better long-run odds (edge-neutral). Money Collect is where the game pays, but it needs the wild-on-reels-1-and-2 trigger, which is rare by design.
