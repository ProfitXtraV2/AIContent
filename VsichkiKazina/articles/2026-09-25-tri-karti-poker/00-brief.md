## FACTS (all researched — see 06-verification.md for URLs)
- Ante & Play house edge: 3.37% of the ante; element of risk 2.01% (Wizard of Odds, Ante Bonus Pay Table 1).
- Ante Bonus (Pay Table 1, most common): straight 1:1, three of a kind 4:1, straight flush 5:1.
- Pair Plus (original / flush 4:1): пара 1:1, флъш 4:1, кента 6:1, трипс 30:1, стрейт флъш 40:1 → house edge 2.32%.
- Pair Plus (now most common / flush 3:1): пара 1:1, флъш 3:1, кента 6:1, трипс 30:1, стрейт флъш 40:1 → house edge 7.28%.
- Paytables VARY by casino — present common ones and say others exist.
- Dealer qualifies with Queen high or better.
- Optimal strategy threshold: Q-6-4 or better → play; below → fold.
- 3-card ranking (high→low): straight flush > three of a kind > straight > flush > pair > high card.
- Combinations out of C(52,3)=22,100: straight flush 48, three of a kind 52, straight 720, flush 1,096, pair 3,744, high card 16,440.
